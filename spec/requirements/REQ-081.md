  text: >-
    The summary shows the net premium at the planned entries: "Get Rs 4,364.75" for a credit or "Pay Rs 3,334.50" for a
    debit, with the per-unit figure beside it, from the engine (sells minus buys, price x quantity). Real case: the

- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    A Charges action shows the engine's round-trip estimate itemised as ADR-082 lists it - brokerage, STT, exchange
    transaction charges, SEBI charges, stamp duty, GST - with the total, the date of the rates used, and the label
    "estimate". Real case: the same condor's round trip totals Rs 214.96 (ADR-082, evidence W-075 AC-7). Each line is
    shown rounded to the paisa from its exact amount and the total is the rounded exact sum, so the lines may add to a
    figure a paisa or two from the total; no note is shown (ADR-205; real case 2026-10-28: lines Rs 263.13, total
    Rs 263.15).
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    The summary shows the strategy's intrinsic value (each option leg's value if it expired at the current spot) and
    time value (current premium minus intrinsic), signed so that premium paid is positive and premium received is
    negative, labelled "value of the options, not P&L". Example: a bought NIFTY 22,900 CE at LTP 150.00 with spot
    22,976.85, 1 lot: intrinsic 76.85 x 65 = Rs 4,995.25, time value 73.15 x 65 = Rs 4,754.75.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    The summary shows Reward/Risk = max profit / max loss, both including charges (ADR-082), to two decimals, with a
    switch to Risk/Reward. Real case: the condor above, max profit Rs 4,149.79 and max loss Rs 8,850.21, reads
    Reward/Risk 0.47 (Risk/Reward 2.13). It shows "-" with the reason when either side is Unlimited, the max loss is
    0, or the engine has no worst case (mixed expiries, ADR-143).
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    With a Zerodha session and at least one open position, the margin line (ADR-099) offers "Count my open positions".
    On, the figure is Zerodha's basket margin with the user's open positions considered, labelled "Margin needed (with
    your open positions)", and may be lower than the strategy alone; off, it is labelled "Margin needed (this strategy
    alone)". The choice is a per-user setting, on by default as in Sensibull. No real case yet: the owner's account had
    no open positions on 2026-10-21.
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    The hard gate before the basket stays REQ-055 with the ADR-094 figure whatever this setting says; the planning
    figure is information only (ADR-099). Where the position-aware figure is lower than the gate's, Execute can still
    block on margin while the planning line shows enough; the Execute review then names both figures. This is stricter
    than Sensibull, which does not block a shortfall, and stays so under ADR-152 exception (a).

- id: AC-8
  text: >-

    a partial sum of the legs that do have prices. A change of leg, side, lots, multiplier or planned price recomputes
    the figures; the margin is fetched again only on such a change, never on every tick (ADR-099).

  text: >-

# REQ-081: Builder summary money figures

Why: before committing, a user asks "what do I pay or get today, what will it cost in charges, and is the possible
gain worth the possible loss?". Sensibull's summary strip answers these (study 2026-10-21); the owner made Sensibull
the default (ADR-152, re-triage of MR-014 and MR-017). Every figure is the engine's (ADR-008).

Register: MR-014, MR-017. Sensibull's "% of funds" next to max profit and max loss was first held back because it reads
as a return figure (ADR-055 (1)); the owner then decided to show both, same as Sensibull (ADR-164, a SPEC CHANGE to
ADR-055 (1) for this one figure), written as AC-9 to AC-11.

Decisions: ADR-164, ADR-152, ADR-008, ADR-082, ADR-094, ADR-099, ADR-016, ADR-143.
