# REQ-094: Dark theme

Why: many traders watch screens for hours and prefer a dark theme; Sensibull offers one (study 2026-10-21). Money and
health colours must keep their meaning in both themes.

Register: MR-156.

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-049, ADR-010.
