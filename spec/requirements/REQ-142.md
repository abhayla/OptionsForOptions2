# REQ-142: Funds needed in the builder summary

Why: a user who places a hedged strategy needs more money than the margin figure, because the buy legs are paid for
before the sell legs reduce the margin. Sensibull shows both figures (screens 128, 241); OFO2 shows only Margin needed
and Margin available, so a user with exactly the margin figure in the account could not place the strategy.
REQ-136 already defines the tooltip text for Funds needed, but no requirement made the figure itself exist (gap list
G-038 cited REQ-081, whose "funds" is the available funds read from Zerodha).
