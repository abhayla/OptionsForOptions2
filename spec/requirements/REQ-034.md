# REQ-034: Outcome view and scenario levels

Every user sees what can happen to their money at different market levels before executing.

Decisions: ADR-008. Sources: M §22; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §29.

**Owner decision (2026-10-19, ADR-120):** no what-if sliders (spot, IV, days) in V1; AC-6's two modes are unchanged.
**Owner decision (2026-10-21, ADR-151):** SPEC CHANGE to ADR-120 (1): the builder gets target-level, target-date and
IV-change what-if inputs, all computed by the server engine; AC-6 was rewritten in the master-register docs PR. The
ADR-120 line above is kept as history and no longer holds for the builder.
**Finding (2026-10-19):** F-49 - the Strategy Builder page is built but missing from `REAL_PAGES`, so its nav item
shows the placeholder; the outcome view is unreachable from the menu until it is wired.
