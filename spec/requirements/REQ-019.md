# REQ-019: Complimentary Pro for qualifying Zerodha customers

A user whose verified Client ID is on the admin list gets Pro free with no expiry.

Decisions: ADR-024. Sources: M §65; H2 §7.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-26 - an Authorised Person may not charge clients, and NSE bars incentives
"for account opening ... or any kind of subscription plan"; free Pro for clients who opened through the owner is
exposed as written. Open for owner decision as Q260.

**Open:** What happens when an ID is removed from the list: open (ADR-024). See `spec/open-questions.md`.
