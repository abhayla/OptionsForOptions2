- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    The After is the whole trade, not the open legs alone: the booked P&L of legs closed earlier and of legs this
    adjustment closes is part of the After's max profit, max loss, breakevens and POP (F-74). Example: a condor that
    booked +1,200 on an earlier close shows an After max loss 1,200 smaller than its open legs alone would give.
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    The adjustment's own charges (its closing orders plus its opening orders) show as their own line, apart from the
    charges already paid on earlier orders; neither is folded into the other and both enter the whole-trade figures
    once (F-74).
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    POP is computed for every level the engine derives, the after-charges breakevens included. A level finer than paise
    is rounded at the one place it is produced, by a rounding rule stated in that code and its test (example from F-75:
    22771.372 becomes 22771.37). A level POP still cannot use shows "POP not available" with the reason, never an
    exception or a blank (F-75; knowledge/findings/interpolated-level-finer-than-paise-refused.json).
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Spot comes from the live feed; if no fresh tick is held, from a Kite REST index quote (the quote allow-list
    accepts index symbols that contain a space, such as `NSE:NIFTY 50` and `BSE:SENSEX`, F-76); if that fails or is
    empty, from the last close per ADR-212. The spot shown is always labelled with its source and time. If every source
    is absent or failed, no After is computed and the screen says "Spot not available" with each source's reason.
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Margin for each approach comes from Zerodha's basket margin for the After legs. Stated: shown with its time.
    Failed, timed out or not signed in: "Margin not available" with Zerodha's reason, the other figures still shown.
    Empty or missing: shown as not available, never as 0.
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    Pricing 7 approaches for one strategy takes at most 1 s of server time, broker round trips (quote, margin)
    excluded, measured on the development server (F-73 measured the engine part at 0.27 s for 7 calls).
- id: AC-8
  text: >-
    An approach whose landing contract is not listed, not tradable, or known to be blocked for fresh orders for low OI
    (catalogue M-15) is refused with its reason naming the leg, for example "No 22,350 PE is listed for 3 Nov", while
    every other approach still shows. Zerodha does not publish its low-OI thresholds, so an unknown state shows a
    warning on the leg ("Zerodha may refuse fresh orders in low-OI contracts"), not a refusal.

# REQ-155: Adjustment approach builder and whole-trade After

Why: REQ-046 AC-4 promises a Before/After once the user picks an approach, but the core proof of 2026-10-30 (F-73 to
F-76) found the engine fast and correct on a real chain while the After left out booked P&L and the adjustment's own
charges, POP failed on one real breakeven, and spot could not be read without the feed. This requirement is the core
everything else in ADR-214 builds on: the compare (REQ-156), the filters (REQ-157) and the history (REQ-163).

Suggest-only (ADR-003): building an approach never places, changes or cancels an order.

Decisions: ADR-214, ADR-008, ADR-206, ADR-212.
