# REQ-044: Strategy control center and Positions screen

The Strategy screen is where a strategy is controlled; Positions is secondary.

Decisions: ADR-010. Sources: M §30, §55; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md).

## Owner decision (2026-10-18, ADR-109)
- Approved. AC-3's Positions screen is built first: strategy-linked positions (the app's own fills) and outside lots
  (bought outside the app) shown apart; outside lots are not part of any strategy and block none (REQ-060, ADR-109).
  AC-1, AC-2 and AC-4 follow later.

## Owner decision (2026-10-18, ADR-110): a contract with an open plan
- While the plan is open, the row reads "Order placed - waiting for Check with Zerodha", shows Zerodha's quantity
  marked "not yet confirmed", makes no own/outside split, and links to the strategy's Execute screen; the strategy is
  blocked. The waiting ends when Zerodha shows the plan's orders final (by tag), even if Check is never pressed.

## Real P&L (Lane S, 26 Nov 2026; ADR-330 (10) and (24); owner questions LQS-1..3, option (a), decided in ADR-330 (24))
`spec_similar` ("Real P&L total from Zerodha positions", 26 Nov 2026) found no existing text on this (best match 0.22,
ADR-017); this paragraph extends AC-3 ("P&L") and ADR-330 (10) ("Real P&L", "Paper P&L", never added; real rows from
Zerodha first, ADR-016) and does not add a decision row.
- **The figure.** Zerodha reports one `pnl` per contract. The engine (`ofo.engine.broker_pnl`, ADR-008) re-derives it
  from the same row's own fields, exact decimal: `sell_value - buy_value + quantity x last_price x multiplier`, both
  sides rounded to 0.01 (half to even). The row shows the figure only when the two agree. A missing, null, boolean,
  float or non-decimal field is never read as 0: the contract has no figure. Several Zerodha rows of one contract
  (NRML + MIS) are summed before the check. Real case: NIFTY26O1923650CE, 16 Oct 2026: closed row (15:10:06)
  `201.5 - 331.5 + 0 x 3.1 = -130.00`, Zerodha says -130, so stated; open row (12:21:07) `0 - 331.5 + 65 x 5.1 = 0.00`,
  Zerodha says 0, so stated; a row whose `pnl` is -131 is a mismatch and shows no figure.
- **Where.** `GET /positions` (same route, same read) adds, per strategy contract row, `pnl` (exact string, 2 places),
  `pnl_text` (signed paisa text, the one formatter the paper totals use), `pnl_state` and `pnl_reason`, and a
  `real_pnl` object: `value`, `pnl_text`, `state`, `reason`, `source` ("Zerodha"), `read_at`, `caption`, `excludes_note`.
  The top-level `pnl_text` equals `real_pnl.pnl_text`. A malformed P&L field makes that contract "not available"; it
  does not refuse the read. No order endpoint, no store, no browser arithmetic; Real is never added to Paper.
- **Row states, and the sentence shown with no figure.** stated (figure shown); not_available: "Zerodha's reply for
  {contract} lacks a figure needed to check its P&L; no figure is shown."; mismatch: "Zerodha's P&L for {contract} does
  not match its own trade values; no figure is shown."; shared (Zerodha's net on the contract is not this strategy's
  own units: another strategy or outside lots hold part; never a split): "This contract is shared (with another
  strategy or outside lots); Zerodha reports one P&L for the whole contract, so no split is shown."; pending (ADR-110):
  "This contract is not yet confirmed, so no P&L is shown."; stale (Zerodha read more than 90 seconds before the
  server's clock): "Zerodha was last read more than 90 seconds ago, so no figure is shown until it is read again."
- **The total (LQS-2).** It is the exact sum of the rows' figures, stated only when every row is stated. State
  "empty" (no strategy contract rows): no figure, "No real positions are open in your strategies."; "partial": no
  figure, "{n} of {m} contracts have no figure, so no total is shown."; "stale": no figure, the stale sentence. Caption:
  "Zerodha, as at HH:MM:SS IST". When outside lots exist or Zerodha's reply held rows closed today, the total adds:
  "Excludes contracts not in a strategy here and contracts closed today." Example: the 16 Oct 15:10 reply has only a
  closed row, so the list has no row and the total is empty with that line. W-111's derived case (Zerodha 130, own 65,
  65 outside): the row is shared and the total partial.
- **Page.** The Real total shows the stated text, else a dash and the server's reason, plus the caption and the
  excludes line as sent. A refused read (409: not connected, unreadable) shows the dash and the refusal's own message.
  Each real contract row of the one Positions list shows its figure, or a dash and its reason. Only a server reply with
  no `real_pnl` key keeps "Real P&L is not shown yet: the server does not price Zerodha positions."
