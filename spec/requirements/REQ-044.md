# REQ-044: Strategy control center and Positions screen

The Strategy screen is where a strategy is controlled; Positions is secondary.

Decisions: ADR-010. Sources: M §30, §55; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md).

## Owner decision (2026-10-18, ADR-109)
- Approved. AC-3's Positions screen is built first: strategy-linked positions (the app's own fills) and outside lots
  (bought outside the app) shown apart; outside lots are not part of any strategy and block none (REQ-060, ADR-109).
  AC-1, AC-2 and AC-4 follow later.

## Owner decision (2026-10-18, ADR-110): a contract with an open plan
- While the plan is open, the row reads "Order placed - waiting for Check with Zerodha", shows Zerodha's quantity
  marked "not yet confirmed", makes no own/outside split, and links to the strategy's Execute screen; the strategy is
  blocked. The waiting ends when Zerodha shows the plan's orders final (by tag), even if Check is never pressed.
