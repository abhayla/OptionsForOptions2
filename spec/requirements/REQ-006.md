# REQ-006: Three UX levels

Users choose Guided, Standard or Advanced and can switch at any time; no level bypasses a safety rule.

Decisions: ADR-004. Sources: M §3; H1 §2.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q232): which level shows Greeks
- AC-3 used to say Standard shows Greeks, contradicting REQ-035 AC-7. The owner chose REQ-035: Guided shows no Greeks,
  Standard adds % return and breakevens, Advanced adds IV and Greeks. AC-3 is corrected to match.
