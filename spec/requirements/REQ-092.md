# REQ-092: Pre-trade check-in

Why: the product's promise is "Plan your trade. Follow your strategy. Then execute." (ADR-027); an impulsive entry is
the opposite. Sensibull offers an optional "Mindful Trading" pause with a check-in (study 2026-10-21). OFO2 adds the
user's own exit rules to the check-in, and never slows an exit.

Register: MR-153.

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-003, ADR-009, ADR-027.
