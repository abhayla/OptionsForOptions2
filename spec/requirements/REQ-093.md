# REQ-093: Instrument watchlists

Why: a user follows a few strikes and futures between planning sessions without rebuilding a strategy each time.
Sensibull has numbered watchlists of instruments and strategies (study 2026-10-21). Saved strategies are already
followed live on Live Strategies (W-113); this adds single contracts.

Register: MR-154.

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-012, ADR-015, ADR-019, ADR-002.
