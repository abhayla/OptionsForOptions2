# REQ-010: Public site and live-data access

All real-time data is behind login; the public site is marketing only.

Decisions: ADR-027. Sources: M §41; H2 §6; Q58 = A, Q59 = A.

## Owner decision (2026-10-19, ADR-114)
- Approved. The ACs above are the business rules, unchanged; content and layout come from the design note
  `docs/design/screens-research-2026-10-19.md`.

## Owner decision (2026-10-20, ADR-144)
- While ADR-055 (2) holds, the Pricing block reads "Free during early access. Paid plans will be announced before they
  start, and you will be told before you are charged." No rupee amount, plan name, trial length or referral reward on
  any public page.

## Owner decision (2026-10-20, ADR-145)
- The footer of every public page carries the Zerodha AP disclosure block, the market-risk warning (at least 10 px)
  and "Strategies shown are for planning and education, not advice to trade."; no public page goes live before
  Zerodha approves it.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-16 - brokers themselves show signed-out visitors delayed option prices
only (Dhan "delayed by 15 mins", Upstox "Price is delayed"), consistent with AC-1. The exchange data rule behind it is
stream S1b's (hypothesis H1).
