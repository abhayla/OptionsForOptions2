- id: AC-1
  text: >-
    Edge, not level: a rule opens one suggestion record when its condition goes from false to true and the crossing
    is confirmed on the close of a candle of the style's length (intraday 1 min, weekly 5 min, monthly 15 min) still
    beyond the line. While it stays true, no new record and no new alert. Example: on 2026-11-02 strategy 66's T-07
    profit target was raised 30 times between 10:38 and 11:09 as it wobbled across the line; under these rules it gives
    the few records its preset allows (66 likely expires 3 Nov, so the intraday preset applies; exact count derived in
    GC-ALERT1 step 0), not 30.
- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Re-arm: after a record, the same rule may open a new one only when the value has first cleared the line by the
    re-arm margin (intraday 10%, weekly 20%, monthly 25% of the distance from the stage's entry value to the threshold,
    read as the threshold's own size: ADR-237 (1); T-07 at 80 % intraday re-arms at or below 72 %)
    AND the cool-down has passed (intraday 5 min, weekly 30 min, monthly 2 h). An open record lapses when its value
    clears the line by that margin, not at the first close back under the line (ADR-237 (4)).
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    Escalation: if the value reaches the next stage's threshold of the same rule (for example T-03 delta 0.30 -> 0.35),
    a new record opens at once, ignoring the cool-down; the record says it is worse than the last one. This holds
    after a dismiss too (ADR-236): a dismiss silences the dismissed stage only, and the new record says "Worse than
    the one you dismissed"; the daily cap and quiet time (AC-4) still apply.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    Daily cap per strategy (intraday 6, weekly 4, monthly 2 records a day) and quiet time after the open (intraday
    first 5 min, weekly until 09:30 as S-01 already says, monthly until 09:45). A capped or quiet-time crossing is
    shown in the strategy's timeline as "held: daily limit" / "held: quiet time", never dropped silently.
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Risk exits are not delayed: a stop-loss or conditional exit (REQ-041 / paper triggers) acts on the live price at
    once; this requirement applies only to adjustment suggestions.
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Where each value lives (open question Q-AD-1 below): the check speed is an admin setting only, per style (REQ-166, ADR-235);
    the re-arm margin and the daily cap are admin settings (the user may lower the cap, never raise it); confirmation

    trading style picks the whole preset in one step.
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    Every value is read at run time (REQ-166 AC-1): an admin or user change applies to the next check without a deploy.

# REQ-170: Adjustment suggestions raised once per crossing (alert discipline)

**Approved 2026-11-02 (owner answers Q-AD-1..Q-AD-4); nothing built yet.** Basis: docs/research/adjustment-alert-frequency-2026-11-02.md (own
measurement 2026-11-02; reference product 30 s / 45 s refresh; Zerodha GTT/ATO fire once; thinkorswim alerts "once" /
"once per bar"; Streak / AlgoTest act on candle close; tastytrade / Option Alpha manage positions about daily).

| | Intraday (0-1 day) | Weekly (2-7 days) | Monthly (8-30+ days) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Check speed (admin, ADR-235) | 10 s | 30 s | 120 s |
| Confirm on candle close | 1 min | 5 min | 15 min |
| Cool-down, same rule | 5 min | 30 min | 2 h |
| Re-arm margin (admin) | 10% | 20% | 25% |
| Daily cap per strategy | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| Quiet time after open | 5 min | until 09:30 | until 09:45 |

## Open questions for the owner

- Q-AD-1 ANSWERED (owner 2026-11-02 ~14:15 IST, question tool: "Preferences, per trading style"): the user-changeable
  values (confirm on candle close, cool-down, quiet time, a lower daily cap) live on the Preferences tab (REQ-168) as
  one small table per trading style, starting from the admin's defaults; the re-arm margin, the daily-cap ceiling and
  the check speed stay admin-only (REQ-167). Not in playbooks: they are not a rule's stage numbers.
- Q-AD-2 ANSWERED (owner 2026-11-02 ~14:16 IST, question tool: "From the strategy, user can override"): the style is
  taken from the strategy and changes by itself as expiry nears: 0-1 day to expiry = intraday rules, a weekly expiry =
  weekly, a monthly expiry = monthly (the same days-left logic as the playbook stages, ADR-214); the user may pin a
  style per strategy.
- Q-AD-3 ANSWERED (owner 2026-11-02 ~14:17 IST, question tool: "Use these defaults"): the numbers in AC-1..AC-4 and
  the table below are the starting presets; tune after a week of live paper data (alerts per strategy per day before
  and after).
- Q-AD-4 ANSWERED (owner 2026-11-02 ~14:18 IST, question tool: "Per style: 10 / 30 / 120 s"): ADR-235, intraday 10 s,
  weekly 30 s, monthly 2 min, admin settings.
- Technical definitions (ADR-237, 2026-11-02 step 2): band base, candle close (last value read in the candle, processed
  on the worker's 1 s wake), record timing (close + one check interval), lapse at the band, storage (held crossings
  table, "worse" column), one writer (the background worker; the screen check writes no record).
- Q-AD-5 ANSWERED (owner 2026-11-02 20:4x IST, chat: "go ahead with your recommendation"): after a dismiss, one stage
  worse still opens a new suggestion at once (ADR-236, amends ADR-217); the same level stays quiet until it re-arms.
- Live day 2026-11-04 (F-101, ADR-246): 27 records / 8 lapsed against 103 / 75 on 2026-11-02 in 09:30-11:50; a 1-day
  SENSEX condor past its breakeven gave 5 records in an hour (4 lapsed) then hit the intraday daily limit. Owner
  (question tool, "Keep presets for now"): presets unchanged; the intraday preset is reviewed with two more expiry days.
