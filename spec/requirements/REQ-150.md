# REQ-150: Strike drawer Greeks view

Why: the Sensibull Greeks view lets a user compare delta, theta, vega and gamma across strikes before choosing a leg. Ours stops at delta and IV (gap G-077), and the drawer opens empty until the user picks an expiry, one extra step Sensibull does not need (gap G-070).
