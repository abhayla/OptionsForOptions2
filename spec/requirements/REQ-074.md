# REQ-074: Security incident reporting and log retention

CERT-In's directions apply to every body corporate now: report cyber incidents "within 6 hours of noticing" and keep
logs "for a rolling period of 180 days" (F-27). The DPDP Rules' operative duties start on 13 May 2027 (F-27), so they
get their own requirement before then (AC-3).

Decisions: ADR-029. Placed in master-plan Stage 7 (operations) and the Stage 8 release gate.
