# REQ-079: Ready-made shapes and quick picks (held)

Why: the fastest way for a beginner to start is to pick the shape they have in mind and see it on real strikes,
then adjust. Sensibull does this in one click (study 2026-10-21: 38 ready-mades, straddle/strangle quick select).
A strike placed by the platform could be read as a recommendation, so the owner held it for the legal answer that
ADR-055 already waits on (ADR-150 (2)); the requirement is written now so nothing is lost.

Register: MR-004 and MR-029 (WRITE, held, ADR-150 (2)); MR-043 follows MR-004 and is covered by REQ-027 AC-1 under the
same hold (AC-10).

Decisions: ADR-150, ADR-055, ADR-157.
