# REQ-052: Market-data sourcing and licensing

V1 uses Zerodha live data in the interim; production use depends on verified terms; a licensed NSE+BSE vendor is the long-term source.

Decisions: ADR-014. Sources: M §38, §39, §71; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §17; H2 §5; IC §13.

**Open:** Q204, Q210. TrueData's 'NSE certificate' remark is unverified and not encoded. See `spec/open-questions.md`.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-23 - SEBI bars sharing real-time prices with third-party platforms; NSE
bars redistribution without an agreement and prices display per medium; Kite's terms bar public display but allow
platforms offered to other Zerodha clients after exchange approvals (AC-2's written confirmation is still needed).
F-16 - brokers show signed-out visitors delayed prices only.

**Findings (2026-10-08):** F-33 - the owner's Kite Connect plan returns historical minute candles with OI (344 for
NIFTY 50 and for an option on 2026-10-08) and daily candles, which contradicts AC-1's "with no historical data";
open for owner decision (whether V1 uses it, and its display rights per F-23).
