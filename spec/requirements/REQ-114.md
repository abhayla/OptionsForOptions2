# REQ-114: Live-feed reconnect policy

Why: a socket can go quiet without closing; if the app keeps showing its last prices as live, the user plans and the
monitor acts on numbers that stopped moving. Sensibull's relay uses this same 15 s / 60 s / capped back-off pattern.

Example: at 11:02:10 a user's socket goes silent. At 11:03:10 it is closed and data turns stale with "Last updated
11:02:10". Reconnects try at about 1 s, 2 s and 4 s; the third succeeds, subscriptions are restored, and at 11:03:19 the
first fresh tick turns the strategy back to monitoring.

Technical decision (orchestrator, master register 2026-10-21, TECH direction).

Decisions: ADR-015, ADR-053.
