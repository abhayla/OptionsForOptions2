# REQ-165: Playbook triggers checked in the background

Why: a rule is useful when the user is away. Example: the user tracks an iron condor and leaves at 11:00. At 11:40 NIFTY
falls to within 0.3% of the short put, inside the iron condor playbook's Middle-stage limit of 0.5%. The workers flag it at 11:40;
the inbox and alert carry it; the user sees it when they next open the app, with the time it was found.

How often, in plain words: on each new price for something the strategy holds, at most once a second. Not every
millisecond: Zerodha sends about one price per option per second, and a person adjusts in minutes.
