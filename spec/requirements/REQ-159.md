# REQ-159: Playbook content

Why: the owner said the stage boundaries and fixed values in the first mockup must be configurable, and that date and
day rules (weekend, holiday, leave) belong in the playbook (ADR-214 (4c)).

Decisions: ADR-214.

## How the open triggers are measured (GC-ADJ2 contract design decisions, 2026-10-31)

The catalogue left four measures open (`tests/fixtures/adjust_scenarios/_playbooks.json` "conventions"). The GC-ADJ2
goal contract, approved by the owner, fixed them; every figure comes from the one engine (ADR-008), exact Decimal:

- **T-14 "loss from a 1% move"**: the engine's P&L change of the whole trade at spot +1% and at spot -1%, at today's IV
  and time left; the larger loss of the two, as a percent of the margin Zerodha states for the position. No stated
  margin -> not checked, with the reason.
- **T-19 "premium still left"**: the sum over open short legs of (mid x quantity) minus the same sum over open long
  legs, compared with the adjustment's own charges (the "This adjustment" figure of the GC-ADJ1 compare). The iron
  condor fires below 1x that cost (ADR-216).
- **"% of premium paid"** (long options, PB-08, Draft playbooks only): current value / debit paid - 1.
- **"Tighten every trigger by X%"** (a day-and-date rule action, AC-4): every trigger fires sooner for the rest of that
  session. A distance threshold ("spot within N% / N points") is multiplied by (1 + X/100); a loss, delta or profit
  threshold by (1 - X/100).

A condition that already holds when tracking starts (F-71) is shown "Already true when tracking started" and opens no
opportunity until it has stopped holding and holds again (AC-2's "says why").
