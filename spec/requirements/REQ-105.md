# REQ-105: Delayed prices before a broker login

Why: a new user wants to try planning a strategy on real market prices before linking Zerodha, as Sensibull allows
(ADR-155). Prices are delayed so they cannot be mistaken for a trading feed.

Example: a user signs in with Google, opens the option chain without Zerodha, and sees NIFTY 24,512.30 stamped "Delayed 1
min - as of 10:41:05" with the banner "Connect Zerodha for real-time prices". They build a bull call spread and
paper-track it.

Held until: written data permission from Zerodha or a licensed vendor, and its cost (ADR-155, ADR-014, ADR-034); until
then ADR-027 stays the production behaviour.

Decisions: ADR-155 (SPEC CHANGE to ADR-027).
