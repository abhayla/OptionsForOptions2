  text: >-
    The builder's "Strategy price" tab plots, for the strategy's current legs, the combined net price per unit (sells

    price on a second axis. Example: a bull call spread BUY 23,000 CE / SELL 23,200 CE plots -51.30 at a bar where the
    legs traded at 81.30 and 30.00.
- id: AC-3

# REQ-091: Strategy price history chart (held)

Why: a user deciding whether to enter now wants to see how this exact combination has been priced over the last days.
Sensibull's builder has a Strategy Chart with an invert switch (study 2026-10-21). The owner chose Sensibull's history
screens and held them until display rights are confirmed (ADR-158). The market-wide history screens (IV percentile,
IV chart, OI over time, straddle charts, candle chart with OI profile, register rows MR-066 and MR-068) are REQ-107.

Register: MR-026.

Decisions: ADR-158, ADR-066, ADR-008, ADR-012.
