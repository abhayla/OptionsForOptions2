# REQ-001: V1 scope: underlyings, leg types, broker

V1 supports the index underlyings NIFTY, BANKNIFTY and FINNIFTY (NSE) and SENSEX (BSE), the six leg types, and Zerodha
as the only broker.

**Owner decision (2026-10-21, ADR-157):** SPEC CHANGE to ADR-001: BANKNIFTY and FINNIFTY added, no stock options. AC-1
and AC-4 were amended in the master-register docs PR; the evidence for AC-1 and AC-4 predates the change and must be
re-verified for the two added indices. ADR-161 (native apps in scope) does not change this requirement.

Decisions: ADR-001, ADR-157, ADR-161. Sources: M §1, §38, §94; H1 §1, §8; CH1 Q8; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md).
