- id: AC-1
  text: >-
    The strategy shows each trigger in one of these states, each with the time Zerodha was last read - "Held at
    Zerodha", "Fired - orders sent by Zerodha", "Removed", "Not held - check Kite" (Zerodha no longer shows it and the
    app did not remove it), "Out of step with this strategy" (AC-3). A state that could not be refreshed shows "as of
    <time> - could not refresh", never as current.
- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Reconciliation (REQ-060 AC-1 moments, plus each time the strategy screen opens) reads Zerodha's trigger list and
    compares each stored trigger by id - condition, legs, quantities, sides and products. A trigger changed or deleted
    on Kite by the user is recorded as an external change (ADR-018) on the timeline and shown side by side; nothing is
    changed back automatically.
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    Any change to the strategy's held net per contract (an exit or adjustment fill, a partial fill, a recorded
    reconciliation action, an intraday auto square-off under ADR-159, an expiry close) re-checks the trigger the same
    moment. When the trigger's legs no longer equal the orders that would close the new net, the trigger is "Out of step
    with this strategy", the strategy shows the difference (example - trigger buys 130 of 24500 CE, strategy now holds
    65 short), and the user is offered "Update the stop" (one confirmation, REQ-100 AC-4) or "Remove the stop".
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    The exit review of an app exit that closes the whole strategy includes removing its trigger as part of the same
    confirmation, so a closed strategy never leaves a live trigger behind; the removal is sent only after the exit's
    fills are confirmed by Zerodha, and the review says so. A partial exit's plan removes the trigger too (AC-15): once
    Zerodha reports the exit's orders in a final state the trigger is removed even when they filled only in part,
    because a trigger sized for the old net would open a position; the strategy then says "No stop on this strategy
    now - set a new one".
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Remove sends one delete to Zerodha and reads the list back. Answer states - stated (deleted) shows "Removed";
    not found (Zerodha already has no such trigger) shows "Removed" with a note "Zerodha had already removed it"; failed
    shows Kite's reason and keeps "Held at Zerodha"; a timeout is unknown until the read-back settles it, never retried
    blindly; ambiguous shows "Check your triggers on Kite". Remove is allowed with the kill switch on and with a
    reconciliation mismatch open.
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    When Zerodha shows the trigger fired, its orders are read from Zerodha and matched to the strategy; fills change the
    strategy's position only through reconciliation (REQ-057 AC-2), realised P&L comes from those fills (ADR-102), and a
    leg that did not fill or filled in part is a partial execution handled by REQ-058 and ADR-136, never retried.
    Orders that cannot be matched to the trigger by its legs show "Exit sent by Zerodha - check Kite" and block the
    strategy as a mismatch (ADR-018) until the user resolves it (ADR-137). A trigger holds only short buy-backs
    (REQ-100 AC-3); once they are confirmed filled, the app at its next run sells the strategy's long legs under the
    phase 2 rules of REQ-100 AC-17 (one at a time, each confirmed, never while a short it protects is open), or, when
    that cannot run, shows "Protective legs still open - close them" with one prepared basket.

  text: >-
    A firing, an external change and an out-of-step state each create an alert through the shared Notifier (REQ-116),

    the read time.

  text: >-
    After the daily 06:00 session expiry (ADR-053) the app cannot read Zerodha for that user: every trigger shows its

- id: AC-9
  text: >-
    Every create, update, removal, external change, firing and out-of-step event is an append-only timeline entry

- id: AC-10
  text: >-
    A platform-run P&L stop (REQ-100 AC-11) is shown on its strategy with the same states as AC-1 where they apply
    ("Armed - run by OFO2", "Fired - orders sent by OFO2", "Removed", "Not running - reconnect Zerodha"), each with the
    time of its last evaluation. When the server cannot evaluate it (platform down, no live data, Zerodha session
    expired after 06:00, ADR-053) it shows "not running" with the reason and the last evaluation time, never as armed.
- id: AC-11
  text: >-
    When a platform-run stop fires, its exit orders are read back from Zerodha and matched to the strategy through the
    per-order tag (ADR-105); fills change the strategy's position only through reconciliation (REQ-057 AC-2), a leg that
    did not fill or filled in part is a partial execution handled by REQ-058 and ADR-136, and nothing is resent
    automatically (ADR-017). The firing, a "not running" transition and a failed or unknown send each create an alert
    through the shared Notifier (REQ-116).
- id: AC-12
  text: >-
    A platform-run stop is removable by the user at any time, including with the kill switch on and with a
    reconciliation mismatch open (removing sends no order), and is removed or marked out of step under the same rules as
    AC-3 and AC-4 when the strategy's held net changes or the strategy is closed. Every arming, evaluation gap, firing
    and removal is an append-only timeline entry (REQ-040).
- id: AC-13
  text: >-
    A trigger never outlives its position. Every broker trigger is created with an expiry no later than its contract's
    expiry day (for a trigger over several legs, the earliest leg's expiry), never Zerodha's default validity; the
    trigger builder refuses a create without that expiry. Example - a trigger buying back the NIFTY 28 Oct 24500 CE
    short expires on 28 Oct.
- id: AC-14
  text: >-
    At every user login and at every reconciliation (AC-2), the app first lists the user's Zerodha triggers, before any
    other action for that user. A trigger whose legs no longer match a held position (a contract not held, a side that
    would open instead of reduce, a quantity above the held net) is deleted when the app created it (with the AC-5
    read-back), and is marked "Delete this trigger on Kite" for the user when the app did not create it; the app never
    deletes a trigger it did not create. Each case is a timeline entry (AC-9) and an alert (AC-7).
- id: AC-15
  text: >-
    Any exit the app prepares or sends removes that strategy's triggers in the same plan, and the plan says so. For a
    basket the user places on Kite, removal follows AC-4. For an exit the server sends (REQ-100 AC-17), the triggers are
    deleted and read back as gone before the first exit order is sent; if the deletion fails, times out or is ambiguous,
    the server sends nothing, the broker trigger stays the protection, and the user is alerted - so a trigger and a
    server exit never close the same short twice.
- id: AC-16
  text: >-
    An intraday (MIS) position (ADR-159) gets no broker trigger, because Zerodha's same-day auto square-off would leave
    the trigger with no position; a price-level stop on it is not offered as broker-held, and the screen says why. Its
    P&L stop runs only on the platform (REQ-100 AC-11).
- id: AC-17
  text: >-
    The strategy screen states the remaining gap in words beside every broker-held stop: "If you exit this on Kite while
    OFO2 cannot reach Zerodha, delete the trigger on Kite too".
- id: AC-18
  text: >-
    Answer states of the trigger-list read (AC-2, AC-14). Stated (a list returned) - each stored trigger is compared by
    id. Absent (a reply with no list) - treated as failed. Not found (a stored trigger missing from the list) - "Not held
    - check Kite" (AC-1). Empty (no triggers at all) - every stored active trigger becomes "Not held - check Kite".
    Failed (an error or a timeout) or ambiguous (two Zerodha triggers match one stored trigger, or one Zerodha trigger
    matches two strategies) - fail closed: new Execute is blocked for that user, the user is alerted, every trigger shows
    "as of <time> - could not refresh" or "Check your triggers on Kite", and the block lifts only when a later read is
    stated or empty and every mismatch is resolved.

# REQ-101: Broker-held stop-loss, lifecycle

Why: once a stop sits at Zerodha, the user needs to know it is still there, that it still matches what they hold, and
what happened when it fired. A stop that silently falls out of step is worse than none: if the user exits on Kite and the
trigger still holds "BUY 65", it opens a fresh long when it fires.

Example: the user exits the condor in the app at 11:00. The exit review lists "Remove the stop held at Zerodha after the
exit fills". Zerodha confirms all four fills at 11:01; the platform deletes the trigger, reads the list back, and the
strategy shows "Removed - read 11:01:20".

Held until: as REQ-100 (static-IP proof for build, ADR-153/ADR-154; Q211 legal review for launch).

Decisions: ADR-153, ADR-162, ADR-018.
