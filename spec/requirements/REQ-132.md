# REQ-132: Native mobile apps

Why: the owner put native apps in scope as Sensibull has them (ADR-161 (3)), with timing left to prioritisation.
The risk with a second and third client is a second set of numbers or a side door to orders; AC-1 and AC-3 close both.

Example: a user checks a live strategy on the Android app during a meeting; the P&L, the health colour and the
"Review exit" basket are the same as on the web, because all three come from the server (AC-1, AC-3).

Decisions: ADR-161, ADR-152. Register row: MR-138. Note: REQ-004 AC-4 is amended to match.
