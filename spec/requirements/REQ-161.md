# REQ-161: Strategy plan sheet at Start tracking

Why: a new strategy starts from its type's active playbook, and the plan sheet lets the user pick another playbook or
change the numbers for that one strategy (ADR-214 (4b)). Whether the sheet can be skipped is an open owner question
(ADR-214 consequences); until answered, "Use playbook as is" keeps it to one click.

Decisions: ADR-214.
