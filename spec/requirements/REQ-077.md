# REQ-077: Builder entry prices

Why: a user often plans around the price they expect to get, not the last traded price ("if I can sell this at 81.30,
what is my max loss?"), and wants to know when the market has moved away from their plan without the plan silently
changing under them. Sensibull's builder has an editable price, Reset Prices and a "prices have changed" marker
(study 2026-10-21); ADR-152 makes it the default. ADR-068 (1) and ADR-075 already require that entries change only
when the user asks.

Register: MR-012, MR-052.

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-068, ADR-075, ADR-085, ADR-080, ADR-082.
