# REQ-046: Adjustment Opportunity panel and Before/After

Every opportunity is shown in a four-layer panel, and any chosen approach gets a full Before/After analysis.

Decisions: ADR-011. Sources: M §33; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H2 §13.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q255): how the platform builds a chosen approach's configuration (AC-4)
- The platform keeps the structure's **widths** and re-places the short strike(s) at their **entry distance from
  spot**, snapped to the nearest listed strike. Example: the golden condor entered at spot 23,047 with its short call
  353 points above (23,400); with spot now 23,300, the rolled call spread becomes 23,650 / 23,850 (23,300 + 353 =
  23,653 → 23,650 on NIFTY's 50-point grid; 200-point wing kept). The result is only a proposal: every strike is
  editable, the Before/After view is shown, and nothing is sent until the user confirms (W-027 flow).
