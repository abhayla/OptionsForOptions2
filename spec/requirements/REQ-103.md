# REQ-103: Orders page and funds view

Why: a user who just placed a basket wants to see, in the app, whether Zerodha accepted, filled or rejected each order
and how much money is left, as Sensibull shows (ADR-160), without trusting a copy that may be out of date.

Example: at 10:42 the user opens Orders. Three orders of strategy 9 plan 2 show "Complete" and one "Rejected by Zerodha:
insufficient margin", each "read 10:42:05", with "Open on Kite" beside each. The next refresh fails, and every row reads
"status as of 10:42:05 - could not refresh".

Decisions: ADR-160 (SPEC CHANGE to ADR-134 (1) and (2)).
