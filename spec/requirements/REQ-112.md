# REQ-112: Suggestion outcomes

Why: a user reviewing a past trade wants to see what the app suggested and what they did about it; today triggers and
confirmed changes are recorded, but nothing links one to the other (register MR-121).

Example: at 11:20 "Adjustment opportunity detected - NIFTY within 0.5% of your 24,400 CE short" is shown. The user builds
a roll, prepares plan 3, places it on Kite; Zerodha fills both orders at 11:24. The suggestion reads "Completed - plan 3 -
11:24".

Decisions: ADR-011, ADR-019 (via REQ-040, REQ-045).
