  text: >-
    The entry review offers exactly two products, "Carry forward (NRML)" selected by default and "Intraday (MIS)". The

  text: >-

    segment, held as dated data with its source (like the ADR-119 holiday list), never typed into code. NSE and BSE
    rows are held separately. When no current value is held for the segment, MIS is not offered for that strategy and
    the review says "Intraday is unavailable right now" (fail closed).
- id: AC-3
  text: >-

    (dated data, as AC-2); the review explains "Intraday orders are closed for today; carry forward is available".
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    The margin check (ADR-094) asks Zerodha for the basket's margin with the chosen product; a reply quoting a different
    product, or none, makes the check fail closed. Example - the same NIFTY condor needs less margin quoted as MIS than as
    NRML; the review shows the figure Zerodha quoted for MIS.
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Every exit and adjustment order carries the product of the Zerodha position it changes, read from Zerodha's position
    row (ADR-080 (1), extended to adjustments by ADR-159); the reduce-only check (REQ-059) compares product too. A
    contract held under both products in one strategy is refused for adjustment with "This strategy holds the contract
    as both intraday and carry forward; close one on Kite first".
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Reconciliation recognises an auto square-off when, at or after the segment's auto square-off time, a strategy's MIS
    net on a contract goes to zero through Zerodha orders the app did not tag and Zerodha marks as its own square-off
    (the marker is read from Zerodha's order record, its exact field confirmed on a real case at build). The strategy's
    legs are marked exited with timeline entry "Closed by Zerodha (intraday auto square-off)", realised P&L from those
    fills (ADR-102), and no mismatch is raised.
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    Any other change to an MIS position - before the cut-off, a partial close, or a close Zerodha's record does not mark
    as its square-off, or a record that cannot be read - is an external change (ADR-018) that blocks the strategy until
    the user resolves it (ADR-137); the app never guesses a square-off.
- id: AC-8
  text: >-
    A strategy with an MIS position that is still open 10 minutes before the auto square-off time shows "Zerodha will
    close this intraday strategy at about <time>" and sends one alert through the Notifier (REQ-116); after the
    square-off it shows the strategy as closed by Zerodha.
- id: AC-9
  text: >-
    A daily 06:00 session expiry (ADR-053) on the day after an MIS entry changes nothing here: the square-off has already
    happened the day before and is reconciled at the next connect; until then the strategy shows its last read state
    with its time.

# REQ-102: Intraday (MIS) as a product choice

Why: some users want an intraday-only trade with lower margin, as Sensibull offers (ADR-159). The trap ADR-080 named - a
multi-day plan closed by Zerodha the same afternoon - is handled by stating the cut-off before Execute and recognising the
broker's exit afterwards.

Example: a user picks MIS for a NIFTY iron condor at 10:15. The review reads "Intraday (MIS): Zerodha closes these
positions automatically at about 15:20 today". At 15:10 an alert says Zerodha will close it. At 15:20 Zerodha closes all
four legs; the timeline shows "Closed by Zerodha (intraday auto square-off)" and the realised P&L from those fills.

Decisions: ADR-159 (SPEC CHANGE to ADR-080 (1)).
