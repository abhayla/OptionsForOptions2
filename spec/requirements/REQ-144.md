# REQ-144: Projected profit / Projected loss badge

Why: a user who sets a what-if target wants the answer to "what happens at that point" at a glance, without reading the
curve or hovering. Sensibull shows it as a badge on screens 012 and 123. OFO2 draws the curve and the hover values
(REQ-083) but has no single stated figure. The percent uses OFO2's funds basis (REQ-081 AC-9), not Sensibull's.
