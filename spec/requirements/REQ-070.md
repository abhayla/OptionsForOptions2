# REQ-070: Builder history and restore

## My Strategies screen wording (ADR-330 (8), owner 2026-11-26)

Each row that can be deleted has a tick box; a row marked held at the broker has none and shows "This strategy is marked
as held at your broker, so it cannot be deleted." in place of Delete (a row whose orders were sent keeps "Orders were
already sent for this strategy, so it cannot be deleted."). "Select all N shown" selects only the rows left after the
search; "Delete selected (N)" is disabled at 0. One confirmation reads "Delete N strategies?" (singular "Delete 1
strategy?"), plus " K of them are running." when K is above 0 (" 1 of them is running." when K is 1; C-04 / N-25, Lane R 26 Nov 2026), K counting rows whose status word is Running. The result
line is the server's summary, each kept strategy is named with the server's reason text, and one Undo restores every
deleted strategy. The result line keeps its "Undo" and a "Dismiss" button until the user presses one of them,
starts another delete or leaves the page, for a single delete as for a bulk one (C-04, owner screen check 26 Nov 2026: the
pop-up alone lasted about 2-3 s; ADR-330 (8): every rule of a single delete applies; "Dismiss" is new wording). A failed call keeps the selection and removes nothing. The single-row confirm is its own full-width
line below the row, so Rename and Delete do not move. The list read shows a skeleton while it runs and, on failure or
after 15 seconds, "Could not load your strategies. Nothing was changed." with Retry, which reads again (ADR-320).

Strategy changes in the Builder are kept as a simple activity history the user can restore from, and the history stays after execution.
