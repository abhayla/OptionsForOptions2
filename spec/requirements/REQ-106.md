acceptance_criteria:
- id: AC-1
  text: >-
    ATM IV is the mean of the CE and PE implied volatilities at the strike nearest the expiry's parity forward (ADR-061),
    from the one engine, shown to one decimal as a percent. Example - NIFTY 28-Oct expiry, forward 24,530, ATM 24,550,
    CE IV 13.2 and PE IV 13.6 give "ATM IV 13.4%".
- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Put-call ratio is total put open interest divided by total call open interest over the strikes the platform holds
    for that expiry, to two decimals, and the strip says how many strikes it covers ("PCR 1.08 over 41 strikes"); it is
    never presented as covering the whole chain when it does not.
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    Max pain is the strike, among the held strikes, at which the total intrinsic value owed to option holders (sum over
    strikes of OI x intrinsic value at that strike) is smallest; ties go to the strike nearest the forward. It is shown
    with the strike count as in AC-2.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    India VIX is the index's last price from the user's own feed, read through the instrument store like the index spot
    of REQ-072, never a typed instrument number.
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Each value is computed once per market snapshot and shared by every user entitled to that data (REQ-050 AC-1, AC-3),
    never in the browser (ADR-008).
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Each value shows its as-of time; a value whose inputs are stale or missing shows "-" with "not available - as of
    <time>" (ADR-015), never an old number as current. A strike with OI absent is left out of PCR and max pain and the
    strike count drops accordingly; with fewer than 5 strikes holding OI on either side, PCR and max pain show "-".
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    Wording is descriptive only (ADR-003): labels and help text explain what each number is; no text says what the
    number means for direction ("bullish", "expect", "should").
- id: AC-8
  text: >-
    The strip works for every underlying in scope (ADR-157); an underlying whose data the user is not entitled to see
    shows no strip rather than another user's data (ADR-053).

# REQ-106: Market context strip

Why: a user deciding on a strategy wants the few numbers traders look at first - how expensive options are (ATM IV, India
VIX) and where open interest sits (PCR, max pain) - next to the chain and builder, as Sensibull shows.

Example: on the NIFTY 28-Oct expiry the strip reads "ATM IV 13.4% - PCR 1.08 over 41 strikes - Max pain 24,500 - India
VIX 12.85 - as of 10:42:05".

Decisions: ADR-012, ADR-053 (computed from the user's own feed); ADR-152 (Sensibull default).
