- id: AC-1
  text: >-
    The engine's builder reply carries a list of warning codes per strategy and per leg; the screen shows each with
    text from the reviewed template catalogue (ADR-003 Q226), never text built in the browser. Codes: unhedged_short,
    weekend_holiday_decay, low_liquidity, expiry_day (the W-086 warning, unchanged) and market_event (held, AC-6).
- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    unhedged_short fires for a sold option leg with no bought option of the same type and expiry further out of the
    money in the strategy (the ADR-143 (2) test). Example: a NIFTY short strangle SELL 22,700 PE / SELL 22,900 CE
    flags both legs; adding BUY 22,500 PE clears the put leg's warning only.
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    weekend_holiday_decay fires when the next trading day is two or more calendar days away (weekend or exchange
    holiday, from the market calendar of ADR-119) and the engine's total theta is not zero; the text carries the
    engine's estimate of the change over those days, for example "Over the 3 days to Monday, time decay changes this
    strategy's value by about +Rs 1,240 (estimate)."
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    low_liquidity fires for a leg with no trade in the last 5 minutes, no bid or no offer, or a bid-ask spread wider
    than 10% of the mid price; the two thresholds are admin settings. Example: a far NIFTY 21,000 PE with bid 0.40 and
    ask 0.60 (spread 40% of mid) is flagged. With no live depth the leg shows "Liquidity unknown - no live depth"
    instead, never a verdict from stale data (ADR-015).
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    Warning texts state the condition and the number only; none tells the user what to trade or uses the ADR-003
    forbidden words. Example allowed: "This sold 22,900 CE has no bought call above it in this strategy; its loss has
    no fixed limit." Not allowed: "You should buy a hedge."
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Held until: an events data source, its licence and its cost are cleared (ADR-156 (3)). The market_event code then
    fires when an event that REQ-108 lists for the strategy (dated before its last expiry) exists, and points to that
    list; until then the code is never emitted.
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    When the leg set matches a catalogue template (REQ-076 AC-8), the builder shows that template's "How this trade
    works" bullets from the Learn texts (REQ-011; one home for the text), filled with the user's own strikes and expiry,
    for example "You buy the 23,000 CE because you expect NIFTY to rise; you sell the 23,200 CE to lower the cost." Any
    number in a bullet comes from the engine. A Custom Strategy shows no bullets and a link to Learn.
- id: AC-8
  text: >-
    A warning or bullet whose data is missing is left out with no placeholder; the warnings area with no warning
    reads "No warnings for this strategy", never an empty box.

# REQ-085: Builder warnings and plain explanations

Why: beginners miss risks they have not met yet - a sold option with nothing protecting it, a weekend of time decay,
a contract nobody is trading. Sensibull sends such insights as codes and shows plain "How does this trade work?"
bullets (study 2026-10-21). OFO2 keeps the text in its reviewed catalogue (ADR-003) and fires a warning only on a
measured condition.

Register: MR-037, MR-044. The event warning waits on ADR-156 (3) (register MR-067, not written here).

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-003, ADR-011, ADR-119, ADR-143, ADR-156.
