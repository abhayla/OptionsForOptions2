# REQ-029: Option Chain display and live data

The Option Chain is an exploration and construction tool with a clean default view.

Decisions: ADR-007. Sources: T1 #118-#150; Q34, Q38, Q39, Q45, Q46.

## Owner decision (2026-10-19, ADR-114)
- Approved. The ACs above are the business rules, unchanged; layout and column order inside them come from the design
  note `docs/design/screens-research-2026-10-19.md` (section Option Chain). AC-6 holds even though the studied brokers
  place orders from their chains.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-19):** F-47 - the live feed carries no previous close, so AC-2's Change column is
sent as null by the chain route (W-116) until the data path adds it.
