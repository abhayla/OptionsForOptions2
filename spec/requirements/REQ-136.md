- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Definitions say what the figure is and how it is measured, never what to do: they pass the ADR-003 wording check
    (no "should", "best", "safest", "recommended" ...) and live in one text catalogue that the wording test reads, not
    scattered in screen code. Funds needed says that buy legs are placed before sell legs to reach that figure; max
    loss on mixed expiries says no single maximum is shown and why (ADR-143).
- id: AC-3
  text: >-

    visible when numbers are shown, never only inside a hover.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-

    volatility, with each expiry's implied dividend yield", REQ-032); a
    figure that is exact at expiry says so ("Exact at expiry from strikes and entry prices").

# REQ-136: Builder figure definitions and the projections note

Why: beginners (ADR-004) see a dozen money figures and need to know what each means without leaving the screen;
Sensibull puts a definition behind every figure and one "Important info" note under them (study 2026-10-21). ADR-152
makes that the default; ADR-003 keeps every definition descriptive, never advice.
