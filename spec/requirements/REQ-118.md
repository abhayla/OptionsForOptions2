# REQ-118: Broker rate limits and back-pressure

Why: Kite limits how fast an app may call it. With many users on one app key, a burst (market open, a reconnect wave) can
hit the limit; a margin check refused for that reason must not look like "not enough margin", and a trigger create must
never be sent twice (register MR-095).

Example: at 09:15:02 forty users open the builder at once; margin requests queue; one waits 6 s and the user sees "Zerodha
is busy - try again in a moment" instead of a wrong margin figure.

Technical decision (orchestrator, master register 2026-10-21, TECH row MR-095).

Decisions: ADR-017, ADR-016.
