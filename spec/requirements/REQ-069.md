# REQ-069: Suggested setups in the Guided Builder

The Guided Builder turns a strategy type plus the user's range into a few concrete, explained setups that load into the Builder as an editable 'Suggested Setup'.

Decisions: ADR-005. Sources: T2 #49-#68; Q119-Q127.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-24 and F-25 - suggested setups with specific strikes on a paid plan may
count as research under SEBI's RA definition (no tool exemption; a comparable app holds RA registration). Open as Q261.

**Open:** Subject to the legal review in open-questions Q211. See `spec/open-questions.md`.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q250): what makes a setup "valid", and the "effective range"
- **Valid setup:** every leg is a listed, lot-aligned contract in the catalogue; the setup fits the user's capital
  and max-loss limits; and its P&L at expiry is ≥ 0 **across the user's whole expected range** for a range-bound
  strategy (e.g. Iron Condor, short strangle), or **at the favourable end of the range** for a directional strategy
  (e.g. bull call spread → the upper end). Invalid setups are never shown.
- **Effective range:** the band of index levels between the setup's breakevens where P&L at expiry is ≥ 0 (for an
  unbounded side, from the breakeven outward). Example: the golden condor (spec §6 fixture) has breakevens
  22,909–23,491; a user range of 22,950–23,450 is valid, 22,800–23,500 is not (it loses below 22,909).
