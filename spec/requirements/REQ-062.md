# REQ-062: Notifications

Alerts go out on four channels according to priority, while avoiding alert fatigue.

Decisions: ADR-028. Sources: M §56; H1 §26; H2 §21.

**Stage 4 scope (owner approval 2026-10-15):** master-plan Stage 4c builds the in-app channel and the WhatsApp
channel through the shared Notifier only, plus AC-3 (no repeat without a change) and AC-4 (Alerts list); the
WhatsApp alert goes only to the owner's allowed test number through the existing approved owner-alert template.
Push, email and the full AC-2 matrix are later-stage work and are placed as such in the coverage register.

**Open:** Provider, consent/WhatsApp opt-in, quiet hours, templates, escalation, dedupe, rate limits, audit, retry: open. Content needs legal review (Q211). See `spec/open-questions.md`.
