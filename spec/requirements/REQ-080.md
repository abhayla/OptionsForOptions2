# REQ-080: Leg picker with prices, depth popup, futures and forward prices

Why: choosing strikes is the main job in the builder, and a user cannot choose well from a list of bare numbers.
Sensibull's picker shows prices, OI and Greeks per strike and adds several legs at once; each leg has a depth popup
(study 2026-10-21). Today OFO2 adds one leg at a time through dropdowns with no price on screen (register MR-028).

Register: MR-013, MR-027, MR-028. The straddle and strangle tabs of Sensibull's picker are platform-placed quick picks
and live in REQ-079 (held).

Decisions: ADR-152, ADR-012, ADR-056, ADR-061, ADR-068.
