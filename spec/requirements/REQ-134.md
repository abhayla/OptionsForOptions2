# REQ-134: Saved strategy name, Save As, rename and search

Why: a user who plans more than two strategies cannot find them again without names, and "try a wider version without
losing this one" needs Save As. Sensibull's builder has all four (study 2026-10-21, screens 115-120, 270-272); ADR-152
makes that the default. Delete stays under OFO2's stricter REQ-070 AC-6 (ADR-118).
