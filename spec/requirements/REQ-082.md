# REQ-082: Probability of profit (held)

Why: beginners ask "how likely is this to make money?" before anything else. Sensibull answers with a POP tile (study
2026-10-21). A probability can read as a forecast, so the owner held it until the legal review approves the label
(ADR-156 (1)); ADR-008's own example ("Estimated probability of profit: ~73%") already sets the precision rule.

Register: MR-015.

Decisions: ADR-156, ADR-152, ADR-008, ADR-055, ADR-003, ADR-063.
