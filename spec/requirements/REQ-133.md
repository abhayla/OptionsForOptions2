# REQ-133: Drop late replies; wait for the first price after a reconnect

Why: the 2026-10-21 research found two display defects (register rows MR-142 and MR-144). One, seen in a studied app,
draws an old answer over a newer edit (REQ-119 owns the request/response half; this requirement adds the live-socket
half); the other is in OFO2 today: a screen that has just reconnected reports
DATA_UNHEALTHY before its first tick arrives. Both show the user a wrong number or a false alarm.

Example: during a fast edit the live P&L shown is the 3-lot figure, never the 2-lot frame that arrived late (AC-2); after a Wi-Fi drop at 11:20 the chain shows "Waiting for the first live price" for one second, then
live prices, with no red banner (AC-3).

Decisions: ADR-015, ADR-008. Overlap with REQ-119 and REQ-114: REQ-119 is the complete rule for late replies (MR-142);
REQ-133 keeps only the socket-version and first-price rules (MR-144); REQ-114 AC-5 (switch to live on the first fresh tick)
is the connection side of the same case.
