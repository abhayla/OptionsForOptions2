# REQ-148: Calendar and mixed-expiry strategies in the builder

Why: calendar and diagonal spreads put legs on two expiries, and Sensibull analyses them (screen 104). On 2026-10-27
OFO2 answered HTTP 500 when one leg was moved to 9 Nov, and the summary and chart vanished (issue #586). The defect
fix is separate; this requirement states what the builder shows, so the fix is tested against a written rule.
