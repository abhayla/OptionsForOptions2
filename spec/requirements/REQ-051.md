# REQ-051: Historical storage tiers and simulation

History is kept in tiers, built from the live feed where licensed; simple simulation comes after the live core.

Decisions: ADR-013. Sources: M §36, §72; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §16; H2 §17.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-15 and F-25 - showing past or expected returns of a strategy exposes the
associated broker and AP (SEBI order of 25 Mar 2026; NSE/COMP/55482 §5.7); verified past performance is limited to
PaRRVA-registered IAs, RAs and algo services. F-23 - historical exchange data needs its own rights. Open as Q261.
F-34 (2026-10-08) - one-minute bars built from our own ticks match Kite's candles on close and OI but not on open, high
and low, and a feed drop leaves a gap; so the one-minute tier is built live and made final from Kite's candles after
the close (ADR-067, internal use per ADR-066).

**Open:** Under the interim Zerodha source, historical data is deferred. See `spec/open-questions.md`.
