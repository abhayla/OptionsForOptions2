# REQ-051: Historical storage tiers and simulation

History is kept in tiers, built from the live feed where licensed; simple simulation comes after the live core.

Decisions: ADR-013. Sources: M §36, §72; C Q110–Q113, Q168, Q169, Q177, Q183; H1 §16; H2 §17.

**Open:** Under the interim Zerodha source, historical data is deferred. See `spec/open-questions.md`.
