# REQ-023: Paid Pro and billing

Pro costs ₹600/month, paid through Razorpay behind a replaceable adapter, with admin-configurable commercial rules.

Decisions: ADR-026. Sources: M §68; H2 §7; CH1 Q66; Q64 = A, Q66 = A.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-26 - an Authorised Person "shall not charge any amount from the
clients"; who sells Pro is open for owner decision as Q260. F-28 - payment-gateway, e-mandate and GST facts (unverified
until re-read in Stage 3).

**Open:** Legal check of a paid subscription sold by a Zerodha AP: Q211. See `spec/open-questions.md`.
