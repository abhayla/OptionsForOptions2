# REQ-066: Compliance gates

Features with legal or licensing exposure do not reach production until a compliance review is recorded.

Decisions: ADR-003, ADR-029. Sources: M §2, §70; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); open-questions Q211.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-02):** F-06 - whether a SaaS that sends many unrelated users' orders counts as an
"algo provider" (exchange empanelment, strategy registration, a static IP whose sharing Zerodha limits to family) is a
broker-integration-terms item for AC-1; open for owner decision as Q258 and part of the Q211 legal review.
Primary text read 2026-10-07: F-11 (SEBI circular, empanelment of algo providers) and F-12 (NSE implementation
standards, static IP per client); the legal review gets these as evidence, not as a verdict.
