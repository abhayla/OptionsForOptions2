# REQ-115: Background workers

Why: monitoring is the second half of the product and nothing runs it today (register MR-114). It has to survive
restarts, stop when its data is stale, and never become a way to send orders without the user.

Example: the worker restarts at 12:05 after a deploy. By 12:06 the monitors for all 14 active strategies are rebuilt from
the database; strategy 9's "Watch" alert sent at 11:50 is not sent again; strategy 11, whose feed is stale, shows
"Monitoring paused".

Technical decision (orchestrator, master register 2026-10-21, TECH row MR-114).

Decisions: ADR-009, ADR-112, ADR-153, ADR-015, ADR-053.
