# REQ-135: Builder leg list housekeeping

Why: four small controls a planner uses every session. Sensibull clears all trades in one click, shows days left in
every expiry dropdown and stamps when prices were last read (study 2026-10-21); ADR-152 makes that the default. OFO2
drops Sensibull's manual refresh button because prices are pushed live (Sensibull recomputes every 30 seconds), and
keeps the stale-data label of ADR-015 in place of an old time.
