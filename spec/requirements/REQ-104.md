# REQ-104: Import of Zerodha orders and positions

Why: users who traded on Kite before joining, or who place some trades on Kite directly, want those trades inside their
strategies so the app's P&L and monitoring cover them (ADR-160 (3)).

Example: the user bought 65 NIFTY 24500 CE on Kite in the morning. Import lists it as importable; the user chooses
"Add to strategy 9"; the strategy now holds 130 with the Kite average 7.025 kept exactly, and reconciliation runs.

Decisions: ADR-160, ADR-084, ADR-096.
