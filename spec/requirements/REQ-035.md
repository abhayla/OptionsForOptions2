# REQ-035: Single strategy table

A strategy's legs and scenarios appear in one horizontally scrollable table with a locked column order.

Decisions: ADR-008. Sources: M §23, §26, §27; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §30, §34, §35.

## Owner clarification (2026-09-29 morning, Q227): Guided scenario headings
- AC-7's "NIFTY at expiry | You make/lose" is a PAIR of headings for the scenario section, as in the owner-reviewed T1
  table (`| NIFTY at expiry | Strategy P&L |`), not a label repeated on every column. Each scenario column's own
  heading is its index level (e.g. 22,900), with the CURRENT and 0-P&L columns marked as such (e.g. "CURRENT 23,047",
  "0-P&L 23,491"). The phrase is the caption of the scenario section. (Issue #21.)

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q233): TOTAL row P&L % and Entry Value
- **TOTAL P&L % = unrealized P&L ÷ max loss** (risk-based), shown with the "estimate"/live labelling of the P&L it
  uses; "—" when max loss is UNLIMITED or zero. Example (golden condor, spec §6 fixture, 75 units): credit ₹91, 200-point wings, max loss (200 − 91) × 75 = ₹8,175;
  unrealized P&L at the fixture LTPs (91 − 72.80) × 75 = +₹1,365; TOTAL P&L % = +16.7%. (Percent of
  premium is not used: for a credit strategy it reads −200% on a ₹182 loss after ₹91 credit.)
- **TOTAL Entry Value** is shown only for options-only strategies (the NET entry premium: sells minus buys, × quantity, labelled Cr for a credit and Dr for a debit —
  golden condor: (86 + 91.50 − 42.50 − 44) × 75 = ₹6,825 Cr; owner Q236 corrected the earlier ambiguous "signed as the
  legs" wording); a strategy with any futures leg shows "—", because premium and futures notional can't be added.
- **Clarification (orchestrator, 2026-09-29, W-034; confirmed by the owner, Q242):** TOTAL P&L % also shows "—" when any
  leg has no live price (no unrealized P&L to divide) or the strategy spans more than one expiry (the engine gives an
  exact max loss for single-expiry strategies only). Reason: the table never shows a number the one engine cannot
  compute (ADR-008).
- **Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q241): no moneyness (ITM/ATM/OTM) column or badge.** The locked column order stays as
  reviewed; Status keeps the T1 values (leg "Open", total "Healthy").
- **Owner decision (2026-10-10): the leg picker is REQ-035 AC-8.** ADR-068 item 4 put a minimal leg picker on the
  stage-4a Builder screen but cited REQ-067 AC-1 (data proven first), which cannot carry its evidence; REQ-027
  (strike selection modes) is about suggestions that do not exist yet. Example: a user building the W-063 NIFTY
  13-Oct iron condor picks four legs here (SELL 22800CE, BUY 23000CE, SELL 22400PE, BUY 22200PE, 1 lot each).
- **Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q242):** the scenario caption ("NIFTY at expiry | You make/lose", SENSEX for SENSEX)
  is shown at every UX level, not only Guided (Standard and Advanced keep the same scenario columns and add more).
