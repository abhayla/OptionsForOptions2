# REQ-152: Number format of builder figures

Why: summary figures with paise and no separator are hard to read at a glance ("-14118.00"), while Sensibull shows whole signed rupees ("+6,026", "-6,975"). The paise stay where they matter: per-unit prices and charges. Owner choice: Indian grouping without the L abbreviation; Sensibull uses 1.89L for large values.
