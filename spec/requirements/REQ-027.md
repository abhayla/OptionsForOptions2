# REQ-027: Strike selection modes

Strike suggestions come in four modes, labelled 'Suggested setup', always overridable.

Decisions: ADR-005. Sources: M §16; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §15.

**Open:** Legal review: Q211. See `spec/open-questions.md`.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q249): what the strike modes mean
- The modes are defined by the **short strike's delta** (Black-Scholes delta per scenario-calculations §4 and Q248):
  **Conservative 0.10–0.15, Balanced 0.20–0.25, Aggressive 0.30–0.35** (absolute delta; admin-tunable bands). Wing
  widths stay at each template's default. Every suggested strike stays user-editable (AC-4); Manual Custom sets none.
  Example (NIFTY 23,047, Iron Condor): Conservative sells roughly 23,600 CE / 22,500 PE; Aggressive roughly 23,250 CE /
  22,850 PE (illustrative — the real strikes come from the engine and the catalogue).
