# REQ-021: Referral rewards

A successful qualifying Zerodha referral earns 30 days of Pro (admin-configurable), stackable by default.

Decisions: ADR-025. Sources: M §67; H2 §7; Q61 = A, Q63 = C.

**Open:** How success reaches the platform: Q209. See `spec/open-questions.md`.
