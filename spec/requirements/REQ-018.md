# REQ-018: Trial and Limited/Read-Only mode

Every new user gets a 7-day Pro trial; afterwards, without another entitlement, they become Limited, never locked out.

Decisions: ADR-023. Sources: M §62, §63; H2 §7, §11; CH1 Q67, Q90, Q91; Q88–Q93.

**Open:** Trial users without Zerodha have no price source: Q206. See `spec/open-questions.md`.
