# REQ-140: Profit left and loss left for a tracked strategy

Why: once a trade is on, "how much more can this make or lose from here" matters more than the maximums at entry.
Sensibull leads with Profit left / Loss left for existing trades (study 2026-10-21); ADR-152 makes that the default.
