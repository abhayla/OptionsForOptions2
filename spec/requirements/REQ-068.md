# REQ-068: Strategy preferences

Users mark preferred strategy types; the Guided Builder shows valid setups of those types first and the Builder offers related alternatives without ever replacing the current strategy on its own.

Decisions: ADR-032. Sources: T2 #35-#80; Q112-Q118, Q128-Q133.

**Open:** Preferences never override market validity, risk limits, broker constraints or the user's range. See `spec/open-questions.md`.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q250)
- "Valid" setups (AC-4) and the "effective range" are defined in REQ-069 "Owner decision (Q250)".

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q251): level, market view and objective of each strategy
The owner chose to apply these rules directly (no separate review of a 21-row table):
- **Beginner:** long call, long put, bull call / bull put / bear call / bear put vertical spreads.
- **Intermediate:** Iron Condor, Iron Butterfly, short strangle, short straddle, covered call, cash-secured put.
- **Advanced:** calendars, diagonals, ratio spreads, Jade Lizard, broken-wing butterfly, and any strategy with naked
  short legs beyond a short straddle/strangle.
- Each strategy also carries one or more **market views** (bullish / bearish / range-bound / volatile) and one or more
  **objectives** (income / directional / hedge / volatility). A template these rules do not clearly place is labelled
  by the nearest rule and listed in the building work item's report for the owner to see.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q254): what makes a change "material" (AC-5)
- A change is material when the strategy's **structure** changes (a leg added or removed, or the template match
  changes) **or** its **max loss or any breakeven moves by 5% or more** (admin-tunable). Anything smaller (e.g. a
  one-strike nudge of a short leg) is not material and raises no notification. Removing a protective wing is always
  material (structure change).
