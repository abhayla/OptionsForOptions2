# REQ-130: Product analytics and session replay, opt-in and held

Why: the owner applied the Sensibull default (ADR-152) and held this feature behind the consent design (ADR-156 (2)).
Error tracking is separate and is in REQ-126 now.

Example: Rahul turns on "Help improve the product" in Settings; his clicks in the builder are counted as
"builder.shift_click" events with no strikes or prices (AC-3). Anil leaves it off; his browser never contacts the
analytics host (AC-2).

Decisions: ADR-156, ADR-152. Register row: MR-150.
