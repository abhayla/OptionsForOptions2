# REQ-107: History, IV and OI charts (held)

Why: traders judge whether options are cheap or dear and where positions are building by looking at history, as
Sensibull's screens let them (ADR-158). The data is licensed to the platform for use; showing it to users needs display
rights nobody has confirmed yet.

Example: once rights are confirmed, the builder shows "IV percentile 72 (last 1 year)" for NIFTY; before that the field is
absent.

Held until: written display rights for historical data, and the charting library's licence check (ADR-158).

The strategy's own price history (register row MR-026) belongs with the builder screens, not here.

Decisions: ADR-158 (SPEC CHANGE to ADR-066 and REQ-047 AC-1).
