# REQ-005: Product language and positioning

All product text positions the platform as decision support, never as advice or a promise.

Decisions: ADR-003. Sources: M §2, §8, §16, §31; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §1, §6, §15; H2 §2; CH1 continuation prompt.

**Open:** Legal review of advisory-like wording: Q211. See `spec/open-questions.md`.

**Findings (spec/findings.md, 2026-10-07):** F-15 - SEBI penalised a stock broker for its association with a SaaS algo
platform whose strategies showed "guaranteed returns/misleading content" (order of 25 Mar 2026; circular of 2 Sep 2022,
clause 4.2: brokers must disassociate from platforms referring to "past or expected future return/performance of an
algorithm"). What our screens say therefore bears on Zerodha and on the owner as a Zerodha Authorised Person; AC-2,
AC-3 and AC-6 are the existing guard. Open for owner decision whether past-performance or backtest display needs a
further rule.
