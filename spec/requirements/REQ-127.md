# REQ-127: DPDP duties

Why: REQ-074 AC-3 says a separate requirement must cover the DPDP Rules' operative duties before 13 May 2027 (F-27).
The 2026-10-21 register (MR-136) found no such requirement yet, and ADR-156 (2) makes product analytics and session
replay depend on the user's consent, which needs the consent record of AC-2.

Example: Priya signs up, accepts the required notice and leaves analytics off. She uses the builder for a month; no
analytics event carries her id (AC-2, AC-3). She then asks to delete her account: her strategies and profile are gone
within 30 days, and only the Client ID-to-trial record and the legally required logs remain, each with its period
(AC-5).

Decisions: ADR-156, ADR-022. Findings: F-27. Open: Q211 (legal review of the wording and periods).
