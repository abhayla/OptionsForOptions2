# REQ-108: Events calendar and FII/DII activity (held)

Why: a user holding a short strangle over an RBI policy day wants to know the event is coming before expiry; Sensibull
shows events and FII/DII flows (ADR-156 (3), ADR-161 (2)). Both need an outside data source the platform does not have.

Example: a NIFTY strategy expiring 4 Nov lists "RBI policy decision - Thu 30 Oct, 10:00" and "Weekly expiry - Tue 28
Oct". The Market page shows "FII net -1,245.30 cr, DII net +2,010.75 cr - 20 Oct 2026 - source <vendor>".

Held until: events - data source, licence and cost cleared (ADR-156 (3)); FII/DII - data source, licence and cost cleared
(ADR-161 (2)).

Decisions: ADR-156, ADR-161.
