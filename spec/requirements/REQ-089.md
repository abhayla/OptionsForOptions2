# REQ-089: Manual P&L entries

Why: a user who adjusted a strategy outside the app, or carried a loss from an earlier position, wants the strategy's
numbers to reflect their whole trade. Sensibull lets them add a manual amount (study 2026-10-21); the owner chose the
same, kept visibly apart from the P&L Zerodha can confirm (ADR-161 (1), a SPEC CHANGE to ADR-102).

Register: MR-031.

Decisions: ADR-161, ADR-152, ADR-102, ADR-008, ADR-018.
