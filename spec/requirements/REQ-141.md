    Opening the Positions tab reads the user's Zerodha positions fresh (never a cached copy older than the read) and

- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Zerodha's answer by state: stated lists the positions; empty shows "No open positions in Zerodha"; failed or
    timed out shows "Could not read Zerodha" with no automatic retry and a Refresh button; no broker session shows
    "Connect Zerodha to see your positions"; a position whose contract is not one of the four indices' options or
    futures (ADR-157) or has no instrument row is listed as "Not supported in this app" and cannot be ticked; the
    same contract under two products is listed once per product, never merged.
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    Ticked positions load into the builder as an unsaved draft: one leg per position with the same contract, side and
    quantity, entered at Zerodha's average price exactly as reported (ADR-126) and labelled "Zerodha average price";
    every builder number is then computed by the engine as for any other draft. Loading replaces nothing without the
    user's confirm when the builder already holds legs.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    Analyse only (ADR-196 (4), ADR-165): the tab and the loaded draft attach nothing to a strategy, save nothing,
    reconcile nothing and reach no order call; nothing from Zerodha's book is stored (ADR-138 (3)). The user may save
    the draft as a new strategy only through the ordinary Save, as a plan with no link to the Zerodha positions.

# REQ-141: Positions tab, analyse only

Why: a user with an open trade wants to see its payoff, Greeks and what-if numbers in the builder without typing every
leg. Sensibull's builder has a Positions tab that does this. The owner put the tab in this phase as analyse only
(ADR-196 (4), P10): attaching, grouping and reconciling (REQ-104 AC-2 / AC-3, REQ-060, REQ-061) come with the orders
phase.
