# REQ-053: Broker authority and contract eligibility

Zerodha is the final authority on eligibility, margin and execution; the platform never recreates its risk rules.

Decisions: ADR-016. Sources: M §42, §43; C-file (real Q numbers: spec/traceability/question-register.md); H1 §18, §19; CH1 eligibility answer.

## Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q244): protecting the catalogue from a truncated download
- A daily catalogue update is **refused if it would remove any contract whose expiry has not yet passed** (at the
  time of the update). Normal roll-off of expired contracts and the addition of new expiries are accepted. Replaces the
  W-006 per-index "lose at most 50% of contracts" rule, which accepted a truncated file that cut an index by 40% and
  so unlisted live contracts until the next good download. (Issue #10, item 2.)

## Decision (2026-10-02, Q257, owner delegation): revised contract terms follow Zerodha, with history
- When Zerodha's list carries an existing contract (same exchange and instrument_token) with a revised **lot size,
  tick size, expiry or trading symbol**, the stored contract takes the new value, and each change is recorded in an
  append-only history (contract, field, old value, new value, time stamped by the database). Zerodha is the authority
  (AC-1, ADR-016); refusing the revision would freeze every later daily update.
- Identity never changes: instrument_token, exchange, exchange_token, segment, underlying, instrument type and strike.
  A list that changes any of them for a stored token is refused with nothing written.

## Decision (2026-10-02, owner, ADR-050): one identity across brokers - SPEC CHANGE to the Q257 identity list
- Changed text: Q257's identity list above named Zerodha's `instrument_token` as part of a contract's identity. From
  2026-10-02 a contract's identity is **(exchange segment, exchange token)** - the exchange's own contract number, the
  same at every Indian broker (spec/findings.md F-01) - cross-checked by underlying, expiry, strike and instrument type.
  Zerodha's `instrument_token` and trading symbol are Zerodha's data in the per-broker table (F-02, F-03), not identity.
  Revisable terms (lot size, tick size, expiry, trading symbol) still follow the source with history (Q257); lot size,
  tick size and freeze limit are per-broker data with the date they were seen (F-04). Expiry is read from the source,
  never computed from a weekday (F-05). Work item W-056 re-keys the stored catalogue.

## Findings (2026-10-07)
- F-20 - the exchanges' own 6 Oct 2026 files: NIFTY lot 65 (1,918 of 1,918 option rows), expiries on Tuesdays and some
  Mondays; SENSEX lot 20 (603 of 603), expiries on Thursdays.
- F-21 - exchange contract numbers come from a bounded range (NSE: 1-31,980 and 750,001-999,999); reuse after expiry
  is plausible but unverified (#116), so the identity in the ADR-050 decision may need the expiry to stay unique over
  time. Unverified until stream S3's data count.
