- id: AC-1
  text: >-
    "Add to Paper" (ADR-207 (1), Sensibull "Add to Drafts", study 166-173; replaces "Start tracking", ADR-166) is an
    action on one strategy in the builder; an unsaved strategy is saved first under its shown name. It opens a confirm
    list of the ticked legs - B/S, contract, lots and price, each price editable and defaulting to the leg's builder
    entry price (REQ-077) - with the footer "This is not a real trade. Paper orders use no real money."; Confirm
    records one paper "open" order per leg (side, quantity, price, time) in one transaction. A price that is not a
    positive multiple of the tick (0.05) is refused in the dialog and by the server. There is no account-wide
    paper/live switch; paper on one strategy never changes how any other strategy behaves (MB trap T5).
- id: AC-2
  text: >-
    Paper P&L is computed by the one engine (ADR-008) from the paper entry prices and current prices, exact Decimal,
    shown to the paisa as "Paper P&L (not real): +1,240.50", and is never summed with, or shown in the same figure as,
    any real P&L (ADR-102).
- id: AC-3
  text: >-
    A paper-tracked strategy can never be executed as paper: Execute, the basket builder and the trigger builder
    (REQ-100) refuse a paper entry, and a test fails if a paper record reaches them. "Trade this for real" opens the
    normal Execute flow on the strategy definition, with fresh prices, creating a real plan (ADR-105), and the paper
    record stays separate.
- id: AC-4
  text: >-
    A paper position is an order book (ADR-207 (2)): every paper order - open, add, exit, partial exit, settle, stop -
    is an append-only row (side, quantity, price, time, kind) labelled paper (REQ-040). The one engine derives the net
    position per contract from the orders: net quantity, average price (the average of the orders that increased the
    absolute quantity, Zerodha's F&O rule), booked P&L when the quantity falls ((exit - average) x quantity, by side)
    and unbooked P&L of the open quantity at its live price. "Exit all" and a per-leg exit go through the same confirm
    list, pre-filled at the LTP (editable price; editable quantity up to the open quantity, so an exit can be partial
    by quantity); after hours the price is required and labelled "last close". A booked figure never moves again.
- id: AC-5
  text: >-
    When a leg's price is stale or missing (ADR-015), paper P&L shows "Paper P&L paused - prices not live, as of <time>";
    a paper exit at that moment is refused with "Prices are not live; try again when they update". Outside market
    hours (ADR-212, SPEC CHANGE 2026-10-30) the paper screens instead show LTP and P&L from the last close under the
    market-closed banner, as the builder does (ADR-197); paper fills stay refused.
- id: AC-6
  text: >-
    Prices come from the user's own live feed; without a Zerodha session (no connection, or after the 06:00 expiry,
    ADR-053) paper tracking uses delayed prices only when REQ-105's mode is on and labels every figure "on delayed
    prices"; otherwise it pauses as AC-5.
- id: AC-7
  text: >-
    A paper leg still open on its expiry day settles with a "settle" order at its intrinsic value from the official
    closing value of its index that day (NIFTY on NSE, SENSEX on BSE; ADR-207 (6)): max(0, close - strike) for a call,
    max(0, strike - close) for a put, the close for a future; labelled "Paper - settled at expiry". A job runs at 15:45
    IST each trading day and at app start (catch-up for any past expiry). The close is read from Kite's day candle for
    the index, which carries the exchange's official close (F-72). When the official close cannot be read from
    an allow-listed source the leg stays open with "settlement price not available", never a guessed value.
- id: AC-8
  text: >-
    Monitoring rules and alerts may run on a paper strategy; every such alert says "Paper strategy" first, and none
    prepares a real basket. A paper strategy can carry a Conditional Exit (the index crosses above or below a set level
    and every open leg exits) and a per-leg stop (the leg's LTP crosses above or below a set price and that leg exits)
    (ADR-207 (5), Sensibull bundle-findings 7.4): during market hours the server records "stop" paper orders at the LTP
    of the tick that crossed, fires each trigger once (also across a restart), and raises an in-app alert (header bell
    and Alerts page) naming the strategy, what fired and the exit prices. No order, GTT or alert reaches Zerodha
    (ADR-165); a trigger set after hours arms at 09:15 IST.
- id: AC-9
  text: >-
    Opening a paper strategy in the builder enters Drafts Mode (ADR-207 (2), Sensibull catalogue 274-279): the legs are
    the NET position at average prices, not the saved plan; the legs panel has a dashed frame and the label "Paper mode
    - not real trades", and a Net Positions | Orderbook switch. Every leg edit (strike step, lots, remove, add, shift)
    becomes pending paper orders shown as "N pending changes": per contract, the orders that move its net quantity to
    the edited one (a strike step onto a contract not held = an exit of the old contract and an add of the new one; onto
    a contract held on the other side = two exits, because the positions net). "Review and add" opens the confirm
    list with the exact orders; nothing changes until Confirm, and
    "Discard" restores the net position. Save is not offered in Drafts Mode, so a plan can never differ silently from
    the paper position (F-65, F-66). The summary shows "Profit left" and "Loss left" (the open legs' expiry P&L range
    from their LTP) first, with the original Max Profit / Max Loss below; Greeks and payoff are the net position's.
- id: AC-10
  text: >-
    Each paper strategy shows a Charges figure beside its P&L (not subtracted from it; Sensibull study 050), the sum of
    the charges engine's figure for every paper order (ADR-082, ADR-207 (7)); a settlement whose charge rule the engine
    lacks counts 0 and the figure says "excludes settlement charges", never an estimate.

# REQ-109: Paper tracking of a saved strategy

Why: a beginner wants to see how a plan would have done without risking money, as Sensibull Drafts and every studied app
allow (register MR-124). A per-strategy record avoids the global paper switch that, in another app, diverted every
running strategy at once.

Example: the user saves a NIFTY bull call spread at 10:00 and presses "Start tracking"; entry 24500 CE at 112.40 and 24700 CE
at 38.15 are stored. At 14:00 the strategy shows "Paper P&L (not real): +1,462.50". The user presses "Paper exit"; the
figure freezes.

Decisions: ADR-152 (Sensibull default; owner re-triage Q-3); ADR-207 (paper as Sensibull's Draft Portfolios: Add to
Paper, order book, Drafts Mode, settlement, paper stops, charges; findings F-65 to F-70). Existing tracked legs migrate
into the order book as one "open" order each and each recorded exit as one "exit" order, with no change to any P&L.
