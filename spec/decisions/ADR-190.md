# ADR-190: take kit 1.15.0; measure before quoting a saving

Kit 1.15.0 adds instruments, not process: a red-run report and a budget report. The project adopts both and changes
nothing else in ADR-189's process except the two open owner questions (Q3, Q4). The saving the Factory expects is not
recorded until three rows are measured against the 10-23 audit baseline.
