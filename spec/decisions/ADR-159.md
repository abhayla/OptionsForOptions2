# ADR-159: MIS as a product choice

Why: the owner chose to match Sensibull. The risk the owner was shown - a planned multi-day strategy closed by the
broker the same afternoon - is handled by stating the cut-off before Execute and by reconciliation recognising the
broker's exit.
