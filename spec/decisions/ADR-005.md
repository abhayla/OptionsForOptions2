# ADR-005: Strategy creation, discovery, expected range, preferences and strike modes

## Decision
**Guided flow:** 1 What are you trying to achieve? → 2 market/view/risk/capital → 3 strategies to consider →
4 configure → 5 show max profit, max loss, breakevens, margin, charges, payoff, risk → 6 entry/exit/adjustment
conditions → 7 review → 8 prepare/execute.
Objectives: Earn premium · Limit risk · Benefit if market rises · Benefit if market falls · Trade a big move ·
I already know my strategy. Experienced users can pick a known strategy directly.

**Discovery** (§8, Q128) may use objective, market view, risk preference, capital, expected range and constraints.
Wording per ADR-003.

**Expected range** (§17, Q129, Q130, Q135): for a chosen timeframe/expiry (this month end, upcoming expiry, monthly,
next monthly) the user picks a lower and an upper expected level. Two pick lists around the current price in
~100-point steps: lower list = current and below; upper list = current and above; lower ≤ current ≤ upper. The range
is an input for evaluating candidates and risk, **not a prediction**. Ask extra questions only where data cannot be
inferred.

**Preferences** (§18, Q131): e.g. "prefers Iron Condor", "Iron Condor + Short Call". Used with range, market and
constraints to surface currently valid matches. Never silently forced.

**Strike modes** (§16, Q136, Q137): Conservative · Balanced · Aggressive · Manual Custom. Inputs may include spot,
expiry, premiums, IV, expected move, capital, max loss, desired premium. Output labelled *Suggested setup*, never
*Recommended trade*. Manual override always available.

## Gaps
- 100-point pick-list step is stated for NIFTY; SENSEX step not decided (Q208).
- The handoff lists "this weekend by selected expiry" as a timeframe; its meaning is unclear and is not carried
  into the spec until clarified.
