# ADR-005: Strategy creation: guided builder, discovery, expected range and setups

## Owner answers
- **Q3 = C** (T1 #7): strategy-first default + Manual Builder. **Q10 = B** (T1 #21): guided/conversational builder +
  Advanced Manual Builder; numbers are never hidden (max profit, max loss, breakeven, margin, capital at risk, charges,
  payoff, ±1%/±2% moves) and appear in plain language first (*What can I lose? What can I make? Where do I start
  losing?*).
- **Q12 = B** (T1 #25): start from the desired outcome — *Earn premium · Limit my risk · Benefit if the market rises ·
  Benefit if the market falls · Trade a big expected move · I already know my strategy*.
- **Q16 = C** (T1 #33): strike setups *Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive* + *Customize manually*; labelled
  "Suggested setup", never "Recommended trade"; assumptions shown; never placed silently.
- **Q102 = C** (T2 #13): two entry paths — *Guided Strategy Builder* ("Help me build a strategy") and *Advanced Manual
  Builder* ("I know what I want").
- **Q103 = C**: 2–4 strategies prominently + *See more*. **Q104 = C**: layered strategy card (name, market view, max
  profit/loss, breakevens; *View details* for margin, payoff, risk).
- **Q105 = C**: simple market view first (bullish/bearish/neutral/big move/unsure), optional detail (expected move,
  volatility, time horizon).
- **Q106 = C + owner refinement** (T2 #21): *Not sure* → the user picks an expiry and an **expected lower and upper
  market level**; the platform derives the market characteristics and risk and asks only what it cannot infer.
- **Q107 = C**: real expiry dates with labels (*This Week — 24 Sep · Next Week · Monthly · Next Monthly*).
- **Q108** (owner, T2 #25): two **pick lists, 100-point steps**, extending a few thousand points each way.
- **Q109** (owner, T2 #29): lower list = current level and everything below; higher list = current level and
  everything above; so lower ≤ current ≤ higher always; both may equal current.
- **Q110 = C**: both start at the current level; the platform never pre-assumes a range.
- **Q111 = C**: show *"Based on your expected range of X–Y by <expiry>, here are strategies you could consider"*.
- **Q119 = C**: per preferred strategy type, a small curated set of setups (Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive) +
  *View more setups*. A **setup** = one fully configured version of a strategy (T2 #56), not an order.
- **Q120 = C**: layered setup cards (summary + payoff thumbnail; *View details* for legs, payoff, breakevens, margin,
  charges, assumptions, why it fits). **Q121 = C**: *Why you're seeing this*, expandable to detailed reasons.
- **Q122 = C**: review one at a time, plus *Compare*. **Q123 = C**: a chosen setup loads into the Builder in a visible
  *Suggested Setup* state with assumptions and risk summary.
- **Q124 = C**: fully editable; every meaningful change recalculates max P/L, breakevens, P&L, margin, charges,
  payoff, risk; shows when the strategy no longer matches the original range.
- **Q125 = C**: if it drifts from the original range: *Keep original range · Update my expected range · Continue
  anyway*. **Q126 = C**: *Continue anyway* shows original range vs effective risk range, max P/L, breakevens, margin,
  risk changes, then asks for confirmation.
- **Q127 = C**: keep original expiry/range, the generated setup, every user change and final configuration as
  strategy context and audit history.
