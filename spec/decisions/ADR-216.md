decision: >-
  The iron condor system playbook ships with the catalogue's PB-04 numbers, no longer labelled "Draft": entry hint
  shorts at 0.15-0.20 delta, wings 200-400 NIFTY points beyond, at least 5 days left; flag an adjustment when the
  tested short's delta reaches 0.30 (T-03), spot is within 0.5% of a short strike (T-01), the premium still left is
  less than the cost of adjusting (T-19), or the loss reaches 50% of max loss (T-06); profit target 50% of max
  profit; stop line a loss of 50% of max loss or 4% of margin, whichever comes first; at most 2 adjustments; on
  expiry day, close any spread whose short is within 0.3% of spot by 14:30. A user's own playbook may change every
  value. The other system playbooks (PB-01..PB-03, PB-05..PB-13) stay "Draft" until the owner approves them.
  Per stage (REQ-159 AC-2), owner 2026-10-31: the per-stage values of mockup screen 4
  (tests/fixtures/adjust_scenarios/_playbooks.json, iron condor family) are the defaults, with ONE change: T-19 fires
  when the premium still left is below 1x the adjustment's cost (screen 4 had 2x). Weekly Early 5+ / Middle 2-4 /
  Last day: T-01 spot within 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.3 % of a short; T-03 tested short delta 0.30 / 0.35 / 0.40; T-06 loss
  50 / 50 / 40 % of max loss; T-16 loss 4 / 4 / 3 % of margin; T-19 1 / 1 / 1 x cost. Monthly stages and the other
  triggers on by default take screen 4's values as they stand. The single numbers above are the guards (profit
  target, stop line, maximum adjustments, entry guide, expiry-day rule).
