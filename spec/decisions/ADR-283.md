title: 'A rule starting point is the market at the fill (entry or adjustment N), stored with the fill; a time-of-day rule is never "already true"'
decision: >-
  Decided under the owner's strategy delegation (owner 2026-11-09 ~15:00 IST: "Wherever you have question, go with
  your recommendation"), GC-ICGUIDE, after an independent review of the second occurrence of finding
  rule-entry-baseline-taken-at-first-check. (1) Implements ADR-258 as written ("right after the basket fills, each
  rule is re-evaluated"): the starting point of every playbook rule after the entry and after each adjustment basket N
  is the market state at that basket's fill (order clock - the scenario clock in test-data mode -, spot, ATM IV and
  every open leg's price), stored with the fill in the same transaction, like the entry facts (ADR-243). It is never
  the state of whichever check happens to run first. The background worker, the screen check and the playbook test
  read the same stored starting point. No stored starting point (an older basket, a fill with no quote): no rule is
  held, so a true rule warns. (2) A time-of-day rule (the plan's time exit, T-10b and any rule whose value is a clock
  time) is never "already true": once its time has passed it warns, even when the basket was placed after that time.
  Why: measured 2026-11-09 on test data (strategies 122 and 123), the expiry-day 14:30 time exit was shown "Already
  true when tracking started" at 14:31 and raised no warning, and a fresh T-03 crossing after adjustment 1 was hidden
  the same way, because the starting point was taken at the worker's first check (one or two frames late).
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Implements ADR-258 (starting point at the fill, not at the first check); adds (2) for time-of-day rules; extends ADR-243 entry facts to every adjustment basket.'
