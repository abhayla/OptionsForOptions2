# ADR-164: Max profit and loss as a percentage of funds

Why: a beginner sizes a trade against the money they have, not in rupees alone. The compliance concern (a profit percentage read as a return claim) is
reduced by tying the figure to the user's own funds, keeping ADR-003 wording, and listing it for the Q211 review.
