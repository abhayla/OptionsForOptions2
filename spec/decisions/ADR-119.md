# ADR-119: Execute only in NSE market hours

Why: a basket placed outside market hours would sit at Zerodha as an after-market order and fill at the next open, at
a price set from an old close (example: priced from Monday's close, filled at Wednesday's open after the Tuesday
holiday). The owner decided on 2026-10-19 to block it in the product, not to rely on Kite.
