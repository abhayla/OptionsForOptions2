# ADR-008: Calculation engine, outcome view and the single strategy table

## Owner answers
- **Q15 = B** (T1 #31): build our own engine (established libraries allowed for Black-Scholes/IV/Greeks); one engine
  for all screens; **no false precision** — e.g. *"Estimated probability of profit: ~73%"* with assumptions, never
  "73.48291%"; estimates are labelled estimates, not guarantees.
- **Q33** (T1 #83–#98): the owner turned the risk-acknowledgement question into this requirement: every user, novice or
  advanced, sees the strategy table with P&L at each market level.
  - One table, column order: **Leg, Action, Instrument, Expiry, Strike, Quantity, Entry Price, LTP, Entry Value,
    Current Value, Unrealized P&L, P&L %, IV, Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega**, then market-level columns 100 points apart, then
    **Lower BE, Upper BE, Status** (T1 #89–#91).
  - Entry price and live LTP are first-class columns and update live (T1 #85).
  - **Every market-level cell is filled for every leg** with the expiry formula, plus a strategy total row (T1 #93, #95).
  - Sticky left columns; horizontal scroll; current level highlighted.
- **Q33A = C** (T1 #111): scenario columns offer **At Expiry** (default) and **Estimated Now** (model-based,
  labelled as an estimate).
- **Q33B = E** (T1 #113): the range is chosen intelligently (spot, strikes, breakevens, risk boundaries, expected
  move) and the user can customise it.
- **Q33C** (T1 #115, owner: "B + current market price + 0 pnl"): the grid is anchored to **rounded 100-point index levels** (points, not ₹; ₹ is only for money); the
  exact live level and the **0-P&L (breakeven) levels** are shown in addition, not instead.
- **Q33D = C** (T1 #117): the CURRENT MARKET and 0-P&L/breakeven columns are **inserted at their actual price
  position** in the grid, e.g. `22,900 | 22,909 0-P&L | 23,000 | 23,047 CURRENT | 23,100 …`. Reconciled reading: the
  Lower BE / Upper BE summary columns after the grid (T1 #90) stay as well — see open-questions.
- Presentation by level (T1 #84): Guided *NIFTY at expiry | You make/lose*; Standard + % return, breakevens;
  Advanced + Greeks, IV assumptions.
- Strategy header (T1 #86): current underlying, strategy P&L, max profit, max loss, lower/upper breakeven, margin used,
  time to expiry, strategy health.

Formulas, precision and the golden example: `spec/business-rules/scenario-calculations.md`.
