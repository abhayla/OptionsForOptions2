decision: >-
  Decided under the owner rule of 14 Nov ("whenever you have question, go with your recommendation"), PENDING OWNER READ
  for the wording. (1) A paper trigger (a conditional exit on the index, or a leg stop) whose level is already crossed by
  the live price when it is saved (at or beyond the level, the same test that fires it) is refused with a plain message
  and nothing is stored: it would close the trade at once. (2) With no live price (market closed, no feed, a stale or
  failed read) it is saved and arms at the next live tick, as before. (3) Saving the same armed trigger again (same
  strategy, kind, direction, level and contract) returns the one that exists; a cancelled or fired one never blocks a
  new save. (4) Whenever paper orders change a strategy's legs, an armed leg stop on a contract with no open quantity is
  cancelled, and every armed trigger is cancelled when no leg is open; an index exit stays armed while any leg is open.
  OWNER DECIDED 23 Nov 2026 17:10 IST (points 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 are approved; point 4 still awaits the owner):
  (1) and (2) approved 17:12, with the wording "Not saved: the live price is already at or beyond this level, so this
  exit would close the trade straight away. To close now, use Exit." Not chosen: an "are you sure?" box (a hurried
  second click still closes the trade); saving it as before.
  (3) approved, with the wording: the second save of an identical armed trigger stores nothing and the panel says
  "Already set: exit above X" (or "Already set: stop above X"), never "Saved" again. (5) A save that does not go through

# ADR-317: a trigger is judged when it is saved, and follows the legs

Why: a level already crossed fires on the next tick, so a typo exits the trade; an armed trigger on a contract nobody holds is a stop that can never act.
