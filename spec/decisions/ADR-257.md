title: 'Arming a stop on a sold leg asks whether to close its wing too (default on)'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:1x IST (question tool, "Ask when arming"). When a user arms a per-leg stop (ADR-207 (5)) on a
  SOLD option that has a protective bought option of the same type and expiry in the same strategy (its wing), the
  stop row shows a tick box "also close its wing (NIFTY 17 Nov 25050 CE)", ticked by default. Ticked: when the stop
  fires, the sold leg and its wing are closed in one paper basket. Unticked: only the sold leg is closed (today's
  behaviour). The armed stop's line names what it will close. A stop on a bought leg, or on a sold leg with no wing,
  shows no tick box. Why: live test 2026-11-06 (strategy 89): the stop on the sold 24950 CE fired and left the bought
  25050 CE open alone, a stray long call.
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Extends ADR-207 (5) per-leg stops with an optional wing close.'
