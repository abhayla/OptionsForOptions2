# ADR-152: Sensibull as the default reference, with safety, legal and money exceptions

Why: the owner named Sensibull as the product reference in the proposed architecture and answered the first feature
fork with "go with what Sensibull does". A standing default cuts the owner's question load to the items only the
owner can decide, while keeping OFO2's stricter order-safety rules and the legal hold of ADR-055.
