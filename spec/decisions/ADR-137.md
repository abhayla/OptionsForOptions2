title: A blocked strategy is cleared by two actions - "Use Zerodha's numbers for this contract" and "Close via a basket"
decision: '(1) A strategy (or several sharing a contract) blocked by a reconciliation mismatch offers exactly two
  actions: "Use Zerodha''s numbers for this contract" and "Close via a basket". (2) "Use Zerodha''s numbers for this
  contract" is one recorded, audited action that covers every case approved before: Zerodha flat (ADR-116), Zerodha
  matching what all holders expect again (ADR-123), and an earlier-day plan the next-day read cannot resolve (ADR-125);
  it needs a fresh read at the moment of confirming under the per-user lock (#423) and no open order on that contract;
  each holder''s units on that contract become Zerodha''s numbers (attributed as ADR-116/123/125 already say), no order
  is placed, and any outside trade''s P&L reads "unknown (outside trade)". (3) "Close via a basket" is our exit basket,
  shorts first (REQ-058 Q238). (4) "Mark as requiring attention" becomes the "I''ll manage it on Kite" link (ADR-136).
  (5) The block never lifts on its own (ADR-120 (2)).'

# ADR-137: two ways to clear a blocked strategy

Why: four separately approved buttons did the same safe step in different situations; one audited action plus a close
basket keeps the safety (never automatic, fresh read, no open order) with less to learn.
