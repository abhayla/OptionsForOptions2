# ADR-039: Connecting an already-trialled Zerodha Client ID ends a running trial

## Decision
- **Q219 = A (owner, 2026-09-29).** The trial starts at registration (ADR-023, Q88) but trial history follows the
  Client ID (ADR-022, Q82). When account B, during its trial, connects a Client ID that used a trial on account A:
  B's trial **ends at that moment**; B sees "This Zerodha account already used its free trial" and can take the
  **verified account transfer** (Q82/Q83: brings A's history and any Pro) or **subscribe**.
- **Why.** Closes the "new email, 7 more free days" loophole the owner wanted closed (T1 #235), while a genuine user
  who lost account A keeps a route through the transfer.
- Written into ADR-022 and REQ-014 (AC-10).
