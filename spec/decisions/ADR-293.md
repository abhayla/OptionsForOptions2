# ADR-293: paper-only strategies can be deleted

Why: a user who practised on paper could never clean up the list; on 2026-11-13 the owner could delete 29 of 102
strategies. The earlier refusal (ADR-271) protected against leftovers; this decision hides the leftovers with the
strategy and keeps the real-order lock.
