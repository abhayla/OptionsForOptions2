title: 'Paper P&L and rule checks mark a leg at the bid-ask mid when its last trade is stale'
decision: >-
  Decided under the owner's standing delegation for strategy behaviour ("go with your recommendation", 2026-11-05 and
  2026-11-06 ~14:4x). Extends ADR-265 from captured entries to marking: a leg's mark for paper P&L, profit / loss
  left, the adjustment rule checks (T-02, T-06 and every rule reading P&L) and stops is its LTP while that last trade
  is not older than the latest two-sided quote and lies inside bid-ask; otherwise the bid-ask mid; with no two-sided
  quote, the LTP with a "last trade, no live quote" note. A defined-risk strategy's marked loss can never be shown or
  checked as larger than its max loss: when it would be, the mark is treated as inconsistent prices and the rule is
  "not checked" with that reason, never flagged. The basis is a setting per ADR-264 (admin default as above). Why:
  live test 2026-11-06: SENSEX paper condor 90 (19 Nov, 80100/80300 PE, 80700/80900 CE, max loss Rs 309 before
  charges) raised T-06 "Loss reaches 240.7767 % of max loss" at 14:25 from stale last trades, a false loss alert.
status: accepted
