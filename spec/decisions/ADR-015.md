# ADR-015: Market-data health and stale data

## Decision
- Every quote carries a health state: available · stale · delayed · unhealthy · unavailable (Q184).
- Rule evaluation records data health with each trigger (ADR-019, Q203) and does not silently fire important rules
  on stale data.
- Execution safety requires healthy market data where required (§79).
