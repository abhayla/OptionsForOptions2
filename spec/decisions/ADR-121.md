decision: '(1) In the "Not sure" range flow (REQ-026 AC-1), the platform derives the market view from the expected
  range: if the distance from the current level to one end is more than twice the distance to the other end, the view
  is that direction (up = mildly bullish, down = mildly bearish); otherwise it is range-bound (neutral). The screen
  states the reading in one line (for example "Your range leans up") and the user can change it before the cards
  appear. (2) The market-closed message of ADR-119 (3) writes the next trading day as weekday + day + short month, no
  year: "Market closed. You can review your plan; execution opens at 09:15 on Wed 21 Oct."'

# ADR-121: range reading and the next-trading-day format

Why: REQ-026 AC-1 said only that "the platform derives the market characteristics", and the S3 builder had to invent a
rule; ADR-119 fixed the sentence but not the date format. The owner settled both on 2026-10-19.
