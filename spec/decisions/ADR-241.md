title: 'Adjustment approach trades are priced at the live bid-ask mid first (SPEC CHANGE to ADR-068 (1) for the Compare)'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-03 (question tool, "Mid when spread is live"): every planned trade of an adjustment approach (the
  Compare's trades, its cost rows, and T-19's cheapest cost, which is built from the same trades) is priced at the
  bid-ask mid when both sides are live and the ask is at or above the bid, else at the last traded price (none when
  neither exists: the approach is refused naming the leg, as today). This is the engine's one IV price rule
  (ADR-172, ofo.engine.model.iv_price, including its health gate: a side is live only on an AVAILABLE book), reused,
  never a second rule. Real market prices only, never a model price (ADR-020 Q185).
  Why: on a quiet far strike the last traded price can be stale. Real case 2026-11-03, NIFTY 29 Dec 2026: 25,250 CE
  last price 117.75 while the nearer 25,200 CE was 73.65; the stale 117.75 went into a roll's cost.
  SPEC CHANGE: ADR-068 (1) "the leg's LTP captured when the leg is added (or the mid of bid and ask when no LTP
  exists)" no longer applies to adjustment approach trades. A draft leg's planned entry in the builder is unchanged
  (not part of this decision).
status: accepted
