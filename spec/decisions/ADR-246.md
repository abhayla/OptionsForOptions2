title: 'Alert-discipline presets kept after the first live day; the intraday preset is reviewed after two more expiry days'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-04 ~12:5x IST (question tool, "Keep presets for now"): the REQ-170 style presets stay as approved
  (intraday: confirm on a 1-min candle close, 5-min cool-down, quiet until 09:20, re-arm margin 10 %, daily limit 6;
  weekly and monthly unchanged). Basis, measured live 2026-11-04 09:30-11:50 (F-101): 27 suggestion records against
  103 in the same window on 2026-11-02 (-74 %), 8 lapsed on their own against 75 (-89 %); the one noisy case was a
  SENSEX iron condor with 1 day to expiry past its breakeven (strategy 84): 5 records in one hour, 4 self-lapsing,
  then the intraday daily limit held its further adjustment alerts (stop-losses are never held, REQ-170 AC-5, proven
  live 09:24). The intraday preset is reviewed again with the counts of two more expiry days (records, lapses and
  daily-limit holds per strategy per hour, the same read-only queries), and the owner decides then. As ADR-245, every
  preset value remains a default each user and each strategy can override.
status: accepted
