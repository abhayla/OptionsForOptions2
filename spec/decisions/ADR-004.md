# ADR-004: Target users and three UX levels

## Decision
Principle: *Simple on the surface, sophisticated underneath.*

| Level | Shows |
|---|---|
| Guided | Plain language, education, strong risk visibility, guided decisions, Greeks hidden by default |
| Standard | More control, Greeks, payoff, conditions, adjustments |
| Advanced | Full strategy controls, detailed Greeks/IV/expected move, advanced conditions, detailed execution controls |

Users switch level at any time. **Advanced cannot bypass** strategy-only trading, safety controls, margin checks,
broker restrictions or reconciliation blocks.

## Information density (15 Sep handoff §25)
- Default strategy view: status, P&L, target, risk, current underlying, range/condition, next thing to watch.
- Drill-down tabs: Overview · P&L · Risk · Payoff · Legs · Greeks · Conditions · Orders.
- Advanced exposes terminal-like detail.
- The 15 Sep handoff floated a *"What should I do now?"* prompt. It is **not carried over**: it conflicts with
  ADR-003's wording rules (no "you should"). Any such prompt needs a new decision.
