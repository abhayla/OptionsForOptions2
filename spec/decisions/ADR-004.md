# ADR-004: Users and one level for everyone

## Owner answers
- **Q2 = D** (T1 #5): beginner + intermediate, powerful enough for intermediate traders.
- **Q21 = D** (T1 #43): the full builder by default; the Advanced Details content is part of the one screen. The user
  does not need to read Greeks to know whether a strategy needs attention (level clause rewritten to one level by
  ADR-168 (4), 2026-10-24).
- **Q31 = A** (T1 #79, after the owner said UX must depend on experience, #77): superseded. The three-level
  definitions and the question *"How much guidance would you like?"* no longer apply; every user sees the full
  builder: templates, strike controls, conditions, payoff, Greeks, implied volatility, margin, charges, adjustments,
  simulation, leg configuration, expected move, advanced rules, detailed execution controls (level clause rewritten
  to one level by ADR-168 (4), 2026-10-24).
- **Q32 = A** (T1 #81): there is no level to switch. No user setting can: place a standalone order, bypass strategy
  validation, margin checks, broker restrictions, a critical broker rejection, or reconciliation blocks (level clause
  rewritten to one level by ADR-168 (4), 2026-10-24).
- **Q76** (T1 #223): the self-declared market experience may shape presentation, never trading permissions.

## Information density (T1 #42)
Default strategy card: status, P&L, target, risk, underlying vs range, "no action needed"/next thing to watch.
Drill-down tabs: Overview · P&L · Risk · Payoff · Legs · Greeks · Conditions · Orders.
