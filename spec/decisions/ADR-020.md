# ADR-020: Zerodha connection is optional until needed; drafts work without it

## Decision
- Optional until needed: execution, live positions/orders/account, direct-customer eligibility (Q79). Contextual
  prompt: *Connect your Zerodha account to continue. We'll use your Zerodha account to verify availability, check
  margin and prepare/execute your strategy.*
- After onboarding, open Home directly; no forced connection (Q80).
- Zerodha authorization lasts about 24 hours per the project decision; daily re-authentication. Session expiry is
  not identity deletion. Same Client ID reconnecting restores the association; no new trial (§59, Q95).
- **The platform never asks for the user's Zerodha password, PIN or OTP.** Zerodha authentication is separate from
  platform login (Chat1/Chat2 continuation prompt, "important product principles").
- Drafts without Zerodha: allowed; live-data functions locked (Q185, Q186).
- Connecting never auto-activates a strategy (Q187).
- Live data arrives → drafts recalculated automatically; never silently redesigned (Q188).
- Disconnect (Q94, §82): strategy, history, entitlement and account stay; live positions/orders/margin/execution
  unavailable until reconnect.
