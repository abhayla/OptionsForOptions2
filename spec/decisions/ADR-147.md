title: 'A short is uncovered when, for its option type, the strategy sells more units than it buys'
decision: '(1) For ADR-143''s rule, a strategy has an uncovered short when, for any instrument type (CE, PE or FUT), the
  total units sold exceed the total units bought. (2) Expiry is not compared: a buy of the same type counts as cover
  whatever its expiry. (3) When the worst-case loss is unknown and a short is uncovered, the tier is EXCEPTIONAL and needs
  "I accept this risk" (ADR-140); otherwise ELEVATED.'

# ADR-147: Uncovered short counts units per type

Why: the first rule counted any buy of the same type as cover, so a small hedge on a large short skipped the acceptance
step. The owner chose to compare units per type and left expiry out.
