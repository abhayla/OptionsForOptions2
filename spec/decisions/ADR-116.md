title: When Zerodha shows a contract shared by several strategies flat, one recorded action marks that contract's legs exited in every holder; any non-flat state stays blocked
decision: '(1) When a contract held by two or more strategies disagrees with Zerodha and Zerodha shows that contract
  flat, the user confirms ONE recorded "Kite flat, mark exited for every holder" action, and it lifts all holders
  together. (2) Any non-flat state stays blocked for every holder, exactly as REQ-060 Q224 says. (3) The action marks
  only that contract''s legs as exited in each holder, never a whole strategy: an Iron Condor''s other legs stay
  monitored. (4) "Flat" means all three: net quantity 0 summed across NRML and MIS and across the day and net rows; no
  open order on that contract at Zerodha; a fresh read at the moment of confirming, taken under the per-user lock of
  issue #423. (5) A contract that has expired follows ADR-083 instead. (6) The exit price of each leg is taken from
  tagged trades where they exist; otherwise it reads "exit price unknown (outside trade)". (7) The user confirms the
  whole action as one audited event.'

# ADR-116: shared contract flat on Zerodha lifts all holders in one recorded action

Why: issue #380 (reconciliation on the live Kite-basket route) needed the answer to OQ-GC2h-2, "what lifts the block
when two strategies share one contract". The spec had only the refusal (REQ-060 Q224). The owner chose the flat-only
lift on 2026-10-19; the expert review narrowed the mechanism so that a whole strategy is never marked exited because
one of its legs is flat.
