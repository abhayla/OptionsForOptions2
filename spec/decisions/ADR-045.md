# ADR-045: Overnight delegation

## Decision
- **Build:** requirements whose decisions are all settled and need no live Zerodha data, credentials, payment or
  legal answer. They are marked **Approved** on this date citing this ADR: REQ-017, REQ-018, REQ-021, REQ-028,
  REQ-032, REQ-033, REQ-034, REQ-035, REQ-053. Added later the same night (pure domain logic, all decisions settled): REQ-019, REQ-020,
  REQ-038, REQ-041, REQ-059, REQ-064, REQ-070. Third set (02:40 IST, pure domain logic, all decisions settled): REQ-040, REQ-049, REQ-056,
  REQ-057, REQ-058, REQ-060, REQ-065.
- **Decide by recommendation:** each open question's recorded recommendation becomes the decision, labelled
  "delegated overnight (ADR-045)", so the owner can reverse it in the morning.
- **Complex questions:** one or two independent reviewers (fresh context) review first; the decision is joint and
  records their findings.
- **Never overnight:** owner-only credentials (Kite API key), spend, production deploy, anything depending on
  Zerodha's written answer (ADR-034), the legal review (Q211).
- **Morning summary:** `docs/owner-review-2026-09-30.md` lists every delegated decision, what was built, and what is
  parked.
