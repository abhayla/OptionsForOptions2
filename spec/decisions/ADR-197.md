# ADR-197: The builder after hours

## Why
- In the evening the builder showed no price at all ("Draft - Live data not connected"), so nobody could plan a trade
  for the next day without typing every price (walkthrough I-5, 26 Oct 20:00 IST).
- The owner chose Sensibull's behaviour; the market-closed banner and the close label keep ADR-015's promise that old
  prices are never passed off as live.
