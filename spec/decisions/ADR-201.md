# ADR-201: Paper strategies on the Positions screen; functional test round

## Why
- The owner wants to place strategies from the builder and follow them on the Positions screen, with no real orders
  (ADR-165 (1) keeps orders out of this scope).
- A separate tab keeps a paper P&L from being added to real money (example: a paper condor at +4,200 next to a real
  long call at -800 must not read as one total of +3,400).
- The owner chose to fix only what does not work in this round; looks are compared and listed.
