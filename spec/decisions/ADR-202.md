# ADR-202: Required meaning, Sensibull's form

## Why
- On 2026-10-28 our builder at 1536 x 864 showed the summary sentences, an amber banner, a red margin paragraph and an
  "Important info" box above the payoff graph; Sensibull's builder shows the graph on the first screen (study 011).
- REQ-136 already describes Sensibull's pattern (a definition behind every figure, one note); this decision applies it to
  every sentence that today sits under a figure.
- Money-safety and honesty texts (funds warning, "estimated from spot") stay visible: ADR-152 keeps Sensibull from
  overriding safety and money.
