title: 'T-11b, T-11c and T-20b read India VIX and daily index candles from Kite; the app stores its own daily ATM IV from day one'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-08 ~18:00 IST (GC-ICTRACK, answer A2). (1) T-11b reads the live India VIX quote (Kite instrument token
  264969) through the one index-quote reader; a 0 or empty value is "missing", never zero. (2) T-11c uses the India VIX
  percentile over the last 250 trading days as a stand-in for an IV percentile, for NIFTY only; SENSEX answers "not
  available" with the reason (no volatility index for SENSEX on Kite). (3) T-20b is ADX(14) on Kite daily candles of
  NIFTY or SENSEX: 25 or more = trending, 20 or less = range-bound. (4) The app writes its own ATM IV once per
  underlying per trading day from now on, so a true IV percentile can replace the stand-in later. (5) T-11c and T-20b
  lose their greyed "not available" flag in the playbook. (6) These are trigger inputs, not a history screen: ADR-203's
  hold on IV-percentile history screens is unchanged.
status: accepted
