# ADR-076: TOTAL Current Value is net, with Cr/Dr

## Why
- A user reads the TOTAL row to compare what they took in with what it costs to close now; a net figure on both
  sides makes the row add up to the unrealized P&L, while a plain sum next to a net entry cannot be compared.

## Alternatives not taken
- Keep the plain sum of the legs; show "—" for TOTAL Current Value.
