# ADR-027: Home, navigation and the public site

## Owner answers
- **Q7 = B** (T1 #15): *"What would you like to do?"* home — Create a Strategy · My Strategies · Alerts · Market ·
  Learn, with a small market strip — not a Bloomberg-style terminal.
- **Q81 = C** (T2 #3): **hybrid dashboard** — no strategies: *Create Strategy + Explore Option Chain + Learn*;
  strategies but none active: *My Strategies + Create Strategy + recent results*; active strategies: *Active
  Strategies + Important Alerts + what needs attention*. Also for new users (T1 #234): *Welcome, [Name]*, *Plan your
  trade. Follow your strategy. Then execute.*, 7-day trial status, *I already know my strategy* shortcut, a quiet
  *Already trade with Zerodha? Connect when ready* line. No forced Zerodha connection.
- **Q78** (T1 #227): Home · Strategies · Positions · Market · Orders · Alerts · Learn, and avatar → Account &
  Settings (Profile, Zerodha connection, Subscription & Billing, Free Eligibility, Referrals, Notifications, Security,
  Preferences, Strategy Preferences). Positions and Orders are first-class; Orders never offers order entry.
- **Q58 = A** (T1 #181): all real-time/detailed data behind login. **Q59 = A** (T1 #183): public site = product
  explanation, sample/static screenshots, clearly labelled sample/historical payoffs, pricing, FAQ, sign up/login.
