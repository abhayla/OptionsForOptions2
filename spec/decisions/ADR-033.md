# ADR-033: Delegated ChatGPT answers are binding; the spec must carry every answer's substance

## Decision
- **Delegated answers.** From T2 #83 ("for each question, you yourself give an answer … from user perspective") and
  #99, ChatGPT answered questions itself while the owner watched. Those answers are binding decisions. Two exceptions
  are raised to the owner, one question at a time: (1) a delegated answer that contradicts an owner answer; (2) a
  delegated answer that touches money (prices, entitlements, payments), legal/compliance, or what Zerodha allows.
- **What "recorded" means.** Every Q1–Q203 answer, and every requirement-bearing statement in the other imported
  files (`docs/reference/chatgpt/`), is written into `spec/` text with its details. A register row or a bare
  "see Q33C" citation is not enough.
- **How it is checked.** An independent reviewer (fresh context, not the spec's author) reads each question and answer
  in the transcripts and compares it with the spec text. Gaps are fixed in the spec; contradictions go to the owner.
