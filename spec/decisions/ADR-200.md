# ADR-200: Building ADR-197 now, and the proof rule for GC-BM

## Why
- After 15:30 on 2026-10-27 the real app showed no numbers at all, while ADR-197 (decided that morning), REQ-105 AC-4
  and REQ-114 AC-7 say the last close is shown, labelled. The owner asked for it to work now.
- The GC-BM contract required a walk "in market hours" because after hours there were no prices. With ADR-197 built,
  the real app has prices after hours, so the owner accepts a closing-price walk on the real app as the proof.
- The first version of this row restated ADR-197 from memory and contradicted it on the entry label; it was corrected
  the same night (spec-first R1: the decisions were not searched for "last close" before writing).
