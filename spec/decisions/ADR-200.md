# ADR-200: The builder outside market hours

## Why
- After 15:30 the real app showed no numbers at all, while REQ-105 AC-4 and REQ-114 AC-7 say the last close is shown,
  labelled. The owner wants planning to work after hours on the last closing prices.
- The GC-BM contract required a walk "in market hours" because after hours there were no prices. With (1) the real
  app has prices after hours, so the owner accepts a closing-price walk on the real app as the proof.
