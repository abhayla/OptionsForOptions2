title: 'A dismiss silences the same level; one stage worse still opens a new suggestion at once'
decision: >-
  (1) After the user dismisses an adjustment suggestion, the same rule on the same strategy stays quiet at that stage
  until the value clears the line by the re-arm margin and the cool-down has passed (ADR-217 and REQ-170 AC-2 together).
  The cool-down counts from the record, not from the dismissal; a dismiss never reopens on a timer alone.
  (2) If, before that, the value reaches the next stage's threshold of the same rule (REQ-170 AC-3), a new suggestion
  opens at once, ignoring the dismiss and the cool-down, and says "Worse than the one you dismissed". (3) The daily cap
  and the quiet time (REQ-170 AC-4) still apply to that new suggestion; a held one shows "held: daily limit" /
  "held: quiet time" in the timeline. (4) A further dismiss of the worse suggestion silences that new stage the same way.
