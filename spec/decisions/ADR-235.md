title: 'The background check speed follows the strategy''s trading style: intraday 10 s, weekly 30 s, monthly 2 min'
decision: >-
  (1) The background playbook check speed (REQ-166 AC-1 "background checks") is three admin settings, one per trading
  style, with defaults intraday 10 s, weekly 30 s, monthly 120 s per strategy. (2) A strategy's style is taken from the
  strategy as it nears expiry (REQ-170 Q-AD-2): 0-1 day to expiry = intraday, a weekly expiry = weekly, a monthly expiry
  = monthly; a user-pinned style applies to the alert rules of REQ-170 and to this speed alike. (3) A strategy is still
  checked only when a price it depends on changed (REQ-165 AC-2); the worker keeps waking about once a second
  (ADR-233 (6)). (4) The promise of REQ-165 AC-1 becomes "recorded within the style's check interval of the crossing
  price" (about 10 s intraday, 30 s weekly, 2 min monthly). (5) What the trader is SHOWN is governed by REQ-170
  (confirm, cool-down, re-arm, cap, quiet time), not by this speed.
