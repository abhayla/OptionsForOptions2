# ADR-002: Strategy-only execution, controlled modification and Strategy Guard

## Owner answers
- **Q29 = A** (T1 #71): strategy-only; no standalone "Buy NIFTY CE" button. Lifecycle: Create → Configure → Validate →
  Execute → Monitor → Adjust → Exit.
- Owner (T1 #73): strategy-only is a **headline selling point** — users can't place unplanned orders, which builds
  confidence. Wording (ChatGPT, owner confirmed T1 #75): *Every trade follows a strategy. No impulsive standalone
  orders.* / *Plan your trade. Follow your strategy. Then execute.* (canonical wording for all UI copy; SPEC CHANGE copy-only,
  delegated overnight ADR-045, audit S-MASTER-§1) Never promise it will "always reduce losses".
- **Q30 = A** (T1 #75): users may modify a live strategy through **Modify Strategy**: proposed change → recalculate max
  profit/loss, breakevens, current P&L, payoff, margin, charges, risk status → Before/After → user confirms → orders
  prepared. *You can change your strategy, but you cannot bypass the strategy.*
- **Strategy Guard** (T1 #74, confirmed #75): before execution or modification the platform checks whether the action
  fits the strategy; if not: *"This action changes your strategy's risk profile"* and shows the consequences first.

## ChatGPT (delegated)
- **Q157** (T2 #90): after execution the Strategy Plan stays editable through Modify Strategy; the historical record
  of what was executed is never silently changed.
