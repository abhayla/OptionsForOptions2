# ADR-196: Sensibull's builder and chain features, our rules on top

## Why
- The owner's goal for the builder is "fully functional, similar UI as Sensibull", with the option chain as part of it.
- ADR-193 (1) had kept Sensibull-only features out until the owner decided them one by one; the owner decided them
  together for the builder and the chain, and asked that our own requirements be "additional", not replaced.
- Stock options were offered and declined, so ADR-157 stands.
