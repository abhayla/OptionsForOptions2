  the whole trade closed at the planned prices is a loss AFTER charges, as well as when it is a loss before charges.
  A trader is never shown "Take profit" beside an after-charges loss. Exit (A-31) stays offered. This settles the
  clash of ADR-238 (6) with ADR-294 (4) in favour of ADR-238 (6); ADR-294 (4)'s before-charges test stays as the
  first test, and the offer screen keeps both figures.
  (2) Q3, D-40: ADR-324 points (1) to (3) are accepted as built (header figures over the strategies that can be
  priced; expired ones left out with the count line; the Live card's expiry sentence).
  (3) Q4, D-38: ADR-324 point (4) is accepted as built (timeout words; a failed pick keeps the typed fields).
  (4) Q5, D-57: 24-hour IST time everywhere, the disconnect sentence included: ADR-015 Q182 now reads "Last updated:
  10:42:17 IST" (was "10:42:17 AM"), and REQ-049 AC-5 follows it. No screen shows AM or PM.
  (5) Q6, D-67: an adjustment pop-up for a rule that was already true when the trade was entered is not raised in
  the first 5 minutes after entry (the 5-minute figure matches the REQ-170 alert cool-down). After 5 minutes, if the
  rule is still true, it is raised as before. A rule that becomes true after entry is raised at once. The rule itself
  and its record are unchanged; only the pop-up waits.
  Refined by the owner the same day (question tool, Part F): "already true at entry" means first found true within 5

  would not have quieted C10 166 / C11 167 of 23 Nov); compute the entry baseline for Greek rules (larger, later).
  (6) Q7, D-68: ADR-325 point (2) is accepted as built: "Today" is the whole result after charges of the real trades
  opened or closed on the current trading day.
  (7) Q8, D-47: ADR-325 point (4) is accepted as built: the lots box appears after the first click on a strike.
