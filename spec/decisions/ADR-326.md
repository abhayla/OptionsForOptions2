id: ADR-326
date: '2026-11-25'
title: 'In market hours a paper trigger whose level cannot be checked against a live price is refused, not saved'
decision: >-
  OWNER DECIDED 25 Nov 2026 ~16:10 IST (question tool, after the live screen test of 25 Nov). SPEC CHANGE to
  ADR-317 (2) for market hours only. (1) Saving a paper trigger (a conditional exit on the index, or a leg stop) first
  reads the live price it would be compared with, asking the feed for that price (index for a conditional exit, the
  leg's own contract for a leg stop) and waiting up to 3 seconds for it. (2) Market open and still no live price after
  that wait (no feed, a stale or failed read): the save is refused and nothing is stored, with the line
  "Not saved: the live price could not be read, so this level cannot be checked. Try again in a moment." (3) Market
  closed: unchanged from ADR-317 (2), the trigger is saved and arms at the next live tick. (4) A level the live price has
  already crossed is still refused as ADR-317 (1) says.
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Supersedes ADR-317 (2) in market hours; ADR-317 (2) still holds when the market is closed.'
provenance:
- docs/process/screen-checks-2026-11-25.md (D-01, 15:02 and 15:11 rows)
- spec/decisions/ADR-317.md
---

Why: on 25 Nov 2026 at 15:02 IST "Above 25,700" was saved on paper strategy 169 while NIFTY was 25,772 and the trade
closed 30 seconds later. Root cause (reproduced on recorded frames): the save check read NIFTY without asking the feed
for it, so in bus mode the API held no NIFTY price, the check saw "no price", and ADR-317 (2) saved the level. The same
missing read made leg-stop alerts say "the index value at that moment was not available" (S-20). Saving an unchecked
level in market hours is the hazard ADR-317 already rejected ("a mistyped digit closed the trade").

Options not chosen: keep ADR-317 (2) (save and arm; fires the moment prices return if already crossed); save but hold
unarmed until a live price confirms it (more code and a new state on screen).
