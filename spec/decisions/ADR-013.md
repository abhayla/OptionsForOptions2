# ADR-013: Historical data, adjustment data and simulation

## Owner answers
- **Q19 = B** (T1 #39): simple historical simulation (e.g. last 30 days: best/worst outcome, winning days, max
  drawdown; "every Thursday for 6 months"), clearly labelled as simulation, not proof.
- **Q99 = C** (T2 #7): prefer the live vendor's history; plug in a separate historical provider if needed.
- **Q100 = C**: EOD default, intraday later. **Q101 = C**: flexible data layer, daily close required for V1.
- Owner (T2 #11): **simulation is lower priority**; other features first.
- Owner (T2 #93): the adjustment engine must have **all data an adjustment could need** — e.g. the Greeks and other
  values used in a YouTube Iron Condor adjustment video the owner shared (transcript still to be provided, T2 #95).
- **Q180** (owner decision T2 #121): **no historical data for now** — the interim source (Zerodha) charges for it;
  historical candles, simulation and historical adjustment analysis stay off until a licensed source exists.

## ChatGPT (delegated)
- Q161 keep time series of adjustment metrics; Q162 leg + strategy data; Q163 underlying data alone can trigger;
  Q164 new metrics addable without redesign; Q165 collect even without user rules.
- **Q168**: no tick-by-tick history for every option. Tiers: real-time · aggregated intraday (1/5-minute) · daily ·
  strategy snapshots. **Q169**: build history from the live feed where practical and licensed.
- Data catalogue (T2 #94): market (spot, change, high/low, futures, volume, volatility measures); option contract
  (LTP, bid/ask, volume, OI, OI change, IV, Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho, strike, expiry, DTE); strategy (net
  premium, P&L, P&L %, max P/L, breakevens, distances, net Greeks overall and per expiry, margin, utilisation);
  time/history (previous values, rates of change, snapshots, trajectories).
