  to the builder's legs, and the Replace question is for a replacing draft link that arrives inside the app. A

  once the live prices are showing." Its "pending owner read" mark is cleared.

# ADR-328: owner decisions for modular-doors phases 3 to 8

Why: each point decides what a trader is shown, asked or allowed to do, so each is the owner's. The technical choices
of the plan (module names, lock order, which duplicates are merged, skipped or parked) are the session's and are
recorded in the plan folder, not here.
