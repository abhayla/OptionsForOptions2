# ADR-149: The owner's-own-account rule, as controls the product can enforce

Why: the Kite basket form carries no account, so "placed only on the owner's own account" cannot be enforced as
written. The owner chose to keep placing real orders with controls the product can check, rather than stop real orders
until an account pin exists (whether Kite's basket accepts one is unverified).
