title: 'A roll into a later expiry closes the strategy and opens a new, linked one'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~16:1x IST (question tool, during the live iron condor test): "Consider rollover as a new strategy
  and closure of current strategy and opening of new strategy." SPEC CHANGE to ADR-207 (2) for rolls that change the
  expiry ("a roll as an exit plus an add" inside the same paper order book). (1) An approach or a manual change that
  moves legs into a LATER expiry (A-04 diagonal roll, A-05 roll the tested spread out, a manual roll-out) is shown and
  confirmed as two steps in one confirm list: close the current strategy (its closing orders; it then counts as
  closed per ADR-259, an Exit all per ADR-255, not an adjustment) and open a new strategy with the new legs (its
  opening orders, its own plan from the type's playbook, adjustments used 0). (2) Decided under the owner's
  delegation: the new strategy records "rolled from <old name>" and the old one "rolled into <new name>"; both
  histories link to each other, and the whole-trade totals of each stay separate. (3) Rolls that keep the expiry
  (A-01, A-02, A-06, a strike move) stay adjustments of the same strategy (ADR-207 (2) unchanged for them).
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'SPEC CHANGE: ADR-207 (2) no longer covers expiry-changing rolls; adds the rolled-from / rolled-into link.'
