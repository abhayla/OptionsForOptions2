title: 'A strategy with no open leg moves to Completed Strategies with its final totals'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:1x IST (question tool, "Yes, with final totals"). A strategy (paper or real) whose every leg is
  closed (exited, stopped, conditionally exited or settled at expiry) is completed: it leaves My Strategies' open list
  and Live Strategies and is listed on Strategies > Completed Strategies, newest first, one row each with: name,
  underlying, opened and closed dates (IST), final P&L, charges, net P&L after charges, adjustments used (ADR-255) and a
  "Paper" or "Real" label. Opening a row shows the strategy's history and whole-trade totals (REQ-163). Why: live test
  2026-11-06: fully exited paper strategies 86 and 87 never appeared there (the page's completed-status list was empty,
  closed strategies stayed "draft").
status: accepted
