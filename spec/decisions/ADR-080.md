# ADR-080: Basket order mechanics (product, tick rounding, executable limit)

## Why
- Strategies are monitored across days (REQ-041..043); an MIS position is squared off by Zerodha the same afternoon,
  ending a strategy without any platform rule firing. One fixed product removes a beginner trap.
- REQ-059 AC-4 forbids silently changing the user's strategy; rounding toward the user's own limit keeps every sent
  price at or better than what they accepted, at the cost of at most half a tick of fill chance.
- With LIMIT-only baskets (REQ-073 AC-1), a limit at the LTP often sits unfilled (ADR-079's real case); the depth price
  is what a market order would pay, and the user sees that cost before placing.

## Alternatives not taken
- Q264: MIS selectable at the Advanced level, or a product choice on every strategy (a question beginners cannot answer).
- Q265: round toward the touch (can be worse than the user's limit), or half-even (direction depends on the number).
- Q268: LIMIT at LTP or mid (often unfilled); touch plus N ticks (always pays the buffer; N unmeasured).
