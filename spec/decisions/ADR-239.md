title: 'Iron condor roll approaches A-02, A-04, A-06: which legs move, how far, and refusals'
decision: >-
  Technical definitions for GC-IC1 step 3 (owner scope 2026-11-03 "Full playbook"), decided by the orchestrator under
  the owner's standing rule that technical calls are not asked. They are kept distinct from the existing A-05 (roll the
  tested spread, short and wing together, out to the playbook's Early delta, width kept, REQ-155 / GC-ADJ1).
  (1) A-02 "Roll the tested side away" moves only the tested SHORT outward by the same distance rule as A-05 (the
  first strike beyond it at or under the target delta; with no delta in the chain, the points fallback); its wing stays.
  The spread therefore narrows. Refused when the new short would land at or beyond its wing (it would no longer be
  protected). Example: short 23450 CE, wing 23650 CE, target strike 23550 -> -23550 CE / +23650 CE (width 100).
  (2) A-04 "Roll up/down and out" closes the tested spread and opens the same spread in the NEXT listed expiry of the
  underlying: short at the target delta of that expiry's chain (same distance rule, read on the next expiry), wing at
  the old width. Refused, with the reason, when the next expiry has no chain rows with prices. The result spans two
  expiries (day rules on such a position: issue #640).
  (3) A-06 "Shift a leg by N strikes" shifts the tested spread (short and wing) N listed strikes away from spot through
  the REQ-078 strike-step rule, width kept; N = the user preference adjustments.shift_strikes (default 1, the
  catalogue's one-click step). Moving a single leg stays the leg editor's job (REQ-078 arrows), so the Compare column
  keeps the width. Refused when a target strike is not listed.
  All three pass the existing roll debit guard (ADR-230 roll_debit_limit) and are priced by the one engine as a full
  After (ADR-008), with REQ-155 AC-8 refusals and warnings.
status: accepted
