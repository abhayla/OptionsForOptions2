title: 'Iron condor situations: which act as rules, which are labelled guidance'
decision: >-
  REQ-159 AC-5 keeps S-01 to S-12 in the playbook. GC-IC1 step 6 sorts each into a rule the app checks or guidance
  the user checks.
  (1) Owner 2026-11-03 (question tool, "Label as guidance"): S-03 "Range day", S-04 "IV jumps without a big move" and
  S-05 "IV crush after an event" are shown in the playbook editor and summary with a "Guidance" label (the user checks
  them; the app does not watch for them), because no range-day definition and no event-day list exist yet. They can
  become rules once an event list exists. Example: S-05 reads as guidance, "after an event, consider take profit at
  40% of max profit".
  (2) Rules the app already checks: S-01 (the gap-at-open window, gap_until), S-06 and S-07 (day rules, GC-IC1 step
  1), S-10 (T-21: no suggestion on stale or missing prices), S-11 (only exit and reduce at the adjustment limit).
  (3) Technical (orchestrator, standing rule): S-02 "Trending day" becomes a rule: the position's net delta per lot
  (engine Greeks summed over the legs, divided by the lots of the largest leg) at or beyond +/-0.15 opens one record
  through the alert gate offering the playbook's roll approaches. S-12 "Big win early" becomes a rule: on the session
  the strategy was entered, profit at or above 40% of max profit opens one record offering A-24 and A-25.
