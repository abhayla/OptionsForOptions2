# ADR-185: Unhedged-short warning per strategy and direction, as in Sensibull

## Why
- REQ-085 AC-2 and ADR-143 (2) disagreed on what covers a sold option; the owner asked what Sensibull does and chose the
  Sensibull-like reading: one strategy-level warning per direction of unlimited loss.
- One rule for the Builder and Execute means a user never sees a strategy called covered on one screen and uncovered on
  the other.
