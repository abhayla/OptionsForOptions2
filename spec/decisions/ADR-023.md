# ADR-023: Trial, Limited/Read-Only mode and the entitlement engine

## Owner answers
- Owner (T1 #183): Gmail login for everyone; 7 days free; then paid unless a qualifying customer or referrer.
- **Q88 = A**: trial starts at successful registration. **Q67 = A**: after trial → Limited/Read-Only (log in, view
  strategies and history, settings, eligibility, refer, subscribe, connect Zerodha).
- **Q68 = A**: a Pro action shows a contextual message — *"Your Pro trial has ended … Subscribe to Pro … Want Pro free?
  … Check Eligibility"* — no pop-ups.
- **Q89 = A**: active strategies stay monitored after expiry (live risk, exit/risk alerts) — commercial status never
  removes risk visibility. **Q90 = A**: paid expiry → same Limited model, no grace period in V1.
- **Q91 = A**: no live Option Chain when Limited (active-strategy monitoring is the exception). **Q92 = A**: the chain
  screen shows a Pro gate and may show a preview labelled **Sample / Not Live**. **Q93 = A**: past strategies,
  simulations, P&L, reports, execution history stay viewable forever; new simulations need Pro.

## Entitlement engine (T1 #190, #196, #272)
No `free_user` flag. Sources: Trial Pro · Direct Zerodha Customer Pro · Referral-earned Pro · Paid Monthly Pro ·
Paid Annual Pro · Expired/Limited. Each: type, source, start, end, status, reference, reason, audit history. Several
can co-exist; events are kept, not overwritten. Design is *Identity → Entitlement → Capability*.
Entitlement priority (T1 #272 §21): where several entitlements exist, the system evaluates them by defined rules
rather than overwriting one with another. Example: *Direct Qualifying Zerodha Pro — indefinite* plus *Referral reward*
→ the referral event is still recorded in history even though the direct entitlement already gives Pro. The exact
evaluation rules beyond this example are not stated in the transcripts. **Evaluation rules** (delegated overnight, joint decision of two independent reviewers (ADR-045), audit
X-T1#272; owner approved §21's principle at T1 #271; rules 2-3 touch money and are listed for the owner's
confirmation): (1) Pro while at least one Pro entitlement is in force, else Limited. (2) Time-limited periods
(trial, paid, referral days) never overlap and are never lost: a new grant starts at the current Pro end; a paid plan
bought during the trial starts when the trial ends (trial ends 5 Oct, subscribe 2 Oct → a 30-day paid period runs 5 Oct – 4 Nov, end excluded, so 3 Nov is the
last full day of Pro; the paid period length itself is an owner question). (3)
While an open-ended entitlement is in force (Direct Qualifying Zerodha Customer), referral days are recorded and
banked, and start when it ends; the billing screen shows "30 referral days saved". The same applies to any
time-limited period still running when an open-ended entitlement starts: its remaining days pause and resume later. (4) Every grant is an event; the
screen names the source currently giving Pro. (5) Periods are laid end to end when access is evaluated, in grant
order, not fixed at grant time: revoking or ending one early (refund, fraud, ADR-039) pulls the later periods forward,
so no earned day is lost and no Limited gap opens between them. Example: paid Pro to 10 Oct + referral granted 20 Sep
(then 10 Oct – 9 Nov); paid revoked 25 Sep → referral runs 25 Sep – 25 Oct.

## Clarification (spec-conformant, recorded 2026-09-29, audit item C-17)
- "New strategies need Pro" includes drafts: a Limited user may open the Strategy Builder with **Sample · Not Live**
  prices (Q92) to explore, but cannot **save** a new strategy; Save shows the Pro upgrade prompt. Existing strategies
  stay viewable (REQ-018 AC-3) and active ones can be exited (ADR-037).

## Owner decision (2026-09-29 morning, Q225): new events vs stored history
- **Q225 = A** (owner, this session, issue #12): a NEW entitlement event is checked against the current settings (caps,
  clock skew, no backdating, no post-dating). STORED history is loaded with integrity checks only (order, ids,
  references) and is never re-judged by today's settings: lowering the free-day cap from 90 to 30 does not break a
  history of 7 + 30 + 30 days that was legal when written. No status change (revoke, early end) may be dated more
  than the allowed clock skew after the time it is recorded, so a revoke "effective 2106" is refused and a real fraud
  revoke later is never blocked by it.
