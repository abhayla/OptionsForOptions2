# ADR-114: screen content and layout by a design role; ACs stay the business rules

On 2026-10-19 the owner delegated the content and layout of every screen to a design role that studies similar Indian
broker and options apps (Sensibull, Opstra, Zerodha Kite, Dhan, Upstox, Streak, AlgoTest, Angel One), inside the
business rules the requirements already state. Look and feel comes from the owner's global design preferences file, by
path. A clash between the design role's recommendation and a business rule is never settled by the role: the rule wins
for the build and the owner is asked.
