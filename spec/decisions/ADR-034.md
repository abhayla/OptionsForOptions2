# ADR-034: Ask Zerodha in writing before building anything on Zerodha live market data

## Decision
- **Q210 = A (owner, 2026-09-29).** Email first; build nothing that depends on Zerodha live data until Zerodha answers
  in writing.
- **Why.** Zerodha's own pages (checked 2026-09-28, quoted in `spec/open-questions.md` Q210): the free Personal plan
  "excludes historical or real-time data"; live data is the Connect plan at ₹500 per app per month; and "you cannot
  display data from Kite Connect APIs on other platforms, as this violates the exchange's data vending policies".
  Zerodha also says mass-retail platforms can get Kite Connect "free of cost", without saying whether that includes
  live data or display rights. The T2 #121 plan ("Zerodha's live market data, which is free") rests on that answer.
- **Contact.** The Kite Connect startups page (kite.trade/startups) directs free-access requests to
  `talk@rainmatter.com` (address decoded from the page source on 2026-09-29).
- **Allowed meanwhile.** Calculation engine and scenario table with test fixtures; strategy builder showing only
  **Sample · Not Live** prices (Q92); account, registration and Zerodha login/identity flows (no market data).
- **Blocked until the answer.** Live option chain, live P&L, monitoring and alerts driven by Zerodha data, and any
  "set up your own Zerodha API" guide (Q178).
- **Depends on this:** Q204 (shared vs per-user feed), Q205 (monitoring while the session is expired), Q206 (prices
  for drafts and trials).
