# ADR-084: Grouping existing positions - per underlying and expiry, Zerodha's average as the entry

## Why
- One expiry per group matches what beginners hold and keeps unrelated positions apart.
- Zerodha's average price is the real cost of the position; the user can correct it because a position may have been
  built in several fills or across days.

## Alternatives not taken
- One group per underlying across expiries: lumps unrelated positions.
- No suggestion: departs from REQ-061 AC-1.
