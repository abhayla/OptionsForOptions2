# ADR-064: Closed names for risk limits and preferences

## Why
- REQ-038 AC-1 keeps a definition (rules, risk limits, preferences) separate from live state; AC-5 says live prices
  are never saved inside the strategy.
- A forbidden-names list accepted `{"preferences": {"Spot": "22950.35"}}` (measured on PR 134 at 9ab20d5); any list of
  bad spellings stays incomplete. A closed list of good names cannot miss one.

## Where the names come from
REQ-025 AC-1: "Discovery may use objective, market view, risk preference, capital, expected range and selected
constraints." The three risk-limit names are the limits a beginner sets on a strategy (maximum loss, capital and
margin); none is a market price.
