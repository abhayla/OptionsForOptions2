# ADR-117: two baskets when a sell depends on a protective buy

Why: REQ-056 AC-4 says a sell that depends on a failed protective leg is not submitted; REQ-073 AC-1 says the user
places the whole basket on Kite and the platform sends nothing, so Kite would place the sell regardless. The owner
resolved the conflict on 2026-10-19 by splitting entry into two baskets with the Check with Zerodha step between them.
