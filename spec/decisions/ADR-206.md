# ADR-206: order flow confirmed; Funds needed held

## Why
- The owner restated the order flow in chat; it is the flow ADR-165 and ADR-201 already decide, and Sensibull keeps
  its virtual trades (Draft Portfolios, study 090-092) apart from real positions (050-053) the same way.
- "Funds needed" is the money needed to place orders at the broker; with no broker order, it has no use now, and
  Zerodha's reply cannot give it anyway (F-62).
