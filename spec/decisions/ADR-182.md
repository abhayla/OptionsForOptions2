# ADR-182: Price protection measured from the market price

## Why
- Sensibull measures its market protection from the last traded price; a typed entry price only changes the payoff.
- It keeps REQ-077 AC-8 word for word: the basket never uses a typed price as its limit.
- ADR-095 read literally ("planned premium") would let a typed planning number set a real order's limit once
  protection is wired; this row fixes the base to the market price at Execute.
