title: 'Planned entries are captured at the bid-ask mid (LTP only without a quote); an impossible credit is flagged'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:4x IST (question tool, "Mid, LTP only if no quote"). SPEC CHANGE to REQ-077 AC-5 (was: capture
  from the LTP, the mid only when there is no LTP). (1) A leg's captured planned entry (on adding a leg, a ready-made
  shape, Reset Prices, and the Add to Paper prefill) is the mid of the current bid and ask; the LTP is used only when
  there is no two-sided quote, and the leg says which basis it used. (2) The basis is a setting per ADR-264: choices
  "mid (LTP if no quote)" / "LTP (mid if no LTP)"; admin default mid; users may change it. (3) When a defined-risk
  shape's net credit is more than its widest wing (or a debit is more than the spread's width), the builder shows
  "These prices are inconsistent (credit more than the wing width): check the quotes before relying on max profit /
  max loss" and does not present the trade as risk-free. Why: live test 2026-11-06 14:30: NIFTY 29 Dec condor 24650 /
  24750 PE, 24950 / 25050 CE captured LTPs gave credit 104.60 on 100-point wings (max loss = charges only, Rs -516);
  the chain two minutes later gave 84.85; the 24750 PE last trade 413.55 was stale. SENSEX legs on LTP swung about
  Rs 1,000 each while the net moved Rs 69 (issue 45).
