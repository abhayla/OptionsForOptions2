# ADR-072: "Where do I start losing?" from the loss regions

## Why
- Breakevens are where the expiry P&L equals zero, including points where it only touches zero and the ends of flat
  zero stretches; a loss region is where the P&L is below zero. In those shapes the two stop matching: a zero-cost
  bull put spread (sell 22400PE, buy 22200PE, both at 100) was told "above 22,400" though every loss is below 22,400;
  a 1x2 put ratio has two breakevens and one loss region; a butterfly costing its width has one breakeven and two loss
  regions (W-066 verifier, 2026-10-09; reproduced through the real outcome service).
- Two rounds of rules over breakevens both failed (finding loss-region-inferred-from-breakevens); an independent
  review checked region-from-payoff against a brute-force scan on 400 random strategies: 0 mismatches.

## Alternatives not taken
- Keep a rule over breakevens and add cases (failed twice; every new shape breaks it).
- Answer with a list of breakevens only (true but does not answer "where do I lose").
