# ADR-065: Same trust boundary for pricing guards as for wording

## Why
- On 2026-10-08 every normal path of W-060 refused or labelled a stale spot (verified); the four remaining holes
  needed deliberately forged code, e.g. `getattr(model, "_bs")` to reach the raw pricing module, or
  `ModelInputs.__new__(ModelInputs)` to copy a stale model and blank its labels.
- Chasing deliberate forging with runtime detectors is the endless pattern that parked W-024; review and the CI scan
  are the right controls for code that is written on purpose.
