# ADR-205: charges lines stay rounded from exact amounts, no note

## Why
- ADR-082 already decided exact amounts to match Zerodha's order-level reply (F-35); a tidy line sum would make our
  total differ from Zerodha's by a paisa.
- The owner chose not to add a note: the difference is at most a paisa or two per strategy.
