title: 'Background playbook checks run at most once every 30 seconds per strategy by default'
decision: >-
  (1) The default of the admin setting for background playbook checks (REQ-166 AC-1, "background checks") is 30
  seconds per strategy, not 1 second. A strategy is still checked only when a price it depends on changed (REQ-165
  AC-2); with prices moving, it is checked at most once every 30 s. (2) The promise to the user is that an opportunity
  is recorded within 30 seconds of the fresh price that crosses its rule (REQ-165 AC-1). (3) The value stays an admin
  setting read at run time: a change applies across the app without a deploy (REQ-166 AC-1). (4) The other three
  speeds of REQ-166 AC-1 (per-option figures, a strategy open on a screen, a saved draft) are not changed by this
  decision. (5) The safety valve of REQ-166 AC-3 (1 s / 2 s / 5 s steps) only slows a speed set below its current step,
  so at 30 s it does not slow background checks further; it still governs the screen speeds. (6) The worker keeps
  waking about once a second and checks each strategy at most once per setting interval and only when one of its
  prices changed, so a crossing is recorded at most about 30 s after its price (not 30 s of sleep plus a cycle).
