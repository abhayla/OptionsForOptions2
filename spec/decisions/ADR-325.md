  (1) D-61: a lone long is a long call or put with no short of the same type among the legs that stay open after the
  approach (any expiry; lots are not compared). The line is decided on the server from the approach's resulting legs,
  for every approach (not only "Buy back the untested short"; A-27 can leave one too). Not chosen: same expiry only;
  only approach A-26.
  (2) D-68: "Today" on the Paper tab header is the total after charges of the real trades opened or closed on the
  current trading day (the India date of the newest market snapshot, so a Saturday shows Friday), counting the whole
  trade; scenario strategies are left out; a trade opened earlier and only partly exited today is left out. One
  paused book pauses the figure with its reason (REQ-109 AC-5). Not chosen: only the part booked today; the calendar
  date (a weekend would read "No trades opened or closed today.").
  (3) D-54: where the Test button was, the playbook page says testing on past expiries is outside V1, in the server's
  words when they load and in a fixed sentence when they do not.
  (4) D-47: the picker's count line keeps "N legs selected" and adds "N already in the draft"; Buy and Sell are always
  drawn. The lots box still appears after the first click on a strike. Not chosen: count the draft legs as selected;
  always show the lots box (doubles the row height).
  (5) D-67: pop-ups sit inside the sticky header block, so they stay on screen and do not float over page buttons; the
  adjustment rule keeps firing from entry, because the spec has no number for "the first minutes after entry"
  (question Q6). Not chosen: a fixed overlay (covers buttons); an invented quiet period. Known residue: the builder's
  sticky left column (top 72 px) can sit under a pop-up while one is shown, and a pop-up is behind an open drawer.
  (6) D-41: the Live card and the My Strategies row of a running trade show the P&L figure of the Positions page's
  paper list, with the Positions page's own paused rule; a read with no answer after 25 s says the card could not
  load, and a later answer replaces that line. "Health" is not built.
  (7) D-56: Home's alert figure is the number on the bell (unread stops plus open adjustment records), so Home and
  the bell never disagree on one screen; its label stays "Unread alerts".
  (8) D-55: Min OI keeps a row only when every side the row shows passes; volume and premium keep "either side".
  Change is the live price minus the feed's previous close (exact decimal); a value the feed does not carry reads
  "not available". A typed filter is sent 300 ms after the last key; the rows stay on screen meanwhile. Not chosen:
  either side passes Min OI (the 23 Nov behaviour).
  (9) D-58: after an adjustment the live T-01 rule line ends "(put side tested)" or "(call side tested)": the side
  whose short strike is nearer the spot (a tie is the call side, as approaches.tested_side). Stored records (inbox,
  history) carry no side and show no suffix.
  (10) D-53: the builder title stays a heading; the name is asked at Save (REQ-134 AC-1). Each name box says the
  20-character limit (ADR-183) in one line.
  (11) D-46: a Greek that would show as zero or one step shows two significant digits ("0.004", "-0.00006"); a true
  zero stays "0.00". Max profit and loss stay whole rupees, half-up (REQ-152 AC-1), now pinned by a test.
  (12) D-66: the Completed page shows when the last closing fill, stop or settlement was recorded, labelled "closed",
  or "closed: time not available". Not chosen: the last save time.
  (13) D-65: a rename reads the saved copy's revision first when the page holds none; the route still refuses a
  missing or stale revision (422 / 409). Not chosen: drop the revision check.
  (14) D-64: between 15:30 and settlement on a leg's expiry day, the builder, Positions and the tracking panel show
  "Market closed. Open legs that expire today settle at 15:45." in place of the paused or no-price line, decided by
  one clock function; a payoff refusal names the leg as the trader knows it ("NIFTY 13 Oct 22600 CE cannot be used:
  expired"), never a raw instrument id; a leg that has a price keeps it.
  (15) D-39: the "prices disconnected" message is shown only when the spot or a held leg has no live price, from the
  same per-quote health the prices use.
  (16) D-52: the empty-builder hint names the Ready-made tab only when the server lists it, else the Create Strategy
  page; it shows nothing until the features read has answered.
  (17) D-57: dates read "23 Nov 2026" and times are 24-hour IST through one formatter, guarded by scans. The two

  "24-hour IST times everywhere" (question Q5).
