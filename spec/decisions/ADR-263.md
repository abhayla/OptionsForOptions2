title: 'Every headline money figure is after charges and says so'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:3x IST (question tool, "After charges everywhere, labelled"). Max profit, max loss, profit
  left, loss left, payoff graph and payoff table values, P&L table totals, total / booked / unbooked P&L, history
  totals and Completed Strategies (ADR-259) are shown AFTER charges (charges already paid plus the estimated charges to
  close what is open, from the one engine, ADR-008) and each carries the words "(after charges)" or a column header
  saying so. The before-charges value and the charges breakdown are one tap away (the existing Charges control). A
  figure that cannot include charges (rates unreadable) says "before charges" and why, never silently. Why: live test
  2026-11-06 (strategy 86): Max Profit +5,272 (after charges) sat above Profit left +5,625.75 (before), the Payoff Table
  showed +5,570.50 (before), and the final "Total P&L -1,277.25" hid Rs 298.17 of charges (real result about -1,575).
