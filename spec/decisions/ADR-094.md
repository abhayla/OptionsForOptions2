# ADR-094: Margin check adds the BUY premium up to the limit price

## Why
- F-39 showed Kite's margin answer prices a BUY at the live price, while a LIMIT order can fill up to its limit; the
  check could pass a basket whose real premium outflow is larger than the funds, and a leg rejected for funds at
  placement leaves a partly placed strategy.
