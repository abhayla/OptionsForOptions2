title: 'Every iron condor trigger tracks real Zerodha strategies as well as paper ones, on live prices and on test data'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-08 ~18:00 IST (GC-ICTRACK plan approval, answer A1). (1) All 27 triggers of the iron condor system
  playbook are checked for real Zerodha strategies and paper strategies alike, on live prices and on test data, on the
  one code path of ADR-275: a strategy's open legs come from paper orders or from real Zerodha fills netted by the one
  netter (ofo.engine.orderbook.net_positions). (2) Tracking only: a trigger raises an alert or a suggestion; no order is
  sent by tracking. (3) The 12 triggers new to the background keep the playbook's OFF default and work when a user
  switches them on (strategy delegation, owner 2026-11-05). (4) For a real strategy, a trigger whose input the app
  cannot yet read (paid charges, adjustments used before the app saw the strategy) answers "not checked" with its
  reason, never a pass. (5) Proof order (owner 2026-11-08 ~18:3x IST, question tool, "Build on, prove later"): no real
  Zerodha position exists in the app to prove on (measured 2026-11-08 on ofo_dev: 0 stored fills, 0 adopted holdings;
  strategy 82 is a paper mirror of an IC held at another broker, soft-deleted 2026-11-06). The real-fill branch is
  built and unit-tested on Kite's reply format now; its real-data proof (an IC opened by the owner in Kite, adopted
  read-only per ADR-096, netted legs compared with Kite's positions) runs at the first market session where the owner
  holds one. Until that proof passes, the real branch is labelled unproven on real data in the handover and PR.
  (6) The core proof (owner 2026-11-09 ~02:1x IST, "we don't need a real Zerodha order or a paper trade"): every one of
  the 27 iron condor triggers reads its inputs correctly (spot, India VIX, the Greeks, per-leg and whole-trade P&L,
  margin, ADX ...) and fires when its condition is met, on test data AND on live market data; then any position's
  playbook raises its adjustment conditions and suggestions. Proof = a per-trigger record (inputs, threshold, fired /
  not checked with its reason) from the background checker in test-data mode across the scenario library and in live
  mode at a market session. The (5) real-position proof is dropped, not parked; the real-fill reader keeps its recorded
  Zerodha reply tests. Fired triggers raise alerts and suggestions only; sending adjustments is the next plan (A5).
status: accepted
