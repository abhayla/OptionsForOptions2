# ADR-067: One-minute history - live first, Kite's candles final

## Why
- F-34: bars built from our own ticks matched Kite's candles on OI every minute and on close almost every minute, but
  open, high and low only in 14-61 of 74 minutes, because full-mode ticks are about one snapshot a second. A laptop
  network drop left a missing minute and a stale close 57.94 points off.
- Kite's candles are exact and are still there the same evening, even for contracts that expired that day.
- Building live keeps intraday history available during the day and keeps a record if the evening fetch fails;
  replacing it with Kite's candles keeps the stored day exact.

## Alternatives not taken
- Live bars only: inexact open, high and low, and gaps stay gaps.
- Kite's candles only: nothing during the day, and nothing at all for a day the fetch fails.
- Raw ticks kept: REQ-051 AC-2 forbids complete tick-by-tick history.
