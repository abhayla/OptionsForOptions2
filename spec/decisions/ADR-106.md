# ADR-106: Delivery process - safeguards where they catch defects

## Why
- Coding took 7-60 minutes per item, but each safeguard ran on every small unit (item, round, run), so a feature cut
  into 8-12 items paid for review, verification and paperwork 8-12 times.
- The first wording of these cuts was unsafe in five places (independent review, 2026-10-17); the revision keeps
  every past real catch (see consequences) and is the version approved.

## Alternatives not taken
- One Tier A review per whole feature: a ~5,000-line review is read badly.
- Stop GC-2e to switch now: ~30-60 min of churn to save ~1-1.5 h, with in-flight work at risk.
