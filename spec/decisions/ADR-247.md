title: 'Default stop for the iron condor playbook: 50 % of max loss or 2x the credit received, whichever comes first; % of margin off by default'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-05 ~00:3x IST (question tool, "50% max loss or 2x credit (Recommended)"): the iron condor system
  playbook's default stop line is a loss of 50 % of max loss or 2x the credit received, whichever comes first. The
  "% of margin" stop and trigger T-16 (loss as a % of margin) stay available but are OFF by default. Basis, measured on
  strategy 82 (NIFTY 29 Dec iron condor 21000 / 21500 PE - 24000 / 24500 CE, 1 lot, margin ~Rs 45,000): 4 % of margin is
  about Rs 1,800 and was crossed on the strategy's first day (~ -Rs 7,975) while the position was ordinary; with this
  default the first stop on 82 is Rs 11,948.52 (50 % of max loss), then Rs 17,836 (2x credit). Decision support only
  (ADR-003): the playbook flags the stop, the user acts. As ADR-245, this is a default every user and every strategy
  can change.
  #627 priority (owner 2026-11-05 ~00:4x IST, question tool, "After this contract (Recommended)"): #627 (the background
  worker cannot read margin, so T-16 is never checked in the background) stays parked until the "Your adjustment
  settings" contract is done. Until then, wherever a user or a strategy turns T-16 or the "% of margin" stop on, the
  settings screen and the plan sheet say plainly: "Checked only while the app is open, not in the background yet."
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'SPEC CHANGE to ADR-216: its stop line "a loss of 50% of max loss or 4% of margin, whichever comes first" becomes "a loss of 50 % of max loss or 2x the credit received, whichever comes first"; T-16 (4 / 4 / 3 % of margin per stage) is off by default, its per-stage values kept for users who turn it on. Bears on #627 (T-16 never runs in the background): off by default lowers its urgency, not its correctness.'
