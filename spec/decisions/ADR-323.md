  (1) D-26 (REQ-112 AC-2): a closed adjustment idea shows when it closed, taken from the close's own timeline entry
  (a later "condition cleared" or "orders placed" entry does not move it): "Lapsed at 14:34:11 IST, fired 14:31:20
  IST". The Review button stays while the trade has an open record. Not chosen: hide Review once the latest check
  stops flagging (the 23 Nov defect).
  (2) D-30: the opportunity counters say what they count: "Counters count conditions, not rows. Conditions: {n}.
  Rows: {m}. One row can hold several conditions." An open record on a strategy whose last expiry is before today
  (IST) lapses with the reason "The contracts have expired" and its time; the expiry day itself stays live. Not chosen:
  count rows in the counters (changes the ADR-260 unit); lapse at 15:30 on the expiry day.
  (3) D-31 (ADR-309 (1)): while a rule has an open record, the panel and the drawer show the record's value as the
  fired value, and the poll's value beside it as "Now ... (as of ...)"; each drawer line names its rule.
  (4) D-22, D-71 (REQ-109 AC-7, AC-8; contract GC-ICLIVE-C): a fired stop stores the live index value at that moment
  in its reason ("{what}; {underlying} was at {level} at that moment", or "...; the index value at that moment was not
  available"); an expiry settlement stores "Paper - settled at expiry: {underlying} closed at {level}". Alerts >
  History lists fired stops and settlements (GET /paper/alerts/records), newest first, empty line "No stops or expiry
  settlements yet."; the closed trade's rows show each reason without a click. No migration: the texts live in the
  existing order reason and alert body. Not chosen: a new column or table for the index value; a new screen.
  (5) D-24 (REQ-109 AC-8): the tracking panel lists each index conditional exit that is armed or fired, from the
  server's level text ("Conditional exit: all legs exit if the index goes above 25,720. Armed." / "... Fired."); a
  cancelled exit is not listed; a failed read says "Could not read the conditional exits. Nothing was changed."
  (6) D-28 (owner 13 Nov; ADR-299 (1)(4)): one server function gives the word Draft / Running / Completed on every
  strategy and paper reply (`status_word`); Running means at least one open leg, so a fully exited book reads
  Completed before its row is closed; a missing or unknown word reads "Status not available". Deferred: a real basket
  that never filled still reads Running (real-order code is frozen, ADR-281).
  (7) D-33 (ADR-320 (5)): the short-funds line reads the owner's sentence wherever the builder shows a shortfall,
  including beside Execute (the margin line has no paper/real mode). Not chosen: a second, paper-only line.
  (8) D-17 (ADR-322 (2)): the time beside time value is the oldest of the spot and leg quote times; on a closed market
  it reads "at the last close prices, as of {time}" (or "at the last close prices"); with no usable time on an open
  market, "at live prices, time not given".
  (9) D-21 (ADR-320 (3)): the "What changes" block per approach shows eight rows, before against after, each marked
  up / down / unchanged / not available, plain words, no colour verdict. The before side is the reply's own Before
  (the same legs as No change). Credit up means a credit, down a debit; breakeven distance up means the nearest
  breakeven moves farther from the index. A reason line appears only when max loss after is above max loss before:
  Reduce and Close part name the side ("This removes credit from the {closed} side, so the {remaining} spread becomes
  the largest loss.") when the closed legs are all one side and the engine's at-expiry P&L shows the other side
  carries the larger loss, else "This removes credit that was held against the loss, so the maximum loss is higher
  than before."; Exit says "Closing now books today's prices as final, so the loss it locks in is higher than the
  maximum loss of the position held to expiry."; Roll, Convert and No change carry no reason line. Not chosen: a
  reason line on every roll that narrows the range (fires on Convert every time and reads as a verdict).
