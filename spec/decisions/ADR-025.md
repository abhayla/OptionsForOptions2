# ADR-025: Referral rewards and the free-eligibility loop

## Decision
- Q61: 1 successful qualifying referral = 1 month Pro free. Success = account opened through the referral +
  attributed to the user.
- Q63: stacking configurable, default **on**. Example: active until Oct 10 + referral on Sep 20 → Nov 9.
- Admin-configurable: reward duration, stacking, max accumulated free days, validity, extension behaviour,
  attribution, fraud controls.
- Loop (§69): has Zerodha → check eligibility / refer someone; no Zerodha → open Zerodha via referral →
  connect → eligibility. Wording: *Zerodha Partner Benefit* or *Zerodha customers may qualify for complimentary
  access*. Never disguise broker acquisition.
