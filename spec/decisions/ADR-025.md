# ADR-025: Referral rewards and the free-eligibility page

## Owner answers
- Owner (T1 #183): a separate page to check free eligibility; ask existing Zerodha users to refer others; users
  without Zerodha can open with the owner's affiliate link.
- **Q60** (T1 #185) was "one referral = permanent free" — **replaced** by the owner in T1 #189: **one successful referral
  = 1 month of Pro**; next month needs another referral or payment. "Month" = **30 days** per referral, the
  number of days set in Admin (Q218 = A, owner 2026-09-29, ADR-038); the screen shows the exact new end date.
- **Q209 = A** (owner, 2026-09-29, ADR-044): success reaches the platform through an admin CSV upload of confirmed
  openings from the owner's Zerodha AP report; matched rows create entitlement events, unmatched rows go to review.
- **Q61 = A** (T1 #187): a referral counts only when the Zerodha account is opened through the referral and attributed;
  a click or started application does not count.
- **Q63 = C** (T1 #195): stacking admin-configurable, **on by default** (Pro until 10 Oct + referral on 20 Sep → 9 Nov).
- Admin settings: reward duration, stacking, max accumulated days, validity, extension of active rewards, one referred
  customer benefiting several referrers, attribution reversal/cancellation, fraud controls.
- Billing text for referral users: *"Pro — Free until 15 October. You earned this month through a successful referral.
  Refer another Zerodha customer to continue free, or subscribe to Pro."*
- Positioning: *"Zerodha Partner Benefit — Zerodha customers may qualify for complimentary access"*; broker
  acquisition is never disguised; the referral system stays out of the trading screens.
