# ADR-207: paper trading as Sensibull's Draft Portfolios

## Why
- In the 2026-10-29 walk an expert could build and "place" a strategy but not manage it: a roll changed the plan to
  22950 while the paper position stayed at 22900 with no warning (F-65); the builder priced an open position at the
  plan's prices, not its fills (F-66); fills were taken at the last traded price, not the price he planned (F-67);
  nothing settles at expiry (F-68); the default Positions tab said he held nothing (F-69); "1045% of funds" read as
  nonsense at low cash (F-70).
- Sensibull already solves each of these with one model, the draft portfolio as an order book, and the owner asked
  for its functionality and UI. Keeping the Paper tab on Positions follows the owner's 2026-10-28 instruction
  (ADR-201), which outranks Sensibull's separate page.
- The percentage basis was recorded as "as Sensibull does" (ADR-164) but Sensibull divides by funds needed
  (catalogue line 42); margin needed is the closest figure Zerodha gives us (F-62).

## Not decided here
- Adjustment playbooks (which adjustments to suggest when) stay with the owner (ADR-165 (6)).
- The adjustment-alert threshold at entry (F-71) is a deferred finding, not part of this decision.
