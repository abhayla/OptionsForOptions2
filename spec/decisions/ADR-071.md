# ADR-071: Money sentences say "before charges and taxes"

## Why
- The engine has no charges number yet (REQ-032: the charges model is open). Real charges are not small next to a
  beginner's numbers: Zerodha's own breakdown for one NIFTY option leg on 2026-10-08 was about ₹33 (F-33), so a
  4-leg iron condor in and out costs roughly ₹260 - about 3% of its ₹8,245.25 maximum loss.
- "At most ₹0.00 at expiry." (the wording that replaced a forbidden "cannot lose" promise) still reads as no risk
  when charges alone make a loss.

## Alternatives not taken
- Leave the sentences without a mention of charges until the charges model is decided.
- Decide the charges model now and put real charges into the numbers (larger work; this phrase goes in meanwhile).
