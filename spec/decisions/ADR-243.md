title: 'A roll places the new short strike at the playbook''s target delta, not at its entry distance from spot (SPEC CHANGE to REQ-046 Q255)'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-04 (question tool, "Target delta"): when an adjustment approach rolls a short strike (A-01, A-02,
  A-04, A-05, A-06 and every later roll), the new short is the first listed strike beyond the old one whose delta is
  at or under the playbook's target delta for the stage (the iron condor's Early delta, e.g. 0.20 for A-02), read on
  the chain of the expiry being opened; with no delta in that chain, the playbook's points fallback (200 weekly,
  500 monthly) applies. Spread widths are kept. The result stays an editable proposal: every strike can be changed,
  the Before/After is shown, and nothing is sent until the user confirms.
  Why: the playbooks were decided later and per strategy type and stage; a delta target tracks risk as volatility
  changes, while a fixed points distance does not; it is what is built and priced today; and the entry-distance rule
  needs an entry spot that is not stored.
  SPEC CHANGE: REQ-046 "Owner decision (2026-09-29, Q255)" ("re-places the short strike(s) at their entry distance
  from spot") is no longer the only rule: it becomes the second basis below.
  CONFIGURABLE (owner 2026-11-04, question tool, "Yes, and per strategy too"): target delta is the DEFAULT, not a
  fixed rule. "Roll basis" is a user adjustment preference with two values - "Target delta" (default) and "Entry
  distance from spot" (Q255: keep widths, re-place the short at its entry distance from spot, snapped to a listed
  strike; needs each strategy's entry spot stored) - and each strategy's plan sheet can override it for that strategy
  only. Precedence: the strategy's plan, else the user's preference, else the default. Every roll approach follows the
  basis in force, and the Compare names which basis built each roll. Owner principle (2026-11-04): every adjustment
  behaviour is a configurable default the user can change for themselves.
