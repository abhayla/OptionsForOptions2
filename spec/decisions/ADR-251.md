title: '"2x the credit received" means 2x the net credit collected so far: entry credit plus or minus every roll''s net credit or debit'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-05 ~09:4x IST (question tool, "Net credit so far (Recommended)"), defining the credit in ADR-247's
  default stop ("50 % of max loss or 2x the credit received, whichever comes first"): the credit is the strategy's net
  credit collected so far - the entry's net credit, plus each later adjustment's net credit, minus each later
  adjustment's net debit (booked trades included, the same trades the strategy's P&L counts). Example: an iron condor
  opened for a Rs 6,000 credit, then a roll that paid Rs 1,500 net -> credit Rs 4,500 -> the stop line is a loss of
  Rs 9,000. When the net credit so far is zero or a net debit, or cannot be read, the 2x-credit stop is "not checked"
  with that reason (fail closed, never a guessed line) and the max-loss stop is still checked and named. Exact decimal
  money (ADR-008). Applies to plans that hold the 2x-credit stop (new plans per ADR-250); a default the user can change
  (ADR-245). Decision support only (ADR-003).
status: accepted
