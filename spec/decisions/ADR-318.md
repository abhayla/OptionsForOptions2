decision: >-
  Owner decided 23 Nov 2026 17:20 IST (question D-04 of the 23 Nov live test). (1) When a strategy has used its plan
  maximum of adjustments (ADR-255 counting), rolls, shifts and added legs are not offered and the reason shown is
  "plan maximum of N reached". (2) Reduce and Close tested spread stay on offer, each marked "this would be adjustment
  N+1, over your plan maximum of N": the count is never capped for display. (3) Exit all stays on offer and shows no
  count, because closing every open leg is not an adjustment (ADR-255). (4) OWNER DECIDED 23 Nov 2026 23:20 IST:
  a strategy without a plan of its own takes its WHOLE rule set (alerts, adjustment ideas and the maximum) from the
  playbook that fits its shape AT ENTRY, for the life of the trade (real case: strategy 164, entered as an iron
  condor, reduced to a put spread, keeps the iron condor rules and "of 2"). A rule that needs a side the trade no
  longer holds answers "not checked" with its reason and never fires. A strategy with its own plan keeps it
  (ADR-214). Entry means the first basket of orders (ADR-255). Only when the entry shape cannot be classified (its
  entry contracts are no longer listed) does the current shape decide, so the rules never vanish. Not chosen: only
  the maximum from entry with the other rules from the current shape; everything from the current shape (the limit
  then changes or vanishes with each adjustment).
  (5) Consequence of (1) to (3), decided by the lead 23 Nov 21:45 so the offer and the server agree: at or over the
  maximum the server accepts a paper basket made only of orders that close or reduce open legs (Reduce, Close tested
  spread, buying back a short, Exit all) and refuses any basket that opens or adds to a leg, with the reason "plan
  maximum of N reached". Before this, the server refused every basket over the maximum except Exit all, so an offered
  Reduce could not be placed.

# ADR-318: at the adjustment limit, only risk-cutting routes, with the true count

Why: a limit that still offers a third roll is not a limit, and a count capped for display hides what the trader is about to do.
