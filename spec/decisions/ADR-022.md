# ADR-022: Zerodha Client ID binding, account transfer and anti-abuse

## Owner answers
- Owner (T1 #235): people may log in with a new Gmail after the trial and connect the same Zerodha ID — this must be
  tracked and blocked (no new trial), while still allowing a genuine email change.
- **Q82 = C** (T1 #237): block the second account; offer verification + transfer; trial history never resets.
  Owner (T1 #237): direct Zerodha customers who first logged in with the wrong Gmail must be able to move to the right
  one — handled by the same verified transfer; their free Pro follows the Client ID. ChatGPT's flow (T1 #238): the
  new account sees *"This Zerodha account is already linked to another account … you can transfer your account
  association after verification."* with **Verify & Switch Account**; after the transfer the old account shows
  *"Account association transferred. Please sign in using your new account."*; the Pro entitlement is not consumed,
  reset or turned into a trial. Outcomes (T1 #238, #248): ordinary user seeking a second trial → no; ordinary user
  legitimately moving → transfer after verification; direct customer moving to a new Gmail → complimentary Pro
  continues; disconnect and reconnect with another Gmail → trial/benefits never reset; a different legitimate Client
  ID → its own trial.
- **Q83 = D**: automatic transfer for straightforward cases; suspicious/repeated ones (repeated transfers, several
  recent email changes, repeated association attempts, unusual account-creation patterns, **conflicting identity
  information, other configurable fraud signals** — T1 #272 §26) go to Admin review; transfers audited and
  rate-limited — no bouncing *Gmail A → B → C → A* without controls (T1 #238). A transfer preserves the Platform User
  data where appropriate, the Zerodha association, trial history, Pro entitlement and referral history, and never
  issues a second trial (T1 #272 §8). Transfer audit record (T1 #238): old platform account, new platform account,
  Client ID, time, reason, verification method, admin/system approval, previous and new entitlement history. Wording: *"We need to verify this account change."* — never "suspected of abuse".
- **Q84 = A**: one Client ID → one platform account; no family/business sharing.
- **Q87 = A**: no attempt to link one person across several legitimate Zerodha accounts. One trial per Client ID,
  with "normal abuse controls such as rate limiting and device/IP signals, but don't aggressively block" (T1 #246
  option A; Q97 still rules out aggressive fingerprinting). The mobile number is **not** a trial-uniqueness key in
  V1 (option B rejected; T1 #272 §4).
- **Q95 = A** (T1 #263): reconnecting the same Client ID restores the association (daily session expiry is a
  connection state, T1 #265–#266). Expired-session message (T1 #272 §6): *"Zerodha connection expired. Re-authenticate
  with Zerodha to continue broker-dependent functionality."* A new session reuses the Client ID and never creates a
  new trial, customer identity or entitlement reset.
- **Q96 = A** (T1 #267): account deletion keeps only a minimal anti-abuse record (Client ID or protected identifier,
  trial consumed, entitlement reference, audit reference, timestamps), subject to privacy policy.
- **Q97 = A** (T1 #269): users who never connect Zerodha get a trial per platform account; no fingerprinting.
- User-facing message: *"This Zerodha account has already been associated with another platform account. Your 7-day
  Pro trial is not available again for this Zerodha account."* Recovery wording (T1 #272 §28): *"If you previously
  created an account using a different email, you can verify your identity and move your account association."*
