  strategy that never had an order. (5) The Undo after a delete is shown where the user is looking (the fixed toast of
  ADR-193, not below the whole list), for the full undo window counted from when it appears.

# ADR-271: paper orders block delete

Why: the iron condor UI test on 2026-11-06 found that every paper condor could be deleted like an empty draft,
leaving its paper legs and alerts behind with no visible strategy, and that the Undo after a delete rendered four
screens below the click.
