# ADR-001: V1 product scope

## Decision
- Underlyings: **NIFTY** (NSE) and **SENSEX** (BSE). Exchanges per §38/Q174.
- Broker: **Zerodha only**.
- Leg types: Call Buy, Call Sell, Put Buy, Put Sell, **Futures Buy, Futures Sell**.
- Responsive web from day 1. Desktop: strategy construction, Option Chain, analysis, wide tables, payoff.
  Mobile: alerts, monitoring, P&L, review, execution. No native app initially.
- The product feels like a strategy operating system, not Bloomberg/Kite. Home is action-first.
- Core promise: *Plan the trade. Follow the strategy. Then execute.* Positioning: *Your Zerodha account. Your
  strategy. Your decision. Our technology.*

## Owner answers on record (Chat1)
- **Q8 = Yes**: NIFTY + SENSEX only, all six leg types, unlimited multi-leg combinations through one engine.
- **Public SaaS = B** (question number not in the excerpt): a public product, not only an internal tool for
  existing customers. Consequence stated then: multi-tenant from the start, launched progressively — small beta
  first, public later.
- **Q14 = B**: responsive web from day 1; desktop for construction/analysis, mobile for monitoring, alerts, review,
  execution; no native apps initially (a responsive PWA/web first).

## Account boundaries (locked in the 15 Sep handoff §8–§9)
Each user connects **their own** Zerodha account; strategies belong to that user's account. No managing other
people's accounts, no pooled money, no copy trading, no discretionary management, **no shared broker credentials**.
No other broker (Angel One, Upstox, Dhan, Groww, Paytm Money) in V1.

## V1 non-goals (§94)
Multiple brokers; unrestricted autonomous trading; full quant backtesting; tick-by-tick history for every option;
native mobile apps; unrestricted order terminal; AI trade-advice chatbot; copy trading; pooled money; discretionary
account management; recreating broker RMS.

## Gaps
- Futures legs are in scope but the locked expiry formulas cover options only: see `spec/open-questions.md` Q207.
