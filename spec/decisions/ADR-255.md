title: 'A partial exit counts as an adjustment against the plan maximum; Exit all does not'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:0x IST (question tool, "Partial exits count, Exit all doesn't"). On a tracked paper strategy,
  every paper basket after entry that changes the open position counts once against the plan's maximum adjustments
  (REQ-161) and in "adjustments used" (REQ-163 totals, ADR-214 compare row, rule T-18): rolls, conversions, shifts,
  added legs, and exits of SOME legs (one leg, several legs, part of a leg). A basket that closes EVERY open leg
  (Exit all, the "Exit the whole strategy" approach, a conditional exit that closes all legs, expiry settlement) is
  the end of the trade: it is not an adjustment, never raises T-18, and the history labels it "Exit", not
  "adjustment N". Why: live test 2026-11-06 (strategy 86) recorded the final Exit all as "adjustment 3" and showed
  "3 of 2" in the compare; the owner judged closing the whole trade is not an adjustment, while closing part of it
  changes the trade's shape and does count.
status: accepted
