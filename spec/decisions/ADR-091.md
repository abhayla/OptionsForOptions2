# ADR-091: Freeze limits refreshed weekly; review warns after 8 days

## Why
- Freeze limits are not set once for years. F-38: NIFTY moved from 1,800 to 3,510 units on 5 Oct 2026, and other index
  limits changed in Sep 2025, Dec 2025 and Mar 2026. A limit learned once goes stale within months.
- A daily download is more than revisions need (a few a year, announced days ahead), so a weekly refresh plus an age
  warning gives the same safety with no scheduled job.
