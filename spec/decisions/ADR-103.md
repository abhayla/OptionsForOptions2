# ADR-103: Out-of-order fills are refused, never a side flip

## Why
- Order updates and trade reads can arrive out of order. Read as-is, an exit fill before its entry shows a short
  position that never existed, and anything acting in that window (monitor, exit, adjustment) would act on a lie.
- Blocking is the existing answer to "the platform and Zerodha disagree" (ADR-018): no new state is invented.

## Alternatives not taken
- Accept and let the end state settle: the in-between state reads as a side flip (F-40 A1).
- Hold the fill in a pending queue and apply it later automatically: a second store of fill facts, which the ledger
  design (one store, finding fill-not-exactly-once) removed on purpose; the broker re-sends the trade on the next read.
