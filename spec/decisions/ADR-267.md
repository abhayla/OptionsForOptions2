id: ADR-267
date: '2026-11-06'
title: 'A level the user asked to watch is never held by the daily alert cap'
decision: >-
  Decided under the owner's standing delegation for strategy behaviour ("go with your recommendation"). A user's own
  index level watch (A-37) raises its alert when crossed even when the strategy's daily alert cap (per alert style)
  is used up, and it does not count against that cap; the cap still limits the automatic playbook alerts. The cap
  value stays a setting (ADR-264). Why: live test 2026-11-06 15:00: monthly condor 91 raised two automatic alerts
  (T-02 breakeven, S-02 trending day) at the 15:00 candle close, using the monthly cap of 2, and the user's watch
  "Above 24,870" was held with reason "daily limit": the one alert the user explicitly asked for was the one
  suppressed.
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Exempts A-37 user level watches from the alert-style daily cap.'
provenance:
- spec/decisions/ADR-264.md
