id: ADR-267
date: '2026-11-06'
title: 'A level the user asked to watch is never held by the daily alert cap'
decision: >-
  Decided under the owner's standing delegation for strategy behaviour ("go with your recommendation"). A user's own
  index level watch (A-37) raises its alert when crossed even when the strategy's daily alert cap (per alert style)
  is used up, and it does not count against that cap; the cap still limits the automatic playbook alerts. The cap
  value stays a setting (ADR-264). Why: live test 2026-11-06 15:00: monthly condor 91 raised two automatic alerts
  (T-02 breakeven, S-02 trending day) at the 15:00 candle close, using the monthly cap of 2, and the user's watch
  "Above 24,870" was held with reason "daily limit": the one alert the user explicitly asked for was the one
  suppressed.
  Amendment (same day, same delegation): day rules (the carry check before a weekend or a market holiday, and any
  other clock/day rule) are exempt from the cap in the same way, and NO crossing is ever dropped without trace: when
  an alert is not raised it is a held row with its reason. Every record carries its "What happened" text (a day rule
  names itself, e.g. "Last session before the weekend, 15:00: run the carry check (overnight margin, gap loss)").
  Cases 2026-11-06 15:05: condor 92's carry-check record 158 had condition and summary empty; condor 91's carry check
  flagged at 15:00 but was neither recorded nor held (cap of 2 already used).
  Amendment 2 (2026-11-27, Lane J under the same delegation, owner away; spec basis: this decision's own reason, "the
  one alert the user explicitly asked for was the one suppressed"): the plan's own exits are exempt from the cap in the
  same way and do not count against it: its loss stop lines (T-06 % of max loss, T-16 % of margin, the 2x-credit
  line), its rupee loss stop and rupee profit target, and its time exit T-10b. The % profit target T-07 stays capped
  for now, although a Target saved in the plan switches it on (ADR-304 (1)): a held target costs unbooked profit, not a
  loss past the stop; whether to exempt it too is listed for the owner. Case 2026-11-27: paper trade A-D
  #200 (weekly style, cap 4) raised T-03, T-14, T-01 and the T-10b time exit, so its Rs 100 loss stop, crossed from
  about 11:00 at a loss of Rs 162.5, was held as "daily limit" and no stop alert showed until the exit at 11:23. Not
  chosen: raise the cap (still drops a stop on a busier day); exempt every plan rule (the cap exists to limit
  automatic playbook alerts).
  Amendment 3 (2026-11-27, Lane J; accepted by the coordinator under the owner's market-hours authority, 13:38 IST: "a
  plan's loss stop is never dropped or held without an alert (REQ-170 AC-5)"; recorded for the owner's review): a
  plan's loss stop (the % of max loss, % of margin and 2x-credit lines, the typed rupee stop, and any plan rule whose
  triggers include T-06 or T-16, whatever its id) is never held by the quiet time after the open or by the daily cap,
  and is not counted against the cap. It opens on the first live check past its line, even inside a candle that saw a
  price pause: the pause keeps that candle from having a close, but never keeps a live check from judging a stop; a
  stale or missing price is never judged (ADR-016). It is never held at entry: a stop already true when the plan is
  accepted alerts at the first check. A skipped check is logged with its reason when a strategy's skip reason changes. Known gap, listed for the owner: a mixed rule (any_of with T-06 or T-16 and an adjustment trigger) has one alert record; once it is open or dismissed on its adjustment member, a later stop crossing inside it raises nothing of its own (a deferred issue). Cases
  2026-11-27: A-E #202 (Stop Rs 50, loss Rs 85.75 from 12:13:44, no alert) and A-G #207 (Stop Rs 80, loss about Rs 199
  from about 13:14, no alert for 19 minutes, zero alert rows). Not chosen: keep quiet time for stops (a stop crossed at
  09:16 would wait to 09:30 or 09:45).
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Exempts A-37 user level watches, day rules (amendment 1) and the plan''s own stops and exits (amendment 2, 27 Nov 2026) from the alert-style daily cap; amendment 3: a plan''s loss stop is never held by quiet time, a paused candle or the at-entry hold.'
