# ADR-031: Source-of-truth order and question numbering

## Decision
- Conflict order: 1 `spec/decisions/` → 2 other `spec/` → 3 full chat transcripts (T1, T2) → 4 handoff summaries,
  Chat1/Chat2 excerpts → 5 technical preference.
- Why transcripts beat handoffs (measured 2026-09-28): the handoffs got Q35 wrong (said A, owner chose C), said Q33A
  and Q81 were unanswered (both answered), had no record of Q60–Q71 (all answered), and their Q102–Q148 records do not
  match the real questions.
- Question numbers Q177 and Q178 were each used twice in T2 (see ADR-014); the register lists both meanings.
- New questions: Q204+, in `spec/open-questions.md`, each with question, recommendation, reason, owner's answer.
