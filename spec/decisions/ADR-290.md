# ADR-290: tracking, not order placing; hand entry of a held iron condor

## Why
- The owner's product is decision support that follows a position to its exit (ADR-003), whichever broker holds it.
  Placing orders is not needed for that and was the slowest, riskiest part of step 4.

## What changes
- Standing direction goal: "A person who is not the owner opens a link, logs in, builds an iron condor or enters one
  they already hold, and the app tracks it and suggests adjustments until they exit."
- ADR-289 part (1) (second person on their own login) stays; part (2) (one real order) is withdrawn.
