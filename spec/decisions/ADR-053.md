# ADR-053: Per-user feeds in V1; visible pause when the session ends

## Evidence
- NSE requires "All API sessions shall be compulsorily logged out every day before the start of the next trading day"
  (F-12, A.8); Kite tokens expire at 6 AM the next day (multi-broker doc §2).
- Live prices may not be shared with third-party platforms (F-23), so one user's feed cannot drive another user's
  screen.
- One user's Kite connection carries a full two-expiry NIFTY and SENSEX chain (1,088 subscriptions of 3,000) plus that
  user's positions (multi-broker doc §4).

## Example
An Iron Condor is active; the user does not log in on Monday; at 11:00 NIFTY falls 400 points. The strategy shows
"Monitoring paused - reconnect Zerodha" from 09:15, the morning reminder went out at 08:45, and no Red alert is
claimed.
