title: 'A trade may have no plan, but the trade page must say so and show the default rules'
decision: >-
  Owner decision 2026-11-13 (planning session, answer D2: a trade may have no plan, but the page must say so). (1)
  Skipping or failing to save the plan sheet is allowed; the app does not save a plan on the user's behalf. (2) A
  trade page whose trade has no saved plan shows a card that says "No plan saved yet: default rules apply", lists the
  default profit target and the default stop (the values in force from the playbook or system default, ADR-214
  precedence: strategy plan, then chosen playbook, then system default), and has a "Set plan" button that opens the
  plan sheet (REQ-161). (3) The card never reads as an empty space: a trade without a plan and a trade whose plan
  could not be read are different states and say different things (a failed read says "could not load, retry"). (4)
  Nothing is written for the user until they press Save in the plan sheet.

# ADR-295: no plan is allowed, and it is stated

Why: on 4 of 8 practice trades the user could not tell whether a plan existed. The owner allowed trades without a
plan but not silence about it.
