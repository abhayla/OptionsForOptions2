title: 'A-37 "watch a level" is an adjustment alert: stored per strategy, a yes/no rule through the alert gate'
decision: >-
  Technical decision for GC-IC1 step 4c, made by the orchestrator under the owner's standing rule that technical calls
  are not asked. (1) A-37 "Alert only / watch a level" is an ADJUSTMENT alert, not a risk exit (REQ-170 AC-5 risk
  exits stay outside the alert gate and never read a watch). (2) A watch is stored per strategy and user, one active
  watch per direction ("above" / "below"), set and cleared only by the strategy's owner (another user's strategy reads
  as not found), as insert-only dated rows (table strategy_level_watch, migration 0038; a clear is a new row with no
  level). The level is a number above zero. (3) The background worker reads the watches on its rebuild pass with the
  rest of the gate load, never per check. The evaluator answers each watch as a yes/no rule with the Identifier-safe
  id watch.level.above / watch.level.below: reached when the spot is at or above / at or below the level (inclusive);
  no spot or a withheld check gives no reading (never false). The reading goes through gate_readings into the alert
  gate, the one writer of records (ADR-237 (6)), so one record opens per crossing episode and the rule re-arms at a
  false candle close (ADR-237 (1)). Example: watch above 23,800; spot 23,790 -> nothing; 23,805 at the next close ->
  one record "Watched level reached (at or above)"; more ticks above -> still one; back to 23,780 at a close then
  23,810 -> a second record. (4) Step 4a's REQ-041 rule object for A-37 is removed: one path. (5) Amends ADR-238:
  A-37 is a non-trade approach (it opens an alert record and builds no trades or Compare column), like A-23's
  delta-neutral figures and A-38's pause; ADR-238's exit and reduce approaches are unchanged.
status: accepted
