title: 'Iron condor system playbook adds S-08 (event day) and S-09 (weekend or holiday) as Guidance situations'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-04 (question tool, "Add both as Guidance"): the iron condor system playbook lists S-08 "the day before
  a known event" and S-09 "a weekend or market holiday ahead" with the other ten situations, each labelled
  "Guidance" (the user checks it; the app does not raise it), with its possible approaches shown as possible, never as
  one best (ADR-003). S-08 stays Guidance until an event list exists; S-09's text points to the before-weekend /
  before-holiday carry-check day rule, which the app does check. The change ships as a new version of the iron condor
  system playbook; users' own copies and saved strategy plans are not changed.
  Why: REQ-159 AC-5 makes S-01 to S-12 part of every playbook and each must be a rule or labelled Guidance; S-08 and
  S-09 were missing and unclassified (GC-IC1 step 10, evidence/W-206/AC-5.md). Same treatment ADR-240 gave S-03..S-05.
