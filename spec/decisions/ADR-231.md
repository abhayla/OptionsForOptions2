title: 'Roll debit limit "Rs 500 or 10% of max profit" means the higher of the two'
decision: >-
  A playbook's "Most debit paid for one roll" written as "Rs 500 or 10% of max profit" allows a roll whose debit is
  at most the HIGHER of Rs 500 and 10% of the strategy's max profit; a roll above it is refused with the reason
  (ADR-230 (1)). Example: max profit Rs 8,000 -> 10% is Rs 800 -> a roll of up to Rs 800 is allowed, Rs 801 is
  refused; max profit Rs 3,000 -> 10% is Rs 300 -> the Rs 500 floor applies. When max profit is unlimited or not
  known, the limit cannot be confirmed: any debit above Rs 500 is refused as "cannot confirm" (fail closed).
