# ADR-021: Registration and identity

## Owner answers
- **Q71** (T1 #213): Google login, then a first-time registration capturing email, **phone number**, first/last name,
  **market experience**, **profession**. (No email/password fallback was chosen.)
- **Q72** (T1 #215): verify the mobile by **WhatsApp OTP** through the **owner's own separate OTP system** (to be
  integrated later); email OTP would not verify the phone. Store number, country code, verification status and time,
  OTP attempt/rate-limit data.
- **Q73 = A**: the Google email counts as verified; no email OTP.
- **Q74, Q75** (T1 #221): date of birth and location are **optional**; no full address.
- **Q76**: experience — New to stock markets · Beginner · Intermediate · Experienced · Advanced/Professional (shapes
  presentation only). **Q77**: profession — Salaried/Employee · Business Owner · Self-employed/Freelancer · Trader ·
  Investor · Finance Professional · Student · Retired · Homemaker · Other (admin may edit later).
- Terms & privacy consent at registration; the 7-day trial starts when registration completes (ADR-023).
- **Q85 = A**: change email inside the same account (Platform User ID, Zerodha link, trial, entitlements, strategies,
  referrals kept; no new trial; audited). **Q86 = A**: the old email stops working as a login immediately; kept only
  in audit history.

## Identity layers (T1 #272, owner approved #271)
Platform User ID (permanent) · Google identity/email · mobile identity · Zerodha Client ID (persistent) · Zerodha
authorization session (temporary, ~daily) · commercial entitlement. Never interchangeable.
The Platform User ID owns (T1 #272 §2): strategies, historical simulations, strategy history, execution history,
adjustment history, notifications/preferences, referral history, subscription history, entitlement history and the
Zerodha association. Changing the Gmail address never creates a new Platform User ID.
