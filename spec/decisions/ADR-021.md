# ADR-021: Identity layers and registration

## Decision
- Six layers (§61): Platform User ID · Google identity/email · Mobile identity · Zerodha Client ID · Zerodha
  authorization/session · Commercial entitlement. Email can change, sessions and Zerodha authorization can expire;
  the Client ID ↔ entitlement history never resets.
- Google Sign-In preferred; its email counts as verified; no email OTP.
- Mobile: WhatsApp OTP through **the owner's own existing OTP system** (Latest handoff §8: "using the user's
  separate OTP system") — reuse it, do not build a new one. Store separately: number, country code, verification status and
  timestamp, attempt/rate-limit metadata, email verification status.
- Required: first name, last name, Google-verified email, WhatsApp-verified mobile, market experience, profession,
  legal/consent acknowledgements. Optional: DOB, city/location. **No full address.**
- Market experience: New to stock markets · Beginner · Intermediate · Experienced · Advanced/Professional.
- Profession: Salaried/Employee · Business Owner · Self-employed/Freelancer · Trader · Investor · Finance
  Professional · Student · Retired · Homemaker · Other.
- Email change keeps Platform User ID, Zerodha association, trial, entitlements, strategies/history, referrals (Q85).
  Old email stops being a login identifier immediately; kept only in security/audit history (Q86).
