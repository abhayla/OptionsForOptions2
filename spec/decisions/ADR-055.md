# ADR-055: Build to the strictest reading until the written answers arrive

## Evidence
- An Authorised Person "shall not charge any amount from the clients" (SEBI Master Circular, 17 Jun 2025); no incentive
  "for account opening ... or any kind of subscription plan" (NSE/COMP/55482 §5.5(a)) - F-26.
- Research services include "any other service of similar nature or character"; no tool exemption (F-24). A broker
  was fined over a SaaS algo platform's return claims (F-15); APs may not associate with such platforms (F-25).

## Why this default
It costs nothing in the core (Stages 4-5 build neither), it keeps the owner's AP licence and Zerodha's relationship
safe, and every restricted item is reversible by one decision row when the answers arrive.

## Examples
- Allowed: "Iron Condor: sell a call and a put, buy wings further out - choose your strikes" with a live payoff.
- Held back: "Sell 25,000 CE and 23,500 PE, stop at 1.5x premium" picked by the platform.
- Held back: "Free Pro forever because you opened your Zerodha account with us".
