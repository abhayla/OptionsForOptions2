# ADR-092: No freeze-limit splitting anywhere

## Why
- The reasons ADR-081 gave for entry baskets (its rejected "split" option; ADR-054's one order per leg) hold equally
  for Complete, Retry and exit, so the scope widens and the reasons are not repeated here.
- One rule everywhere is simpler to explain on the review screen: "at most 54 lots in one order".
