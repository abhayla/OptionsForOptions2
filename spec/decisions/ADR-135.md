  owns them. (3) This replaces REQ-057 AC-1''s seven status words and ADR-133.'

# ADR-135: four order states, the rest is Kite's

Why: the owner's rule keeps the app to sending and confirming; two words could not tell "never sent" or "rejected" from
"placed", so the owner chose four short states and a link to Kite for everything else.
