title: 'Every open strategy has a "Compare approaches" action, with or without an alert'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~14:2x IST (question tool, "Yes, 'Compare approaches' always"). Every open strategy (paper or real)
  shows a "Compare approaches" action on its strategy page. It opens the same compare as an alert does (REQ-156), with
  every approach that applies shown (not only "No change"), and records no opportunity; a basket prepared from it is
  linked to no alert. Why: live test 2026-11-06 (strategy 87): the sold call entered at delta 0.48 (T-03 limit 0.30)
  was "already true" (REQ-159 / F-71), so no alert and no "Review adjustment" button appeared and the trader had no way
  to see the possible rolls.
status: accepted
