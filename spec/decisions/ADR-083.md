# ADR-083: Expiry is a settlement event; expiry-day entry allowed with a warning

## Why
- Index options are cash-settled: an expired leg leaves Zerodha's positions by design, which is not a broker mismatch.
- Treating it as a mismatch would block every calendar strategy once per near expiry.
- Expiry-day trades are common for intermediate users; the warning names the real extra margin, and the
  acknowledgement covers the short-side risk.

## Alternatives not taken
- Block new entries on a contract's expiry day: safer, but removes a common use.
- Treat an expired leg as a mismatch resolved by hand: every calendar blocks once per near expiry.
