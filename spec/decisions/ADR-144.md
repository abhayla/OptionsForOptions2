# ADR-144: Landing page pricing while the paid plan is held

Why: REQ-010 lists pricing on the public site, but ADR-055 keeps every paid plan from going live until Zerodha
compliance answers. The owner chose a plain "free during early access" line, so the page does not promise a price that
is not allowed yet.
