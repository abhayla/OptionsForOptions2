title: 'In the adjustment compare, an approach that leaves no open position shows no Greeks after and cannot lead the Greeks rows'
decision: >-
  In the approach compare (REQ-156), the four "Greeks after" rows (net delta, gamma, theta, vega) describe the position
  that stays open. An approach that leaves no open leg (today "Exit the whole strategy", A-31) shows "No position left
  after exit" in those four rows instead of 0, and cannot lead them, the same way a value that cannot be computed
  cannot lead (REQ-156 AC-3). It still takes part, and can lead, in every other row where it really differs (for
  example "Smallest max loss"). Example from scenario ADJ-02: Exit led 5 of 18 rows, 3 of them (Closest to
  delta-neutral, Lowest gamma, Lowest vega) only because nothing stays open; under this rule those 3 leads go to the
  approaches that keep a position.
