title: 'T-19 (adjustment cost vs premium left) is checked by the background worker in live and test-data mode'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-06 ~02:0x IST (question tool, "Live too"). The background worker checks T-19 the same way the screen
  check does: it prices the cheapest offered approach with the same charge rates the screen uses (read from the app,
  cached 5 minutes, no extra Kite call) and raises the T-19 record when the cost crosses the plan's share of the
  premium left, in live mode and in test-data mode alike. When no charge rates can be read, T-19 is "not checked" with
  its reason, never passed. Why: REQ-165 puts the playbook triggers in the background; before this, the worker passed
  no charge rates, so T-19 answered "no approach priced" on every check and could never raise an alert while no screen
  was open (finding background-trigger-without-the-inputs-it-needs, issue #661). Unlike T-14 (ADR-253), T-19 is a
  warning the user already sees on the screen; this only makes the background raise the same warning.
status: accepted
