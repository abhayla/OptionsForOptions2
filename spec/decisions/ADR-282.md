title: 'Iron condor proof matrix: NIFTY only, 5 condor types, test data first then live prices, through the screens'
decision: >-
  Owner answer 2026-11-09 (question tool, GC-ICGUIDE planning). An iron condor's adjustment guidance counts as proven
  only when each of 5 NIFTY condor types goes through entry, warnings, a guided 1st and 2nd adjustment, and an exit
  or expiry: (1) intraday: entered and closed on expiry day, 0 days left; (2) weekly, entered 5-6 days before its
  expiry; (3) the monthly expiry of this month; (4) next month's monthly; (5) the monthly 2 months out. Each type is
  proven on test data first (the scenario library, tests/adjust_scenarios_gen.py, and the app's scenario mode) and
  then on live prices, every step through the app's own screens as a user would, paper only (ADR-281). A type that
  fails on test data gets no live pass until fixed. SENSEX comes later. Guidance defects in scope: audit C9, C10, C11,
  C12, C13, C6 and issues #623 #647 #650 #651 #652 #654 #661 #662.
