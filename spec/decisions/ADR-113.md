# ADR-113: A closing basket is checked on Zerodha's figure alone

## Why
- ADR-094's top-up guards an entry against paying more premium than the funds cover. For a basket that only closes
  held positions the risk runs the other way: blocking it keeps the user in the position, the outcome ADR-037 forbids
  ("never stop a user getting out of risk").
- PR #418 round 1 passed only closing baskets whose top-up was 0; the independent review showed a normal buy-back
  of a short (limit off the ask, above Zerodha's premium) was still blocked at a negative balance.
