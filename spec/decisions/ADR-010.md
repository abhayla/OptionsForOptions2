# ADR-010: Monitoring, the Strategy Plan and the Live Position view

## Owner answers
- **Q20 = B** (T1 #41): strategy-health monitoring of P&L, underlying, distance to breakevens and short strikes, time to
  expiry, IV changes, Greeks, margin utilisation, entry/adjustment/exit conditions → 🟢 Healthy · 🟡 Watch ·
  🟠 Adjustment opportunity · 🔴 Exit condition reached. Rule-based, no prediction in V1. Calm messages, e.g. *"Watch —
  NIFTY moved closer to your upper short strike. Distance 1.2%, your trigger 0.8%. You don't need to act yet."*
- **Q150** (owner-driven, T2 #87–#90): **before execution** the Strategy screen shows the plan (strategy, market view,
  entry, exit, adjustment rules, what will be monitored, what happens on a trigger); **after execution** live
  monitoring is on the Live Position / Live Strategy view (P&L, underlying, distances, Greeks, margin, DTE, risk state,
  triggers, adjustment opportunities, orders). Tabs such as *Planning · Live Position · History* on one strategy.

## ChatGPT (delegated)
- Q154: monitoring runs even with no exit rule (*"Your strategy is being monitored"*).
- Q156: one **Strategy Plan** view: 1 Strategy · 2 Market view · 3 Entry · 4 Position · 5 Exit · 6 Adjustments ·
  7 Monitoring · 8 Risk.
- Q158: Strategy and Position screens use the same engine; the Strategy view compares plan vs now, the Position view
  shows the live position.
