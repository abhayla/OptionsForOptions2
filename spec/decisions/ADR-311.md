title: 'When an open leg has no live price, the practice total stays paused, the leg without a price is named, and every other open leg keeps its own figure'
decision: >-
  Decided by the run on 2026-11-15 under the owner rule of 14 Nov ("whenever you have question, go with your
  recommendation ... as an option trader"). (1) The total of a practice book stays paused whenever any open leg has
  no live price (REQ-109 AC-5 stands for every TOTAL: unbooked, total P&L, and the figures built from them). A total
  that leaves out a leg is never shown. (2) The pause line names each leg that has no price, by its contract, in plain
  words, with the time the others are as of. (3) Every open leg that does have a live price shows its own unbooked
  figure in the legs table; a leg without a price shows "no price" in that cell, never 0 and never a blank.
  (4) Booked P&L and charges already paid are unaffected by a missing price and are always shown. (5) A book whose
  only unpriced legs are EXPIRED contracts says so, as the reason: the user is told the contract has expired, not that
  prices are "not live". (6) The same rule applies to a real (broker) book wherever the app shows one (practice
  behaves like real).

# ADR-311: the total stays paused, the reason is named

Why: one leg without a price blanked a whole book with no word about which leg. The total must not guess, but the
screen can say which leg is missing and still show the legs it knows.
