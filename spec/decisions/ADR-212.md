# ADR-212: Paper positions after hours show the last close

## Why
- The owner expects every screen that needs market data to show the last close until the market opens.
- REQ-109 AC-5 paused paper P&L on any stale price, which after hours is every price, so the paper screens were blank
  in the evening while the builder (ADR-197) showed the same legs priced.

## What stays
- No fill at a closing price: paper exit and entry still need live prices (REQ-109 AC-5's refusal text in hours;
  ADR-119 outside hours).
- In market hours a stale price still pauses paper P&L, with its "as of <time>".
