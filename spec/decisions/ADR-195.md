# ADR-195: No shape name while a leg is left out

## Why
- REQ-076 AC-8 names the shape for "the current leg set" and AC-6 lets a leg be left out of the numbers; neither says
  which set the name follows, so an iron condor with its call hedge unticked was still called an iron condor.
- Sensibull drops the name and the hedge controls as soon as any leg is unticked (screenshot 111); ADR-152 makes that
  the default.
