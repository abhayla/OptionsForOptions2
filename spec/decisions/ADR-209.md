# ADR-209: what-if P&L at target includes booked P&L on a paper strategy

## Why
- A trader reading "P&L at 23,000" asks where the whole strategy will stand; Max Profit / Max Loss on the same screen
  already include booked, so an open-legs-only target figure disagreed with them by the booked amount (962.00 on 63).
