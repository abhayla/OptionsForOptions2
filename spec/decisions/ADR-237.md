title: 'Alert discipline, technical definitions: re-arm band, candle close, record timing, lapse, storage, one writer'
decision: >-
  Technical reconciliation of REQ-170 with the existing spec (GC-ALERT1 step 2), decided by the orchestrator under the
  owner's standing rule that technical calls are not asked.
  (1) Re-arm band: REQ-170 AC-2 "the distance from the stage's entry value to the threshold" is read as the
  threshold's own size: band = margin x |threshold of the last record's level|. The value at entry is not stored for
  delta and spot-distance rules (T-01, T-03); for the P&L-ratio rules (T-06, T-07) the value at entry is 0, so both
  readings give the same number. Example: strategy 66 T-07 at 80 % intraday (10 %) re-arms only at or below 72 %.
  A yes/no rule (T-02) re-arms at a close where its condition is false.
  (2) Candle close: candles of the style's length are aligned to 09:15 IST; a candle's close is the last value a check
  read in it; a candle with no readable value has no close (neither clears nor opens, as ADR-217). Closes are processed
  on the worker's about-once-a-second wake (ADR-233 (6)) whether or not a price changed; the engine recompute itself
  still runs only on a price change (ADR-235 (3)).
  (3) Record timing: ADR-235 (4) and REQ-165 AC-1 "recorded within the style's check interval of the crossing price"
  become "opened at the close of the candle that confirms the crossing, at most one check interval after that close":
  at most 1 min + 10 s after the crossing price intraday, 5 min + 30 s weekly, 15 min + 2 min monthly.
  (4) Lapse: an open record lapses when a close clears its line by the re-arm band (the episode has ended), or when the
  strategy closes; not at the first close back under the line. Amends REQ-112 AC-2's "Lapsed (the condition stopped
  holding ...)". Example: strategy 66's single 10:40 record stays open through 11:15 (closes 78.69-83.17 %); the old
  rule lapsed 29 of its 30 records within minutes (2026-11-02).
  (5) Storage: the gate state is rebuilt on worker start from adjustment_suggestions (last record per rule: time,
  threshold, dismissed or not; the day's record count for the cap). "Cleared" is not stored: after a restart a rule
  counts as not yet cleared (quiet; an escalation still opens). A held crossing (REQ-170 AC-4) is a row in a new
  insert-only table (strategy, rule, stage, value, threshold, reason daily_limit | quiet_time, held_at by the database
  clock), and a record's "worse" marker (none | last | dismissed) is a new insert-only column of adjustment_suggestions;
  one migration, guarded like the suggestion table (no update, no delete), Tier A.
  (6) One writer: only the background worker opens and lapses records, through the one gate
  (backend/ofo/adjustment/alert_gate.py); the screen's adjustment check shows the current condition and the stored
  records and writes none (REQ-165: checked by the workers, "not when a screen asks").
  (7) The daily cap is per strategy across its rules, escalations included, reset at the first close of a new IST
  date; a cap-held crossing stays held for its episode. A quiet-time crossing is deferred: it opens at the first close
  after the quiet time if still beyond the line and armed.
  (8) Amendment 2026-11-03 (GC-IC1 step 5a, orchestrator): when a strategy's STAGE changes (the ladder moves, e.g.
  T-01 weekly 0.5 -> 0.3 on the last day), an open record is re-levelled against the new ladder by the same rule as a
  playbook change (alert_gate restore); a close beyond the new, stricter line is then worse and opens at once.
  Example: record at T-01 0.5, expiry day ladder (0.3), close 0.256 -> worse, one record.
