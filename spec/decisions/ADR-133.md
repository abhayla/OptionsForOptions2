  words) and the one-line note "Not placed on Kite". (2) No eighth status word is added. (3) Orders that Zerodha did
  receive keep Zerodha''s own status mapped to the seven words.'

# ADR-133: the status of a basket never placed on Kite

Why: Zerodha never received an order, so no Zerodha status applies; the owner kept the seven-word list and added a note.
