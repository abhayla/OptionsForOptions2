# ADR-057: Identity holds while the contract is live; tokens retire after expiry

## Why ADR-052 was wrong
ADR-052 said "a token seen again with a different expiry is a new contract". The raw NSE files show the exchange
moving the expiry of live contracts under the same token:
- 62964: NIFTY 31000 PE expiring 26-Mar-2026 (Jun 2025), then 31-Mar-2026 (from Sep 2025) - the NSE move of expiry
  day from Thursday to Tuesday.
- 61746: NIFTY 23000 CE expiring 27-Dec-2029 (Jan 2025), then 24-Dec-2029 (Sep 2025); later the same token became a
  WIPRO contract (2026).
Under ADR-052 the Sep 2025 expiry move would have created new contracts and orphaned every open strategy on them.

## The rule, with both real cases
- Live contract, expiry moved (62964): same contract; history row "expiry 2026-03-26 -> 2026-03-31".
- Token back after its contract expired (67245: ABCAPITAL 25-Aug-2026 410 PE, then NIFTYNXT50 29-Dec-2026 72200 PE):
  the ABCAPITAL contract is retired; NIFTYNXT50 is a new contract.
- Two live contracts never share a token on the same day (F-21: 0 same-day collisions in 45 files).
