# ADR-176: The reference-product difference register

Why: the studies of 2026-10-23 found 61 gaps across builder rows 1-16, kept in private scratch files nothing checked;
the earlier prose rule had been skipped. One record per difference, gated at brief, verify and close, keeps each gap
visible until it is built or decided.
