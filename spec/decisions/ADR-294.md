# ADR-294: profit target is before charges, said out loud

Why: the owner chose the market-facing basis for the target rule on 13 Nov. The risk of that choice is a "profit"
alert on a trade that ends in a loss (strategy 143: +61.75 before, -218.85 after), so the decision requires that the
basis is named in every alert and that the after-charges result sits beside it.
