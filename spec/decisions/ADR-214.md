title: 'Adjustments live on the strategy card, the builder (adjust mode), an opportunities inbox and a My playbooks page'
decision: 'Where adjustments sit in the app. (1) A detection shows on the strategy''s own card (Live Strategies and
  Strategy-linked positions) with a "Review adjustment" action that opens a side drawer holding REQ-046 layers 1-3
  (why detected, current state, possible approaches; "No change" is always one approach). (2) Picking an approach
  opens the Strategy Builder in adjust mode with the new legs prepared: Before (dashed) and After (solid) on one payoff
  chart. (2a) One situation usually has several possible approaches; the user can compare ALL of them that apply (not a
  fixed 2-3), always including "No change", side by side on named criteria: money now (credit/debit, charges + bid-ask
  cost, margin change), risk after (max loss, loss at -2/-1/+1/+2% moves, breakevens), reward after (max profit,
  premium still left to earn, POP), Greeks after (net delta, gamma, theta, vega), and practical (adjustments used vs
  the plan's maximum, strike liquidity, "would you open this fresh"). Each criterion row marks its leader in plain
  words ("Lowest max loss", "Highest POP", "Lowest cost") and each option shows how many criteria it leads. There is
  NO overall winner, score or "better option" label; the user decides (owner answer below; ADR-003, REQ-045 AC-6,
  REQ-046 AC-3 unchanged). (2b) The user can filter the approaches in the compare. The filters are never a fixed
  list: each filter's choices and ranges are computed from this strategy's applicable approaches at that moment, so a
  different strategy or a different market state shows different values. Starting set: kind of action (roll, reduce,
  close part, convert / re-centre, exit, only the kinds present), side it touches (call, put, both), credit or debit,
  loss capped or not, and ranges for largest debit accepted, max loss at most, chance of profit at least, charges at
  most and orders to place at most, each spanning only the current approaches' own minimum and maximum. "No change"
  is never filtered out because it is the baseline. The count "Showing N of M approaches" is always visible. No separate Before/After page is built. (3) Positions > Adjustment opportunities is the inbox of
  every open opportunity across strategies, grouped (REQ-045 AC-8), with its REQ-112 outcome. (4) Strategies >
  Adjustments is "My playbooks": one editable playbook per strategy family, starting from the system default, with
  "Reset to default"; a per-strategy plan sheet (target, stop, max adjustments, time exit, optional trigger
  customisation) is pre-filled from the matching playbook when tracking starts and is editable later. Precedence:
  strategy plan > user playbook > system default (REQ-045 AC-7). Notification channels stay in Alerts > Notification
  settings; display preferences stay a per-page gear. Next step: a clickable sample UI of these four surfaces with
  example data, plus a throwaway engine check of "roll a leg -> Before/After" on a real chain, before any build.'
