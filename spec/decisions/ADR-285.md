title: 'When the market calendar runs out: a warning from 30 days before its last date, and a date it does not cover reads "no calendar", never "market closed"'
decision: >-
  Decided by the GC-ICSAFE plan review as a technical call under the owner's approval of that contract (2026-11-10),
  issue 686, finding F-103. The holiday list is dated data (ADR-119 (2)) and holds one year at a time; NSE publishes
  the next year's list in December. (1) From 30 days before the calendar's last covered date the app warns once a
  day: a log line, a banner on every screen, and one alert to the owner through the shared notifier. (2) A date after
  the last covered date is its own state, "the market calendar has no dates for <year>". It is never shown as "market
  closed" or as a holiday. (3) On such a date the app still fails closed: no rule check, no paper fill, no basket
  build; each of them says it was not done because the calendar has no dates for that year. (4) The app is NOT
  refused at start-up in December. The issue proposed that; it would stop a working app on days the calendar still
  covers, before NSE has published. (5) Loading the next year's list stays a maintainer step with the source the
  calendar file names.
status: accepted
