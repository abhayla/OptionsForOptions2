title: 'GC-ADJ3 product answers: liquidity limits flag and refuse, a card mark for opportunities, two new Compare cost rows, two stops mean whichever comes first'
decision: >-
  (1) Playbook liquidity limits (REQ-159 AC-6; finding F-78). A strike whose open interest is below the playbook's
  "Hide strikes with open interest below" value, or whose bid-ask spread is above its "% of mid" value, stays visible
  in the adjust drawer and the Compare and is flagged "Below your liquidity limit"; it is never removed silently. A
  roll whose debit is above "Most debit paid for one roll" is refused, with the reason. While no live source gives a
  strike's open interest, a live strike is shown "OI not known", never as passing. (2) Live Strategies card (REQ-162,
  REQ-112). The card shows "N adjustment opportunities", linking to that strategy's row in Positions > Adjustment
  opportunities; dismissing the row there clears the mark. (3) Compare cost line (REQ-155 AC-3, REQ-156 AC-2). Two new
  rows, "Cost as % of premium left" and "Change in max profit"; the charge breakdown (brokerage, STT, exchange, GST,
  stamp) opens on tap under the existing cost row. (4) Two stops (REQ-159 AC-6, REQ-161). When a playbook holds both
  "Stop line (% of max loss)" and "Stop line (% of margin)" and no combining rule, they mean "whichever comes first";
  the plan sheet and summary show both values. Trigger behaviour is unchanged (each stop already fires on its own).
