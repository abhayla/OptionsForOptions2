title: The action that starts following a saved strategy is called "Start tracking"
decision: 'The action on one saved strategy that records each leg at the live price and starts watching it (REQ-109)
  is labelled "Start tracking" on every screen, as ADR-165 (2) names it, not "Paper track". The figures it produces keep
  the not-real label of REQ-109 AC-2 ("Paper P&L (not real)"), so tracked money is never read as real money. REQ-109
  AC-1 is reworded to use the new label; nothing else in REQ-109 changes.'
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'refines REQ-109'
provenance:
- 'owner, 2026-10-21 ~21:25 IST, with the question tool (builder scope run 1): answered "Start tracking (Recommended)"
  over "Paper track" and "Add to Drafts", after being shown that REQ-109 AC-1 said "Paper track" while ADR-165 (2) said
  "Start tracking".'
source: 'ADR-165 (2); REQ-109 AC-1, AC-2; Sensibull study screens 090-092 (Drafts)'
consequences:
- 'Example: the user saves a NIFTY iron condor at 10:00 and presses "Start tracking"; the four legs are stored at their
  10:00 prices and the strategy shows "Paper P&L (not real): +1,240.50" later in the day.'

# ADR-166: "Start tracking" is the label

Why: two approved texts named the same action differently; the owner picked the plain words of the newer scope decision.
