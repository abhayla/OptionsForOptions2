# ADR-041: Futures leg P&L formulas

## Decision
- **Q207 = A (owner, 2026-09-29).**
  - BUY FUTURES: `(Market − Entry Price) × Quantity`
  - SELL FUTURES: `(Entry Price − Market) × Quantity`
- Expiry scenario columns: `Market` = the scenario underlying level. Live P&L: `Market` = the futures contract's LTP
  (the same entry-price / LTP split as ADR-035). No premium term: futures settle at the level.
- **Worked check.** BUY NIFTY futures at 24,000, Quantity 75: at 24,300 → `(24,300 − 24,000) × 75 = +₹22,500`; at
  23,800 → `−₹15,000`. SELL at the same prices gives `−₹22,500` and `+₹15,000`.
- Why: Futures Buy/Sell are V1 leg types (Q8); without a formula a futures leg would leave blank cells, which the
  owner ruled out (T1 #93, #95), and a futures + options hedge would hide its risk.
- Written into `spec/business-rules/scenario-calculations.md` §1–§2.
