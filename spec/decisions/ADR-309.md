  record, which happens at the first candle close beyond the re-arm band of ADR-237; the cool-down only delays when a

  close, held by quiet time or by the daily cap) is shown as "condition met, alert not raised yet" with the reason the
  server gives; it is not shown as fired and it does not flicker to quiet between polls: it leaves that state only when
  a record opens or the gate drops the pending sample. (5) Stops keep ADR-296 and the immediate raise of REQ-170 AC-5;

  forbids; it is parked. A fired stop kept from its record is worded from the record's own figures ("Stop was reached

# ADR-309: the on-screen fired state follows the alert record

Why: the alert was raised once, but the page's flag blinked with every poll. One source, the gate's record, removes
the blink and makes the page agree with the alert.
