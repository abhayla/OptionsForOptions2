# ADR-038: A referral reward is 30 days, not a calendar month

## Decision
- **Q218 = A (owner, 2026-09-29).** The owner said "one month" (T1 #189); ChatGPT's examples used 30 days (#192,
  #194); the spec mixed both. The unit is **30 days per referral**, admin-configurable in days.
- Stacking adds days to the current end date: Pro until 10 Oct + one referral = **9 Nov**; three referrals = 90 days.
- The billing screen shows the exact date ("Pro extended to 9 Nov"). No month-end rule is needed.
- Why days: pricing is already per day (₹20/day, T1 #189), stacking stays simple, no 31 Jan edge case.
- Written into ADR-025 and REQ-021.
