title: 'A breached or intrinsic-only short is never "not checked": delta 1/0 at intrinsic, and T-01 flags at or beyond the strike'
decision: >-
  Decided under the owner's standing delegation for strategy behaviour ("go with your recommendation"). (1) For rule
  checks (T-03 short delta, T-14, situation rules) an option whose price has no time value left (price within one tick
  of its intrinsic value, or IV not solvable while the price is at intrinsic) takes delta 1 when in the money and 0
  when out of the money (sign by side), instead of "delta not available"; the rule line says "delta taken as 1: the
  option trades at intrinsic value". (2) T-01 ("spot within this % of a short strike") also flags when spot is at or
  beyond the short strike (the tested short is in the money), with the wording "spot is beyond the sold 23,500 CE by
  100 points". Why: test-data run 2026-11-06 (PATH-50 frame 7, expiry day 15:25, spot 23,600, sold 23,500 CE worth
  its intrinsic 100): T-03, T-14 and S-02 answered "not available" and T-01 stayed clear (0.42 % > the last-day 0.3 %),
  so no rule warned while the short call was 100 points in the money.
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Fills the intrinsic-value gap for delta-based rules; widens T-01 to the breached case.'
