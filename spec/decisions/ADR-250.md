title: 'The ADR-247 stop default applies to new plans only; saved plans keep the alerts they already hold'
decision: >-
  Owner 2026-11-05 ~09:3x IST (question tool, "New plans only (Recommended)"), answering what happens to existing saved
  plans when the live alert check starts reading stops through the settings in force (contract "Your adjustment
  settings" step 3): a strategy plan saved before ADR-247 keeps its own stop alerts exactly as saved - its per-stage
  "% of max loss" values (the iron condor template held 50 / 50 / 40) and its "% of margin" rule if it was on - and
  does NOT gain the 2x-credit stop. Only plans created after ADR-247 start from its default (50 % of max loss or 2x
  the credit received, whichever comes first; % of margin off). The user can change either kind of plan at any time
  (ADR-245: every answer is a default, never a fixed rule). Measured case: strategy 82 (owner's live iron condor),
  5 Nov 09:2x IST, loss 51.19 % of max loss against its saved 50 % line (Early stage), P&L -Rs 14,371.50; under this
  decision its last-stage line stays 40 %, not loosened to 50 %. Why: nothing under a live position changes without
  the user choosing it. Decision support only (ADR-003).
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Narrows ADR-247 to new plans; the step 3 live-check fix must read a saved plan''s own stop values and must not add the 2x-credit stop to a pre-ADR-247 plan.'
