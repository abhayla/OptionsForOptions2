# ADR-292: a held iron condor typed in by hand

## Why
- ADR-290 says a user may type in a condor they already hold, but not how it is labelled, what its entry time means,
  where its rules start, or how broker changes reach the app. Each choice changes what the user sees, so it is
  written here before the code (spec-first R4).

## Example
- The owner sold a NIFTY 17 Nov 24000/24100 CE and 23400/23300 PE condor at his broker at 09:20 and types it in at
  09:35 with his own fill prices. The app shows "Held at my broker", entry "12 Nov 09:20", "Rules start from
  09:35". A rule already true at 09:35 reads "Already true when tracking started" and raises no alert; a rule
  that crosses at 10:05 alerts at 10:05.
