# ADR-095: Pro-rata split of the price tolerance

## Why
- Equal rupees per leg gives a Rs 2 far-OTM leg the same Rs 12.50 room as a Rs 150 leg, which is many ticks above its
  price; equal ticks ignores how much money each leg carries. Pro rata matches room to money at stake.
