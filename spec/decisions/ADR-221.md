title: 'T-19 compares premium left with the cheapest adjustment the playbook offers at that check'
decision: >-
  The T-19 trigger ("premium still left to earn is below N x the adjustment's cost") uses, at each check, the cost of
  the CHEAPEST adjustment approach the strategy's playbook offers for the current stage, priced by the engine exactly as
  the Compare table's "This adjustment" cost (the adjustment's own charges plus the bid-ask cost, REQ-155 AC-3,
  REQ-156 AC-2). The flag names that approach and shows that cost, so the alert and the Compare show the same figure.
  When no approach of the stage can be priced, T-19 is not checked, with the reason.
status: accepted
questions: []
changes: 'Settles the gap in REQ-159 "How the open triggers are measured" (which approach''s cost counts); replaces the
  built checker''s full-spread + Rs 40 per leg figure (finding F-77).'
provenance:
- 'owner, 2026-10-31 ~21:40 IST, question tool: "Cheapest adjustment (Recommended)" over "Cost to close all" and
  "Playbook''s first choice". Asked while scoping GC-ADJ3 after the gap check found F-77.'
source: 'spec/requirements/REQ-159.md; spec/findings.md F-77; catalogue P-04 (do not pay more to adjust than is left to
  earn).'
consequences:
- 'Each check prices every approach of the stage once (engine time measured 2026-10-30: 7 calls 0.27 s total).'
- 'One engine function owns the cost; the checker and the Compare both call it (ADR-008).'
