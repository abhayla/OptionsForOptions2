# ADR-003: Decision-support positioning and product language

## Owner answers
- **Q4 = B** (T1 #9): guided strategy discovery — the platform shows strategies that match the user's inputs and
  explains why; it never says "this is the strategy you should trade"; no AI trading advisor in V1.
- The continuation prompt the owner wrote for later sessions (T2 #1) fixes the wording lists below.

## Rules
- Preferred: *Strategies you could consider · Suggested setup · Adjustment opportunity detected · Possible adjustment
  · Possible approaches · Review adjustment · Your rule was triggered*.
- Forbidden: *You should take this trade · This is the best trade · Best adjustment · Recommended trade · Guaranteed
  · Risk-free · Certain profit*, and any promise of returns or of reduced losses (T1 #74).
- Adjustments are never described as reducing risk by default; they can increase it (T1 #176).
- Not carried: *"What should I do now?"* (proposed by ChatGPT in T1 #42 and "Suggested action: Move Call Spread"
  in T1 #28) — both clash with these rules and with Q159.
- Legal/SEBI review is required for personalized adjustment logic, advisory-like wording and notification content
  (open-questions Q211).

## Owner decision (2026-09-29 morning, Q226): how strict the wording check is
- **Q226 = strict with reviewed exceptions** (owner, this session, issue #30): every platform message comes from a
  fixed, reviewed template catalogue with typed slots. Templates may not contain the bare words "best", "sure",
  "safe", "guarantee*" or "recommend*" (in addition to the Forbidden phrases above). Named exceptions, reviewed once:
  "best bid", "best ask", "best-case", "make sure"; a new exception needs its own review. Zerodha's or the user's own
  text is only quoted, word for word, in a labelled field and is never part of a template. This is stricter than the
  phrase list above on purpose (single words, not only phrases).
- **Q230 (owner, 2026-09-29, after W-024 round 5) — Q226 made exact:** the ban covers every word form of the five
  words (e.g. "best", "safest", "safely", "safer", "surely", "guaranteed", "recommended", "recommendation"). "must",
  "have to" and "ought to" are NOT banned (a platform instruction such as "You must reconnect Zerodha" is allowed). The
  four exceptions match only as spelled ("best bid", "best ask", "best-case", "make sure"); "best case" with a space
  is not an exception. The checks run inside the one function that builds a message, so no construction route skips
  them.
- **Q231 (owner, 2026-09-29): "safety" is a reviewed exception** to the Q230 word-form ban, because it names a check
  the platform runs (REQ-059 "Pre-execution safety gate"), not a promise about a trade. Allowed: "safety", "safety
  check", "safety checks", "safety gate". Still banned: "safe", "safer", "safest", "safely", "safeguard" and every
  other word form of the five words.
- **Q235 (owner, 2026-09-29, after W-024 round 6) — what the wording check must withstand:** the same trust boundary
  as order safety (W-026): the checks stop accidental misuse by the platform's own code — a template, slot value,
  construction route or future module that carries forbidden wording — and are flagged in CI when code reaches into
  their internals. They are not required to resist code in the same process that deliberately replaces the checker at
  runtime (e.g. reassigning a module's check function); that is out of scope, and the CI source scan flags it.
- **Promise phrases are part of the Forbidden list** (they were missing from the checker on main): at least "assured
  return(s)", "cannot lose", "can't lose", "no loss", "minimise/minimize losses", "reduce(s) your losses", "zero
  risk", "no risk", "certain profit", "guaranteed profit", in every word form — per "any promise of returns or of
  reduced losses" above.
