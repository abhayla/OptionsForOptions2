# ADR-003: Decision-support positioning and product language

## Decision
- Positioning: decision-support and strategy tooling. **Not** individualized investment advice, not an AI advisory
  chatbot.
- Never promise: guaranteed returns, guaranteed loss reduction, profitability, certainty about market movement.
- Preferred wording: *Strategies you could consider · Suggested setup · Adjustment opportunity detected · Possible
  approaches · Review adjustment · Your rule was triggered*.
- Forbidden wording: *You should take this trade · Best trade · Best adjustment · Guaranteed · Risk-free · Certain
  profit · You should do this*.
- Adjustments can increase risk; the product never says otherwise.

## Legal review required (handoff §2, §70, Q158)
Personalized adjustment logic, advisory-like language, notification content. See `spec/open-questions.md` Q211.
