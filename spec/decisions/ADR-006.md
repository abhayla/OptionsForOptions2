# ADR-006: Generic strategy engine with templates and custom strategies

## Decision
- One generic engine over the six leg types; unlimited combinations; multi-leg; multi-expiry.
- Represents custom combinations, modifications, adjustment proposals, exits and execution plans.
- Named templates (Iron Condor, spreads, …) are data above the engine, never separate engines.
- Unmatched combinations are valid as **Custom Strategy**.
