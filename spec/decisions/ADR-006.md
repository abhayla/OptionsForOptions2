# ADR-006: Generic strategy engine, templates and multi-expiry

## Owner answers
- **Q8** (T1 #16–#17): no separate menu of 30 hard-coded strategies — *Strategy Templates* (e.g. Bull Put Spread,
  Bear Call Spread, Iron Condor, Iron Fly, Calendar Spread, Straddle, Strangle, covered strategies, Custom) + *Manual
  Builder*, all built from the same six instruments/actions. Beginner: pick a strategy → answer simple questions →
  system builds it; Advanced: add legs manually.
- **Q40 = B** (T1 #131): a strategy may hold **several expiries**; each leg has its own expiry; payoff, margin, P&L,
  Greeks and execution engines support per-leg expiry. Guided mode can keep calendar/diagonal structures out of the
  way.
- **Q52 = A** (T1 #161): unmatched combinations are a **Custom Strategy** with full analytics; the platform may say
  "This resembles a Bull Put Spread with an additional Call" but never forces a template.
