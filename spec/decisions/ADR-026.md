# ADR-026: Paid Pro: price, annual plan, Razorpay and admin commercial settings

## Owner answers
- Owner (T1 #189): ₹20 per day, monthly and yearly payment, yearly discounted, **everything configurable in Admin**.
  Direct qualifying customers never pay: *"will always get Pro free all the time"* (T1 #191) and *"they will not have
  anything to pay for … the button should be disabled"* (T1 #193); see ADR-024 Q62.
- **Q64 = A** (T1 #197): **₹600/month** billed per month (not ₹20 × remaining days) + a discounted annual plan.
- **Q65** (T1 #199): ChatGPT's ₹5,999/year was **rejected**; the annual price and discount are **set by Admin**.
- **Q66 = A** (T1 #201): **Razorpay** for V1, behind a replaceable payment layer; no second provider.
- Admin settings (ChatGPT's list, T1 #190): daily reference rate, monthly price, annual price, discount, trial duration,
  referral reward, stacking, promotions, currency, GST/tax handling, future plans.
- **Not configurable in V1** (the owner's later answers win over the T1 #190 list): **grace period** — V1: no grace
  period (Q90 = A, T1 #253–#254; ChatGPT: "The Admin can still have configurable commercial settings later if we
  decide to add one"); **what expired users keep** — fixed by Q67/Q89 = A (Limited/Read-Only, and monitoring of
  active strategies is never removed on expiry, T1 #251–#252).
