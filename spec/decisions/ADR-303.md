  Owner decision 2026-11-13 ~18:50-19:00 IST (question tool, picked "Paid in booked, close cost in unbooked
  (Recommended)"). (1) Booked P&L after charges = booked profit minus ALL charges already paid. (2) Unbooked P&L after
  charges = open-leg profit minus the estimated charges to close what is open. (3) Booked + Unbooked = Total P&L after
  charges, exactly. (4) Cost, said plainly (the owner saw it in the option text): while a trade is open, the entry
  charges of the still-open legs sit in Booked, so Booked looks negative early in a trade. Every figure says "(after
  charges)" (ADR-263).

# ADR-303: where charges go between booked and unbooked

Why: ADR-263 put all three figures after charges but did not say how the charges are split; without a rule the three
could stop adding up. The owner chose paid-in-booked, close-cost-in-unbooked on 13 Nov.
