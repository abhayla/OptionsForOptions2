# ADR-082: Charges from Zerodha's published rates; money sentences include them

## Why
- The owner chose Zerodha's published rates as the source (owner, Q267); F-35 shows they reproduce Zerodha's own
  order-level reply to 4 decimals on a real contract, and they work without a login (drafts).
- A max loss stated before charges understates the real loss (about 2.5% on the 1-lot condor); ADR-071 was written as
  a holding phrase until this decision.

## Alternatives not taken
- Zerodha's API charges reply as the source: exact, but only with a session; drafts would show no charges.
- Keep "before charges" with one charges line underneath: headline max loss understated.
