# W-072: Contract names, IST times and four-part errors on the Builder screen

- **Core / Proof:** see frontmatter.
- **Spec basis:** ADR-086 (owner, 2026-10-15: "legs shown by contract (e.g. \"NIFTY 19 Oct 23200 CE\") not raw ids;
  human-readable times not ISO strings; fix the broken \"This request.\" message"); REQ-039 AC-3 ("Every important
  non-normal state shows what happened, when, what is blocked and the next action"); REQ-065 AC-2 (four parts; that
  requirement is blocked by W-070, so its evidence is not claimed here); ADR-008 (money stays the API's display string).
- **Found:** issue #232 items 2-4, from the 4a gate screenshots of 2026-10-15 11:59 IST.
- **Contract-name tests** (ADR-086 item 1) sit in `display-format.test.js` and the e2e spec; no Approved requirement AC
  carries leg naming yet, so they are checked by the verifier under ADR-086, not claimed as AC evidence.
- **Card correction (class 1, 2026-10-15):** the brief said outcome-answer legs lack contract fields; `LegOut` has
  `instrument`, `strike`, `expiry` and the answer has `underlying`, so a draft-URL leg is named from the answer.
  But the NOT_CONNECTED and not-live answers fill those fields with null (`backend/ofo/outcome/service.py:155-171`), so
  in those two states a draft-URL leg reads "Contract details not available" (honest, never the id). Filling them
  from the catalogue is backend work, moved to W-073 item 5.
- **Not in this item:** #232 item 1 (leg picker error on the live screen) - re-checked in the 4a gate re-run on
  current main (#212 fixed the proxy cause); template wording (owner-approved, W-024).
