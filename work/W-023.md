# W-023: Partial execution handling, no automatic retry

When some legs fill and others fail: an explicit exception state with risk/P&L/margin recalculated from the broker's real positions (engine); the user choices in order (Complete Strategy, Retry Failed Leg, Review Manually, Close Partial Strategy); executed legs never unwound automatically; completing re-fetches positions and order status (interfaces), recomputes the remaining strategy, re-checks margin and submits only the missing order(s); never 'complete' because all orders were submitted; no automatic resubmission.

- **Core:** After the golden Iron Condor fills three legs and the 23,600 CE purchase fails, the platform shows the real three-leg risk (unlimited upside) and the four choices, and 'Complete Strategy' prepares only the missing 23,600 CE order after re-checking positions.
- **Proof:** tests/execution/test_partial.py drives that exact case with a fake broker interface and asserts the recomputed metrics, the choice order and the single prepared order.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-058 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
