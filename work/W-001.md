# W-001: Calculation engine core: leg P&L formulas, strategy sum, breakevens, max profit/loss

Build the one calculation engine's core (pure Python, Decimal money, stdlib only): expiry and live P&L for all six leg types, strategy aggregation, and exact breakevens / max profit / max loss for single-expiry strategies.

- **Core:** The engine reproduces the spec's golden Iron Condor exactly from its legs (scenario-calculations.md §6).
- **Proof:** tests/engine/test_golden_iron_condor.py computes every §6 number (4 live P&Ls, strategy +₹1,365.00, max profit ₹6,825, max loss ₹8,175, breakevens 22,909 / 23,491, every listed expiry P&L level and per-leg sample) from the four legs and asserts exact Decimal equality.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-033 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
