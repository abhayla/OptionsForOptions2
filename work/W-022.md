# W-022: Multi-leg execution plan: dependencies, protective-first order, dependent-leg stop

An execution plan builder: leg dependencies (a sold leg depends on the bought leg that protects it), an order sequence by protection, margin impact, dependencies and broker constraints (protective legs before the sells that depend on them, never a hard-coded 'all buys first'); if a protective leg fails, the dependent sell is not submitted; an execution-review summary (strategy, margin, max loss, max profit, current P&L, leg count, sequence, broker) from the engine.

- **Core:** For the golden Iron Condor the plan orders each bought wing before the sold leg it protects, and a simulated failure of the 22,800 PE purchase stops the 23,000 PE sale while the call side proceeds.
- **Proof:** tests/execution/test_plan.py builds the plan for the golden Iron Condor and a ratio spread, simulates leg failures, and asserts the submitted set.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-056 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
