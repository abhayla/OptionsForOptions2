# W-018: Normalized market data model and data health

A normalized quote model (every AC-1 field, Decimal prices, tz-aware timestamp, source metadata) with a per-quote health state (available, stale, delayed, unhealthy, unavailable) derived from explicit, configurable freshness rules; per-strategy monitoring availability computed from the health of the quotes each strategy needs; a 'Live market data disconnected. Last updated: <time>. Live strategy monitoring is paused.' status for affected strategies; health carried into rule trigger records.

- **Core:** A quote older than the freshness limit is 'stale', and a strategy that needs it becomes 'monitoring paused' while another strategy on healthy quotes stays active.
- **Proof:** tests/marketdata/test_health.py builds NIFTY and SENSEX quotes at controlled timestamps and asserts each health state at its boundary and the per-strategy availability split (NIFTY active, SENSEX paused).
- **Spec basis:** REQ-049 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
