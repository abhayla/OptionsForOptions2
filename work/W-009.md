# W-009: Referral rewards and confirmed-openings CSV import

Referral rewards as entitlement events (30 days each, admin-configurable, stacking on by default) and the admin CSV import of confirmed Zerodha openings with matching, review list for unmatched/duplicates, and idempotency (ADR-044).

- **Core:** Importing a confirmed-openings CSV credits exactly the matched referrers once, even when the same file is imported twice.
- **Proof:** tests/referrals/test_import.py imports a 12-row CSV (10 matched, 1 unmatched, 1 duplicate) twice and asserts 10 events, 2 review rows, 0 new events on the second import; Pro until 10 Oct + one referral = 9 Nov.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-021 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
