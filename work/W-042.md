# W-042: Catalogue update never drops an unexpired contract

Addresses item 2 of #10.

- **Core:** Catalogue.update() refuses a new instrument list that drops a single not-yet-expired contract, and accepts a list whose only removals are expired contracts.
- **Proof:** real-fixture tests (a) one live row removed -> refused; (b) 75% NIFTY truncation -> refused; (c) expired-expiry roll-off -> accepted.
- **Spec basis:** OWNER DECISION Q244 (REQ-053): "A daily catalogue update is refused if it would remove any contract whose expiry has not yet passed (at the time of the update). Normal roll-off of expired contracts and the addition of new expiries are accepted. Replaces the W-006 per-index "lose at most 50% of contracts" rule".
