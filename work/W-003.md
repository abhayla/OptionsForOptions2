# W-003: Scenario levels: per-index step, anchored grid, inserted current and breakeven columns, At Expiry / Estimated Now

Generate the scenario level set for a strategy from engine output: admin-configurable step per index (NIFTY 100, SENSEX 300, ADR-042), default range from spot/strikes/breakevens, grid anchored to rounded levels, current level and 0-P&L levels inserted at their price positions, and both At Expiry and Estimated Now values.

- **Core:** For the golden Iron Condor at spot 23,047 the level set is ... 22,900 | 22,909 (0 P&L) | 23,000 | 23,047 CURRENT | 23,100 ... with every value from the engine.
- **Proof:** tests/scenario/test_levels.py builds the golden Iron Condor's levels and asserts the exact inserted columns from scenario-calculations.md §4 and the P&L at each level from §6.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-034 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
