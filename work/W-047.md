# W-047: Strategy library: level, market view and objective per template

Every strategy template carries one level, one or more market views and one or more objectives (closed lists, refused at load).

- **Core:** Every strategy template carries exactly one level plus at least one market view and at least one objective, per Q251; the loader refuses a template missing any of them.
- **Proof:** tests/strategy/test_library.py checks every real template's level against a table derived from the Q251 rule text; deleting a level makes the loader raise; red first on origin/main.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-068 AC-2 (all strategies "labelled Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced") and the owner decision of 2026-09-29 (Q251): "Beginner: long call, long put, bull call / bull put / bear call / bear put vertical spreads. Intermediate: Iron Condor, Iron Butterfly, short strangle, short straddle, covered call, cash-secured put. Advanced: calendars, diagonals, ratio spreads, Jade Lizard, broken-wing butterfly, and any strategy with naked short legs beyond a short straddle/strangle."
