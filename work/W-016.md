# W-016: Builder activity history, undo and restore

Activity history for the Builder: only meaningful changes (add/remove leg, strike, quantity, expiry, material risk/payoff change) create entries with a label; choosing an alternative setup keeps the previous one; restore preserves the current configuration first; undo right after a meaningful change (about 5 s window is a UI concern, the model offers undo of the last change); history persists after execution.

- **Core:** Changing a strike creates one labelled history entry, a cosmetic change creates none, and restoring an earlier entry first preserves the current configuration.
- **Proof:** tests/strategy/test_builder_history.py drives the golden Iron Condor through strike/quantity/leg edits and cosmetic edits and asserts the exact entry labels and restore behaviour.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-070 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
