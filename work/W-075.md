# W-075: Charges in money sentences and expiry values (ADR-082 part 2)

- **Core / Proof:** see frontmatter.
- **Spec basis:** ADR-082 decision (2) and its real case (max loss Rs 8,635.25 before charges, about Rs 221 round
  trip); ADR-071 (the phrase this replaces); REQ-034 AC-7; ADR-008 (one engine, exact Decimal).
- **Decisions by role (product owner, owner delegation 2026-10-02):** (a) "round-trip at planned prices" = each leg's
  entry order plus the opposite-side exit order of the same quantity, both at the leg's planned price - this reproduces
  ADR-082's worked "about Rs 221"; (b) when no rate set is stored, the sentence keeps ADR-071's approved "before charges
  and taxes" wording (honest, no invented text) and the existing CHARGES_UNAVAILABLE flag is raised.
- **Not in this item:** contract-note rounding (unmeasured until the 4b order, F-35); the review screen's charges line.
