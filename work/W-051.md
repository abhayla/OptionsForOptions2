# W-051: Platform base and the trusted database clock

- **Core:** the database stamps `recorded_at` and enforces the 60 s skew window; the application role cannot alter rows
  or switch the guard off.
- **Proof:** (a)-(d) above, as the non-superuser role, on real PostgreSQL; mutation tests on the trigger and the grants.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-064 AC-2 ("Audit and timeline records are append-only."); ADR-023 Q225 clarification ("\"recorded
  at\" is stamped by the ledger from its own clock; a caller can never supply it. Every new event's own date
  (granted/effective) must lie within the clock-skew window of that stamp, both ways."); ADR-023 Q256 (60 seconds);
  ADR-046 (app-tests.yml, path-filtered); ADR-048 (test database, role caps); ADR-047 (copy first).
- **Copy from:** `spec/technical-design/legacy-reuse.md` M1: `app/database.py` (ADAPT), `server_default=func.now()`
  convention, `app/config.py` pattern, `routes/health.py`, conftest `client` fixture pattern, `pytest.ini` asyncio
  settings, `backend-tests.yml` services, pins from `requirements.txt`, `scripts/generate_openapi.py`,
  `.github/scripts/alembic-migration-guard.py`, `.pre-commit-config.yaml`. New: async Alembic env, baseline migration,
  the trigger and grants, a generic error handler.
- **Layout:** `backend/ofo/` stays standard-library and never imports `ofo_app`; app tests live in `tests_app/` with their
  own `pytest-app.ini`, so the kit CI (`tests/`) is unchanged.
