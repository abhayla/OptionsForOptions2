# W-028: Complete and Retry follow the full execution plan (freeze slices, margin, lot-aligned slices)

Deferred #43: partial execution's Complete and Retry use the same MarginPlanner and BrokerConstraints as the W-022 plan (freeze-limit slices, protective slices first, batches within a step); every leg and slice is a whole number of lots from the instrument catalogue; a failed protective slice counts as a failed protective leg so its dependents are withheld.

- **Core:** With a freeze limit below the missing 23,600 CE quantity, Complete prepares lot-aligned slices of that leg and nothing else, and a refused first slice withholds any sell that depends on it.
- **Proof:** tests/execution/test_complete_slices.py drives the golden condor with the 23,600 CE missing, a fake BrokerConstraints with a small freeze limit and the real catalogue fixture, and asserts the prepared slices (count, quantities as multiples of the lot, order) and the withholding.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-058; follow-up of a verifier finding (deferred issue), overnight 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
