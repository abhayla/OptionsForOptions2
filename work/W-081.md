# W-081: Margin check before the basket (REQ-055)

- **Core / Proof:** see frontmatter.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-055 AC-1, AC-3, AC-4; ADR-016 (Zerodha is the authority; margin is a hard execution gate);
  ADR-054 (no order sent by our server).
- **Decision by role (market expert, owner delegation 2026-10-08 for market forks):** the required margin is the reply's
  `final.total` (after the spread/hedge benefit Zerodha gives a basket), not `initial.total` - the 15 Oct reply shows
  Rs 68,498.85 vs Rs 2,96,332.80 for the same 4 legs; using `initial` would block hedged strategies that Zerodha accepts.
- **Unproven on real data:** available funds - the `/user/margins` capture holds field types only (values withheld for
  privacy); `equity.net` is used as available funds per Kite's documented meaning; proven live on 2026-10-16.
