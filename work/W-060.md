# W-060: Index spot, its health, and the parity forward per expiry

- **Core / Proof:** see frontmatter. The expected values were computed by the orchestrator's independent proof code
  (docs/research/kite-proof-2026-10-07/kite_live_checks.py parser) from the same bytes.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-072 AC-1 (NSE_INDEX and BSE_INDEX for the two index rows only; identity (segment, exchange
  token); never mixed with Zerodha's NSE cash rows that share numbers, F-10), AC-2 (same health states as options;
  never used silently when stale), AC-3 (as changed by ADR-061: spot for the CURRENT column and range; the parity
  forward for IV, Greeks and Estimated Now; fallback labelled "estimated from spot"); ADR-061; F-32.
- **Measured robustness (2026-10-08, orchestrator):** on SENSEX 15-Oct at 09:20, 2 of 21 strikes had wide quotes and
  sat up to 132 points off; the median moved 0.04 points when strikes with a quote wider than 5% of its mid were
  dropped (72,452.96 vs 72,453.00). So ADR-061's median is kept as written; the filtered spread (9.98 points here,
  192.61 unfiltered) is stored only as a quality figure.
- **Copy from:** none - the parity forward is new (algochanakya prices from spot); index identity follows W-056/W-057.
