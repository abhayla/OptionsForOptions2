# W-027: Strategy modification proposals

Modify Strategy builds a proposal of leg changes against the active version; a leg is never traded as an unrelated standalone trade (AC-1); the proposal recalculates max profit/loss, breakevens, current P&L, margin, charges and risk through the engine and the margin/charges interfaces (AC-2); adjustment orders are prepared only after the user confirms, and execution goes through the pre-execution gate (AC-4); every modification creates a new strategy version and old versions are kept (AC-5, on the merged StrategyRecord). The Before/After screen (AC-3) is UI work; the before/after data it needs is returned by the proposal.

- **Core:** A proposal that rolls the golden Iron Condor's sold 23,000 PE to 22,900 PE returns engine-recalculated metrics for before and after, creates version 2 only on confirmation, and keeps version 1 unchanged and active until execution.
- **Proof:** tests/strategy/test_modification.py drives the roll on the merged StrategyRecord with fake margin/charges interfaces and asserts the recalculated values (hand-computed from scenario-calculations.md), the version numbers and that nothing is prepared before confirmation.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-037 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045 (fourth set).
