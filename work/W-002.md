# W-002: Engine inputs, Decimal policy, Black-Scholes IV/Greeks, display precision, margin/charges interfaces

Extend the engine with its full input model, Black-Scholes pricing, implied volatility and Greeks (stdlib math, Decimal at the boundary), display formatting without false precision, and margin/charges interfaces with no Zerodha dependency.

- **Core:** Black-Scholes price -> implied volatility -> price round-trips on a published reference case.
- **Proof:** Hull's textbook reference (S=42, K=40, r=10%, sigma=20%, T=0.5: call 4.76, put 0.81) reproduced to the paisa; IV recovered within 1e-6 from the unrounded model price (internal solver), and the public solver round-trips the quoted 4.76 / 0.81 back to themselves.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-032 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
