# W-006: Instrument catalogue from Zerodha's public instrument list

Parse Zerodha's public instrument list (no login) into a contract catalogue for NIFTY (NFO) and SENSEX (BFO) options and futures, kept separate from eligibility, as the only source of lot size and strike gap. Adapt algochanakya services/instrument_master.py with provenance.

- **Core:** Parsing the real Zerodha instrument list yields NIFTY strike gap 50 / lot 65 and SENSEX strike gap 100 / lot 20.
- **Proof:** A real slice of api.kite.trade/instruments captured 2026-09-29 is stored as a test fixture; tests/instruments/test_catalogue.py parses it and asserts the measured gaps and lot sizes.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-053 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
