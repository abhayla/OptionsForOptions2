# W-007: Entitlement engine: access derived from auditable entitlement events

Pure domain model: entitlement events (Trial, Direct Zerodha Customer, Referral, Paid Monthly, Paid Annual) with source/start/expiry/status/reference/audit; access at a point in time is derived, never a stored free/paid flag; combinations (stacking) and independence from strategy/broker state.

- **Core:** Given a user's entitlement events, access(t) is computed correctly at every boundary (trial end, stacked referral end, paid end).
- **Proof:** tests/entitlements/test_engine.py replays the spec's real examples (trial 7 days from registration; Pro until 10 Oct + referral -> 9 Nov) and asserts access at each boundary second; mutation tests on the boundary comparison.
- **Spec basis:** REQ-017 and the decisions it cites; approved 2026-09-29 under ADR-045.
