- The equality re-check under the lock needs a case only it catches: an ADOPTED holding recorded between the gate's read
  and the lock changes neither the plan number, the open-plan check nor the tags, yet the strategy then holds units the gate
  judged it did not (Execute would prepare an ENTRY while the strategy holds 65 adopted units; Return would close plan 1 and
  store entry facts as a first fill although the strategy was entered before, ADR-276). Test idea: during the waits call
  `order_book_store.record_adopted`, expect 409 and nothing stored. Latent today (no route calls `record_adopted`).
- The strategy revision (W-151) is read before every wait and not again under the lock; a save from another tab can land
  in between and the plan is recorded with `strategy_version` from the stale load. Fix: read the revision again under the
  lock and refuse `execute_plan_changed` if it differs from the press's revision.
- Held in my review: two concurrent Executes (open-plan check, number check, record_plan's guards), a Return racing an
  Execute or Return (no double close, no `nothing_placed` from a book older than the plan), `release_connection` commits
  only reads. Limit: the tests interleave on one connection (they prove a commit before each broker request, not a real
  pool hand-back).
