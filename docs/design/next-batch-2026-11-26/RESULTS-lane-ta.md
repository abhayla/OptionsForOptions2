The three leg-stop decisions (I-63, a leg stop on the Stops panel). The owner is asleep: for each, the spec-conformant choice is the one in force today and is what the tests pin; any change needs an owner decision row, and the words below are "pending owner read".
1. **May a leg stop sit on the profit side?** (A level BELOW the price of a SOLD leg, e.g. "below 35.00" with the price at 39.15.) Spec found: ADR-207 (5) "a per-leg stop (a leg's LTP crosses a set price and that leg exits)": no direction is restricted; ADR-327 (4) refuses only a level the live price has already crossed; ADR-330 (6) "informs only and never blocks the save"; REQ-109 AC-8. Spec-conformant choice and my recommendation: KEEP saving it (the spec says the warning never blocks and the product has no stop-side rule), but say which side it is on (decision 2). Why: refusing would need a new rule the owner has not given, and a profit-side exit (take profit on a leg) is a legitimate use. Risk of keeping it: a user who types a profit-side level believing it protects them.
2. **What does the risk line say for a stop that can only fire when the trade is winning?** Spec found: ADR-330 (6) S-22: "If this stop fires, max loss becomes Rs X (now Rs Y)", from the one engine; the route (`leg-stop-risk`) takes no direction. Today it reads "max loss becomes Rs 572.00 (now Rs 11,297.00)" for "below 35.00": true IF it fires, but it reads like protection. Recommendation (a SPEC CHANGE: needs a decision row and the owner's read of the words): when the level is on the profit side, add one line "This stop is on the profit side: it fires when this leg gains, not when it loses." and keep the figure. Why: the line stays true and the side is no longer hidden. Not chosen: hiding the figure.
3. **A level saved with the market closed that is already beyond the price.** Spec found: ADR-327 (3) "Market closed: unchanged from ADR-317 (2), the trigger is saved and arms at the next live tick"; (4) a level the live price has already crossed is refused (market hours only). Today "below 135.00" on a sold leg at 39.15 saves on a Saturday and fires at the first live tick on Monday: the leg exits at the open. Spec-conformant choice: KEEP (the spec says closed saves). Recommendation: when the market is closed, compare with the LAST price (the app already holds the last close, ADR-200) and warn "At the open this stop may fire at once: the last price is already beyond this level", without refusing. Why: it uses data the app already holds, changes no save rule, and removes the surprise at 09:15. Needs a decision row.
