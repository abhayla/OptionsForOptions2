# UI study: Sensibull layout for every OFO2 screen, under our brand (2026-10-26)

Decision built to: ADR-193 (Sensibull layout and interactions for every screen the spec has; our name, logo, GLOBAL.md
section 3 style and ADR-003 wording; ADR-152 exceptions still win). Extends ADR-173 from behaviour to layout.

Sources (read for this study):
- Sensibull study of 2026-10-21, private folder `competitors/sensibull/2026-10-21/` (INDEX.md, catalogue-raw.md and the
  screenshots). Screens are cited by their study number only ("S-012" = screenshot 012). No image and no account data
  from that folder is copied here.
- OFO2 screens captured 2026-10-26 on closing prices, 1536 px wide (owner's scratch folder, "ours/01..16" and three
  builder captures with four legs, cited as "O-B1..O-B3").
- OFO2 frontend code on main @ 24775939 (`frontend/src/pages`, `components/strategy`, `router/nav.js`, `style.css`).
- Walkthrough issues: `docs/process/walkthrough-2026-10-26.md` (I-4, I-7..I-11).

How to read the measurements: Sensibull screenshots S-012..S-026, S-032, S-060..S-064 were taken at about 67 % browser
zoom (the 1488 px wide work area of S-107 shows as about 1000 px in S-012). Sizes below are converted to 100 % zoom and
marked "approx. from S-NNN". Colours read from a screenshot are marked the same way; every final colour below is OUR
token, not Sensibull's.

Tags on every gap item:
- **[layout]** layout only: the data is already on the page.
- **[existing data]** the field exists in an API reply today but the screen does not show it (named).
- **[new data: REQ-NNN]** needs data the server does not send yet; the REQ is named where one exists.

Safety rules kept over Sensibull (ADR-152 exceptions, ADR-002, ADR-016..019, ADR-055, ADR-165):
- Sensibull lets an unticked leg change "Trade All" into "Trade 3" (S-111) and sends a partial basket. OFO2 keeps the
  tick as "left out of the numbers" only; no partial basket button exists (REQ-076 AC-7, ADR-165).
- Sensibull shows a margin shortfall only inside the basket with a red icon (S-039, S-040). OFO2 shows it in the
  builder summary and blocks execution (REQ-055).
- Sensibull per-leg BUY/SELL send buttons inside a review sheet (S-040, S-041) caused a real order during the study.
  OFO2 has no per-leg send button anywhere.
- Sensibull's warning text "Please place a stop loss" (S-234) and "Find the Most Profitable..." are advice. OFO2 states
  the measured fact only (ADR-003, REQ-085).

## a. Design system

### a.1 App shell

Sensibull: S-001, S-002, S-003, S-004, S-012, S-107. Top bar 63 px high (approx. from S-107), white, 1 px bottom
border; logo + "/ <page name>" breadcrumb on the left; section menu centred; avatar + caret on the right; the active
section is underlined in the accent colour (3 px bar, approx. from S-107). Trade and Analyse open full-width mega
menus with three columns of items, each item = icon + title + one-line description (S-002, S-003).

OFO2 today (ours/01..14): top bar 49 px, logo text "OptionsForOptions", seven plain text links, "You" avatar; every page
sits in a 992 px centred column (x 272..1264 at 1536 px); only the Home link shows an active state (ours/02).

Shell spec:

```
| 16 |  Logo  / Page name      Home  Strategies v  Positions v  Market v  Orders v  Alerts v  Learn v      (bell) (avatar v) | 16 |
                                ^ active section: accent text + 3px accent underline across the item width
height 56 px, white, 1px bottom border --line; sticky
```

- UI-001 [layout] Top bar 56 px, sticky, white, 1 px `--line` bottom border; logo (our mark + "OptionsForOptions") then
  "/" then the page title in 15 px/500 `--ink-muted` (the breadcrumb pattern of S-012). Section links centred, 15 px/500,
  `--ink`; active = `--accent` text + 3 px `--accent` underline flush with the bar's bottom edge (S-107).
- UI-002 [layout] Every section with items opens a mega menu on click (and on hover after 150 ms): full-width white
  panel, 1 px bottom border, soft shadow `0 8px 24px rgba(27,31,36,.08)`, items in up to three columns, each item =
  20 px line icon + title (15 px/500) + one-line description (13 px `--ink-muted`), as S-002/S-003. The seven sections
  and their items stay REQ-009's words (AC-1..AC-7); only the presentation changes. Mapping:
  - **Strategies** (Sensibull "Trade", S-002): column 1 "Build" = Strategy Builder ("Build a strategy from legs or
    ready-made shapes and see every outcome"), Create Strategy ("Start guided or from scratch"); column 2 "Follow" = My
    Strategies, Live Strategies, Adjustments; column 3 "Done" = Completed Strategies.
  - **Market** (Sensibull "Analyse", S-003): Option Chain, Underlying/index view, Market context.
  - **Positions**, **Orders**, **Alerts**, **Learn**, **Home**: a small dropdown (single column, same item style) of
    REQ-009's items; clicking the section name itself opens its first item (Sensibull's Positions/Orders are plain
    links, S-012; ours keep the dropdown because REQ-009 gives them items).
  - Alerts also shows a bell icon left of the avatar with a count badge (accent fill, white 11 px text) when the server
    reports active alerts **[new data: REQ-042]**; without that field the bell shows no badge.
- UI-003 [layout] Avatar menu (S-004): 36 px circle (initials), caret; dropdown 240 px wide, items = 16 px icon + label
  14 px, 40 px rows; content = REQ-009 AC-8's ten Account & Settings items, a divider, "Log out". Sensibull's FAQ / Help /
  What's New are REQ-095 (Draft): not added.
- UI-004 [layout] Page width: the Strategy Builder and Option Chain use the full width with 16 px side gutters (Sensibull
  S-107: left column starts at x 16); every other page uses a centred 1200 px column (Sensibull centres Positions,
  Orders and Settings at about 1000-1100 px, approx. from S-050, S-171, S-167). Page background `--bg`.
- UI-005 [layout] Page title row under the bar on non-builder pages: breadcrumb 12 px `--ink-subtle` ("Market"), title
  22 px/600, optional one-line description 14 px `--ink-muted`. The builder has NO page title block (Sensibull puts the
  title in the top bar breadcrumb, S-012); the planning disclaimer moves to the builder's Important-info card (UI-150).

### a.4 Spacing, cards, tables

- UI-008 [layout] Spacing scale 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 px. Column gap 16 px (S-107: left column ends x 588, right
  starts x 604). Card padding 16 px (S-107: "New Strategy" at x 33 inside a card starting x 16).
- UI-009 [layout] Card: `--surface`, 1 px `--line` border, radius 8 px, no shadow (GLOBAL.md data-dense reference:
  "zero decorative gradients/shadows"; Sensibull cards are flat with a thin border, S-107). Drawers and menus are the
  only elements with a shadow. Card header row = title left, actions right, 48 px high.
- UI-010 [layout] Tables: header row 36 px, `--surface-muted` background, 13 px/500 `--ink-subtle`; body rows 40 px
  (builder leg rows 36 px, approx. from S-107; dense tables 28 px, approx. from S-060 at 100 % zoom); 1 px `--line`
  between rows; numbers right-aligned with tabular figures; text left-aligned; the current-level row highlighted with
  `--warn-soft` (Sensibull's yellow current-price row in the payoff table, S-017) - for our tables use `--accent-soft`
  so yellow stays free of meaning; hover row `--surface-muted`.

### a.5 Controls

- UI-016 [layout] Drawer: slides over the page from the left (leg picker, S-032) or right (warnings, S-234; funds,
  S-053); white, full height under the top bar, shadow `0 0 24px rgba(27,31,36,.12)`, header 56 px with title and a
  close "x"; footer 64 px with the actions; Esc closes; focus is trapped inside while open.
- UI-017 [layout] Info icon + tooltip: 14 px circled "?" in `--ink-subtle` after every figure label (S-012 "Max Loss
  (?)"); tooltip dark (`#1B1F24` at 95 %), white 12 px text, max width 280 px, radius 6 px (S-124). Our text comes from
  `lib/figureDefinitions.js` (REQ-136), never retyped. Touch: tap toggles.
- UI-018 [layout] Modal dialog (Save, Charges): 420 px wide, radius 12 px, padding 32 px, title 20 px/600, close "x"

- UI-019 [layout] Toast: bottom-centre, dark, 14 px, 4 s, one line; used only for confirmations ("Saved", "Legs
  cleared - Undo"); errors are never toasts.
- UI-020 [layout] Error message (REQ-065 four parts: what happened, impact, what is blocked, next action): a card with a
  4 px `--loss` left border, `--loss-soft` background, the four parts as label:value lines at 13 px. The `compact`
  form (ErrorMessage `compact`) keeps all four parts and fits the 572 px left column (labels 110 px wide, text wraps).
  It sits directly under the control that failed, never in a toast.
- UI-021 [layout] Empty state (Sensibull S-010, S-050, S-171): centred in its card, a 120 px circle in `--surface-muted`
  holding a 48 px line icon in `--ink-subtle`, a 15 px/600 title, one 14 px `--ink-muted` sentence, and at most one
  button (the existing `EmptyState.vue` contract: one optional action). Wording for a feature not built yet:
  title "Coming soon: <feature>", sentence "<what it will show>"; no date promise, no work-item ids on screen.
- UI-022 [layout] Loading and updating marks: first load = grey skeleton bars in the card (no spinner text); while a
  newer request is pending the old numbers stay at 60 % opacity with a 2 px `--accent` progress line on top of the
  card (today `opacity-60`, StrategyBuilderPage `data-updating`); "This is taking longer than usual." appears after 3 s
  (existing). The "Prices as of HH:MM:SS IST" stamp sits in the 12 px caption line at the bottom of the left column
  (Sensibull S-117 "Prices last updated at 11:05 AM").
