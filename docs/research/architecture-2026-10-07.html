Q259How orders reach Zerodha

NSE counts every API order as an algo order, even one the user confirms (F-17). Options: become an empanelled provider, use Zerodha's own order page (F-22), or let users enter orders in Kite.

Q210Showing a user's own Kite prices

Kite's terms forbid public display but allow platforms for other Zerodha clients after exchange approval (F-23). Needs Zerodha's written answer.

Q262Contract identity

NSE reused 4,768 of 65,265 contract numbers in two months (F-21). Recommendation: add the expiry date to the identity.

Q260Who sells Pro

An Authorised Person may not charge clients, and incentives for account opening are barred (F-26).
